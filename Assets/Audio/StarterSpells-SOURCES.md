# Starter spell sound sources

The six starter spell sound designs use 21 source recordings published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The approved authored mix manifest is `0f32e12303f04e5691b3f801b57760064d68d987d7f6cc51fa5ea6fea7289ab7`. Original files, saved license evidence and full download ledgers are retained in `ArtSource/Audio/StarterSpellDesign/revision2/sources/`.

Runtime WAVs are derivatives of those approved mixes: mono 48 kHz PCM24 complementary phases for contact timing. They retain the approved material balance and use one uniform runtime gain of 0.7768969 (2.19 dB attenuation) for overlap headroom. Ember Spit uses its existing separately authored sound and is outside this source list.

Freesound material here uses publicly served high-quality MP3 previews, not original lossless recordings. Rime’s wooden creak is a disclosed Foley proxy for ice pressure; Jet’s wave and ocean splash are water-motion proxies, not recordings of a magical jet. IgnasD supplies ice effects without specifying the capture technique. Rain’s droplets use isolated water-impact transients.
