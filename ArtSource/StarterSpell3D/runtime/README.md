# Native mesh and motion handoff

`starter_spell_library.json` is generated by `../export_runtime.py` from the
saved Readability `starter_spells.blend`. It contains real carved mesh vertices,
triangles, flat geometric normals, static material colors and sampled motion.
It includes 373 mesh pieces and 45,384 triangles across seven spells. There are
no studio actors, targets, floors, planters, crop leaves, lights or colliders.

The current freeze is [Ember Art19](../../../Docs/Verification/StarterSpell3D/EmberRevision/Art/19-ember-import-freeze.json).

Library SHA256: `158f2432230854309c786569666e453c0ebb6cf2e32394e0dd3748eefc3d4164`.
There are 33 unchanged static material definitions. The Ember-only source
revision is accepted, with native import/visual verification **pending**.
The other six normalized exports and six FBX files remain exactly E00.
These are carved, more graphic forms than the fluid concept images.

## Coordinates and sampling

All dimensions are in cells, one Unity metre per cell. The export maps author
X to Unity Z (forward), author Y to Unity X (lateral), and author Z to Unity Y
(up). Geometric triangle winding agrees with the exported normals. The native
Unity importer must independently accept front faces and material lighting.

Every `poses` array contains 111 entries, sampled at 100 fps from frame 0 to
110. Positions, quaternion rotations in XYZW order, and scales reproduce the
converted source matrices to a maximum error of 2.39e-7. Zero-scale samples use
a neighboring visible orientation because their rotation is underdetermined.
No non-uniform-scale shear is silently discarded. `rgbaSrgb` colors are
explicit sRGB values, with alpha 1; the importer owns Unity color-space handling.

Each study declares its exact `contactFrame`, `clearFrame`, authored distance
and release frame 22. Native retiming preserves cast/charge, maps recorded
travel to the study's release-to-contact interval, and preserves post-contact
choreography. Hidden portions remain hidden through their sampled envelopes. Native
`TargetImpact` starts only at actual copied contact; its private studio lead-in
is omitted in game, while the contact sample already has a readable form.
The source clip's pause to frame 110 is a review pause, not a gameplay cooldown.

## Roles and anchors

| Role | Anchor | Native selection |
|---|---|---|
| ProjectileHead | ProjectileCarrier | Actual copied travel path and carrier progress |
| ProjectileTrail | Source | Each fragment's own source-relative forward coordinate along the copied path |
| TargetImpact | Target | Actual resolved target, with its captured outcome condition |
| ConeCell | Cell | Explicit `(forwardCell,lateralCell)` present in copied affected cells |
| GroundCell | Cell | Actual path cell, selecting one of four authored variants |
| CropCell | Cell | Actual watered crop from recorded target results |
| ReactionCell | Cell | Actual matching recorded water reaction |

Head and trail poses preserve full source-relative positions. For heads,
`projectileProgress` is the actual carrier's forward displacement divided by
`authoredDistanceCells`. Place the carrier on the actual path, then retain
each head pose's residual forward offset, lateral offset and height. The
muzzle is 0.36 cell forward. Progress has intentional small follow-through:
Ember reaches 1 at contact then 1.0266666; Calm reaches 0.98 at contact then
1.02. This is the original 0.08 m contact movement, not a new target.

## Ember-only projectile revision

Ember uses a compact three-layer nose (`ProjectileHead`) and sixteen short,
overlapping defining sections (`ProjectileTrail`/`Source`). Sixteen 16-island
ember cohorts and eight short glow sleeves follow the same route. The tail is
broad near its nose, tapered toward the rear, and grows out of actual travel;
it advances and contracts during the existing post-contact interval. Source
geometry is not classified as cone cells and never changes the first-body hit.

The three small puff lobes, two splash curls, sixteen fading embers and local
glow are `TargetImpact`; the previous radial crown is removed. Exact contact
29.5 remains visibly positive, the carrier curve and all original phase metadata
are unchanged, and everything clears by 67.5. Ember now totals 50 pieces and
6,404 triangles, below its previous 61-piece/8,692-triangle contribution.

The [actual-data identity gate](../../../Docs/Verification/StarterSpell3D/EmberRevision/Art/11-cross-section-controls.json)
checks all vertex caps on 1/2/4-cell cardinal/diagonal routes, 150 adjacent
triangle cross-sections in travel/contact/settle, taper, dense travel islands,
small impact and contraction. Its sideways-shifted middle-volume countercase
fails even when longitudinal bounds still overlap. Exact normalized other-six
mesh/pose/material equality and six FBX byte hashes are independent controls.
The gate counts posed geometry; native pixels establish visible density.

## Explicit adaptations

Flaming Hands uses one adjacent cell `(1,0)`. Jet uses `(1,0)`, `(2,-1)`,
`(2,0)` and `(2,1)`. Retained `CellSurface` geometry is clipped to those
squares and keeps its original eased envelope; new surface roots likewise
remain within half a cell at every sample. A diagonal native cast fits only
these surface pieces horizontally by 1/sqrt(2).

Jet's internal airborne patches have overlapping laps and a shared roll about
one source pivot. This covers the extra .4142m between diagonal cell owners
without changing their actual selected cells. Four essential Surge stitches
reach backward from each current owner, from -1.24 to +.26m along the authored
forward axis. Their complete leading cap stays below .264m, behind the retained
diagonal root's approximately .35m extent. The preceding real owner supplies
the previous path segment; the final owner does not invent a leading segment.
Particle cohorts are redistributed, with no particle-count increase.

Saved-mesh checks sample actual seam vertices at several held frames in all
eight native anchor directions. They include cap vertices, missing-side Jet
coverage, omitted-middle Surge gaps, and exact baseline comparisons for every
CellSurface piece and the five otherwise unchanged spells. These geometry
checks do not replace actual native fog/depth or visual acceptance.

Explicit `AirborneDecoration` pieces retain actual sampled local transforms
and may visually overhang their real cell owner by at most 2.75 cells radially.
They do not extend affected cells. Jet's four raised patches form one continuous
flowing fan across the existing stencil; removing a captured cell removes that
owner's patch. The unchanged Flaming Hands flame crown, Rime crown and open
Calm loop have real depth,
with eight-direction projected bounds and final native pixel review as separate
gates. Decorative halos retain the same physical-cell fog shader contract.

Material `emission` is a static float from 0 to 1 and `glow` is a boolean.
A glowing mesh requires complete flattened RGBA `vertexColors`, white RGB and a
zero-to-positive alpha gradient copied from Blender's POINT `SpellGlow`
attribute. Vertex colors remain optional for nonglow meshes. Native additive rendering uses alpha and emission as local strength;
solid surfaces use their own self-light floor with carved facet response.
No color or alpha shader parameter animates. `rgbaSrgb` still supplies the
base swatch, and conversion to native linear color happens once.

Ground Surge exports all four source cell variants with actual transforms.
Rime's long cold thread is split into short cell-bounded pieces along the
path. Its native-only freeze-water variant derives three low plates from an
actual source chip mesh: twice the ground size and 0.35 times the height,
with measured maximum height 0.054408 cell. These transient plates require
`FreezeWater`; they do not represent persistent water state. They are not a
separately rendered Blender studio branch.

Rain exports exactly the first source crop's cluster as a reusable local
cell template. The native adapter instantiates it only for actual watered
crops. It must not treat generic affected cells as crops: an empty successful
cast can report the source cell while watering nothing.

`FrozenApplied` gates Rime's clamps, seams and rims; neutral drifting chips
remain valid cold-contact feedback. The game's recorded effect type is
`FrozenEffect`, which the native adapter maps to this artist condition.
`PacifiedApplied` gates Calm's relaxing target marks; the open traveling loop
is independent. The game's captured pacification string is `Pacified`.
Every template clears. Persistent state views are separate from these clips.

`reducedEssential` retains defining forms and drops secondary beads, wakes,
foam, rims and extra accents. Reduced mode must preserve outcome and cell
selection, not create new gameplay or remove necessary feedback.

## Verification limits

`export-audit.json` records the current source-export checks. The independent delivered
JSON gate `../audit_runtime_library.py` records 60 checks including geometry
bounds at all 111 samples, low water plates, static material normals, bounded
scale reveals and seven actual data mutations with an unchanged control.

The new `../audit_readability.py` additionally measures actual particle islands,
projected primary bounds in eight directions, exact-contact/hold samples, glow
alpha and real-data countercases. A posed mote is not necessarily visible: only
rendered native pixels reveal occlusion and density.

These receipts establish the handoff data. Native axis appearance, material
lighting, visibility and fog, captured outcome selection, queue timing,
caster pose integration, pooling and performance require Unity acceptance.
The historical [R23 report](../../../Docs/Verification/StarterSpell3D/Integration/NativeAudit/SSN-e29cae1c81ba4cac8fa92f275c1b719b-native.json) passes 94 native checks,
with 968 independent checks, 286 PNGs and 81.6928 measured profile seconds.
[Final visual review](../../../Docs/Verification/StarterSpell3D/Readability/R23-final-visual-acceptance.json) accepts the sampled main and
directional appearances, with brief body occlusion and capture-rate limits.
The [R24 full-suite comparison](../../../Docs/Verification/StarterSpell3D/Readability/R24-final-full/baseline-comparison.json) records
11,307/11,338 passing, the exact same 31 baseline failure names/messages,
zero compiler errors/skips and all 130 added tests passing. These bounded
native fixtures and profile do not imply coverage of every scene or long session.
The Ember-only source gates above do not substitute for its pending new native
verification; the R23/R24 receipts describe the accepted prior pack.

The Reduced handoff retains `Rime__drifting_ice_chip_0` as essential cold-contact
feedback when Frozen is denied. The canonical data gate recorded this gap RED
before the one-flag fix and rejects removal of the remaining neutral chip.

At exact Rain release/contact frame 22, the first defining stroke is already
visibly sized from an eased charge-phase reveal. Its downward fall starts at
22; remaining strokes retain their stagger. This corrects a measured subpixel
contact in the native GPU probe, without changing the crop-only role or timing.
