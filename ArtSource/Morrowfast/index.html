The small diamond is a surveyor for this art study. Roofs, hidden rooms and cleared ground can be inspected here; native Unity gameplay is a separate integration.

How this scene is reconstructed

Individual source components are drawn over reconstructed backing surfaces. Lifting an owner hides all of its visual layers and removes its dynamic collision. Roofs reveal furnished interiors; doors open a real gap in the preview’s walking mask. A blocking component cannot be restored onto the surveyor. Lifting a bridge closes its creek crossing and is refused while the surveyor or a queued route depends on it.

The original image is the visual reference. Interiors and ground hidden in that image are newly inferred surfaces. The comparison buttons pause movement and leave your component state intact.

Character and shop cards show authored scene design. They do not simulate purchases, currency, native AI, quests or faction reputation.

Changes are saved only to this browser’s local workbench. Reset returns the intact scene.
