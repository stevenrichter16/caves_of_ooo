# Starter audio revision2 — individual material studies

Status: source selection and layering in progress. Revision1 was rejected by the user as sounding nearly identical. Do not label the old technical pass as auditory approval.

## Why revision1 failed

Its six definitions reused the same broad, bass-heavy filtered noise, the same rise-to-contact envelope, and the same noise impact. The small material accents sat below this common body. The tests rewarded the same low-frequency distribution and a fixed four-layer format, so they verified consistency instead of audible identity. Distinct words in a layer name did not produce distinct source acoustics.

Replace that structure with real material recordings and different gestures. No common synthesized wind bed or fixed number of layers. Each spell needs one recognizable dominant source, supporting physical action, and a finish that reports its real result. Depth comes from the material's body/resonance and selected pitch processing; it must not erase liquid, electrical, brittle, or tonal identity.

## Flaming Hands

**What it does:** projects heat/flame from two palms into one adjacent cell. It can heat empty ground and ignite oil; it is not Ember's traveling coal or a distant explosion.

**Essential components:** ignition, a brief sustained outflow of flame, heat meeting a surface, diminishing combustion.

**Sound construction:** a real match flare provides the catching edge. Enlarge the flare into the palm release; a low-pitched section of actual burning fire gives hot combustion body. Natural fire crackles remain a smaller close layer. Finish with the fire rapidly losing pressure and a few dry residual snaps. Emphasize a fast outward flare followed by a coarse falling tail; no repeated bass thuds.

**Layer candidates:** match ignition; continuous active fire; small coal/fiber crackles. Any reinforced low body must be derived from the same combustion recording. Contact material effects remain separate from an empty flame release.

## Jet Blast

**What it does:** sends a short pressurized water cone, does modest damage, wets creatures/ground and attempts a one-cell shove.

**Essential components:** gathered liquid, acceleration through a narrow opening, a coherent moving water mass, impact/breakup and runoff.

**Sound construction:** begin with a short liquid cavity/gulp. A trimmed real rushing-wave source gives continuous water mass through release. The main recognizable event is a broad wet slap, supported by overlapping bubbling and falling drops. The tail breaks apart into smaller droplets, not a uniform hiss. Preserve the midrange slap and low gurgle so it cannot be confused with fire.

**Layer candidates:** water rush; discrete splash; liquid bubbles; separated water drops. Ground-only casting has water release/runoff without a creature-shove accent.

## Ground Surge

**What it does:** runs electrical charge along a four-cell ground line, damages and attempts to push contacted targets; Electrified application is conditional.

**Essential components:** charge establishing, rapid connected arcs advancing along the floor, ground/target displacement, electrical discharge.

**Sound construction:** use an actual small Tesla generator's short rough electrical vibration. Advance several irregular but connected spark transients through the brief travel interval. A stony fracture/gravel scrape gives the floor physical weight; a single lower stone event supports contact. Cut the current into a short gritty discharge. Its defining rhythm is a fast crackling pulse train, not wind or distant thunder.

**Layer candidates:** electrical buzz; single arc snaps; broken stone; grit scrape. Full-size hit sound occurs once per contact event; no per-footprint duplicates. A persistent charged-target detail must require real Electrified.

## Rime Grip

**What it does:** cold damage to the nearest target, attempting Frozen restraint and freezing water under/along valid ground branches. It closes on a target; it does not launch an explosive ice projectile.

**Essential components:** material stress increasing, ice formation/creep, a grip closing, brittle settling. A killed target fractures instead of locking.

**Sound construction:** draw a resonant ice/rigid-material creak inward. Follow with two or three close irregular cracks, then one lower hard closure. Leave small glassy/icy fragments ringing and settling. The defining contrast is creak → brittle lock, with audible hard edges and gaps between the larger fractures. It should not carry fire-like continuous crackle or a wind floor.

**Layer candidates:** ice creak/groan; ice crush or fractured glass as a clearly labelled Foley proxy; hard clink and small fragments. Use actual ice where available. Lock is a success layer; dry empty refusal and resisted freezing cannot announce successful restraint.

## Calm

**What it does:** sends a non-damaging single-target pacifying pulse. Successful Pacified suppresses fighting; it is not sleep, healing, recruitment or coercive injury.

**Essential components:** tension becoming less rigid, a gentle directed gesture, a soft release/resolution.

**Sound construction:** use the sustained tail of a real resonant bowl/chime, removing its hard striking transient. A quiet human exhale or soft breath-like Foley makes the release intimate. Let two lightly unsettled resonances settle into a simple stable relation, then decay naturally. Keep a recognizable tonal body; no impact, bass shock, particles, or violent bright chimes. This should be the most clearly different member of the set.

**Layer candidates:** soft resonant bowl; quieter related overtone; short exhale/cloth gesture if a suitable licensed source is found. If no appropriate exhale exists, use fewer layers rather than synthesize the shared wind again. The resolved finish only reports newly applied Pacified.

## Conjure Rain — learnable starter-book utility

**What it does:** waters existing crops in a radius3 square query, topping up their moisture. It does not damage, apply combat Wet, create floor puddles or permanent weather.

**Essential components:** droplets arriving, many small impacts on foliage, gathering/intermittent drips, diminishing rain.

**Sound construction:** actual rain falling on leaves is the dominant source. Add a few individually audible, rounded drops at release and as the rain dwindles. A quieter leaf/ground-patter band fills the middle. Preserve dense small impacts and stochastic spacing, with a longer soft taper. No thunder, low cinematic wind, large water slam or ocean roar.

**Layer candidates:** rain on leaves; discrete drops; optional small foliage taps. It should read as many tiny contacts, while Jet reads as one forceful water mass. An empty crop query must not play crop-success patter during future integration.

## Source, editing and review rules

Sources are selected by the physical sound required, with each download's title/creator/license/source URL and SHA recorded. Prefer CC0. Source recordings remain preserved; crops, pitch/rate changes, equalization, envelope and mix gains are recorded per layer. Output both final mixes and layer-by-layer audition reels. Do not normalize isolated layers louder for the breakdown reel: listeners must hear their actual contribution.

Keep distinct silhouettes: flare; liquid slam; electrical pulses; brittle clamp; resonant release; fine patter. Technical checks enforce exact source/layer lineage, clipping/DC/boundaries, reconstruction and event placement. They are not substitutes for auditory judgement and must not force identical spectral balances or four layers on all spells. Revision1 and the approved Ember remain unchanged; no Unity integration belongs to this revision.
