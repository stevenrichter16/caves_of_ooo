# Source credits and transformation record

All selected publications are CC0, as documented in their source ledgers and independent source audit. Sources are preserved unmodified. Bowl, exhale, wood creak and foliage rain are publicly offered lossy preview encodes where noted in the ledgers; their original lossless files were not downloaded. Ice-pack recording technique is unspecified; the wood creak is an intentional pressure Foley proxy. No claim that these are all literal field recordings of magical actions.
