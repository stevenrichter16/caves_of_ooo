Production concept study for a flat 16x24 pixel roguelike. Reference 1 is the ACTUAL current game screenshot: preserve the entire layout, HUD, quiet dark-green ground, plots, player and all object placement. Replace only the red percent-glyph remains directly below the central gold player with a tiny readable fallen magpie silhouette at exactly the same cell. This is one dead bird, lying on its side, black/grey-green folded wing, cream breast edge, long angular tail, small beak, no standing legs, no bright eye, no blood pool, no glitter or loot glow. Reference2 is the approved native sprite strip: its sixth 16x24 frame is the living magpie; seventh is the viper. Preserve their colors and anatomical identity. Make a landscape concept board: upper two-thirds is the actual scene reinterpretation at its ordinary scale; lower third has three enlarged pixel-design studies labeled MAGPIE REMAINS, VIPER REMAINS, VENOM GLAND. All three must plausibly fit exactly a 16x24 cell with square pixel clusters and much transparent empty space above. Viper remains: low slack angular olive snake with flattened head and broken loose S, distinctly lower/flatter than the alert living viper, pale underside edge, no glow or pool. Venom gland: one small olive/ochre biological sac with tapering duct and quiet ivory membrane highlight, not a potion bottle, not a gem, not food. 4-6 colors per object, no dithering, no gradients, no outlines thicker than one native pixel. This is art direction, not a claim of rendered gameplay. No other scene modifications or invented mechanics.
