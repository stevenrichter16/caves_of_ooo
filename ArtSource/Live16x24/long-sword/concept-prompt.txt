Design reference for one original native 16x24 pixel-art long sword for Caves of Ooo. Reference1 is an actual in-game Examine screenshot: the slash glyph at top right is the asset gap to replace. Reference2 is the accepted loose-item kit; match its restrained chunky silhouette, dark emerald ground, muted earthy colors and sparse clustered pixels. Produce a simple design sheet: left a large sharp nearest-neighbor enlarged single sword on dark green, right the exact same design at smaller game scale, plus a small plain-crossguard / leather-grip / steel-blade breakdown. The sword is a long straight steel blade balanced above a plain crossguard, no ornament or magical glow, no hand, no holder, no scenery, no lens effects. Use a single 16-column by24-row logical pixel frame and keep a full one-pixel margin. Orient blade diagonally from lower left hilt to upper right tip so plain crossguard and short leather grip remain legible. Blade width two or three pixels, one strong steel face and one light edge, at most six flat colors.
