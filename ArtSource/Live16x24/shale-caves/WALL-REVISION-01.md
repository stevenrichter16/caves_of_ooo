# Named source revision: raised-pile wall marks

Root rejected the first WALL4/5 source contact because three isolated bright
flake groups read as raised rock piles on smooth ground. Floor0–3 and their
base palette were approved. The entire first generated draft, builder and
verification/reproducibility records are preserved under draft-v1/; original
concepts, prompts, baseline pins and protected-inputs.json are unchanged.

Revision scope is only the two new wall cells and their internal wall palette.
Use a darker continuous shale body with about20–35% subtle internal lamina
marks. Short broken single-pixel horizontal/stepped runs form a more connected
material field; uneven group heights and lengths avoid full-width stripes.
Remove bright raised tips. Keep the four floor pixels byte-identical, all
family borders uniform, opaque square pixels and flat material. No3D edge,
perimeter shadow, imported texture, gameplay or existing-art change is allowed.

Native source revalidation runs RED against the old render after the authored
wall cells change, then rebuilds and verifies the new geometry. Actual cave
contrast and material identity remain a later gameplay gate; source approval
cannot stand in for it.
