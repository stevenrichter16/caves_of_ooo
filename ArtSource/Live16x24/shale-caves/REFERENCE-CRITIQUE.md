# Shale reference critique — original native production direction

Both root-supplied scene and parts references were inspected before native
production. Exact prompts and original outputs remain unchanged.

The scene usefully separates a lighter traversable shale field from darker
compressed shale mass, with a cool gray material family distinct from pale
limestone. Reject its busy uniform gravel, invented wall geometry, repeated
stripe texture, altered UI/resolution and changed visibility. The screenshot's
actual paths, owners, fog and remembered areas remain authoritative.

The parts study improves the quiet ground direction, but wall marks are far
too dense and continuous. Do not copy its texture, gradients, blurry edges,
uneven pixels or large flake count. Author short broken lamina clusters inside
an opaque material body, with all family edges unmarked and continuous. The
wall must read as solid by its darker body plus coherent layers, rather than
as a slightly recolored quiet floor or a raised3D ledge.

Approved finite contract: six original16x24 cells in96x24; floors0–3 use base
RGB84,88,92, drawn from unchanged old shale source; walls4–5 get a separate
cooler/darker body. No perimeter masks, shadow bands, animation or invented
persistent damage variants. Real ShaleWall survives structuralHP1..28 with
hardness2, then normal destruction removes it and creates existing Rubble.
No concept image is sampled, traced, cropped, downsampled or transformed into
native pixels. This critique is a source-production direction, not actual
in-game contrast, whole-area or package acceptance.
