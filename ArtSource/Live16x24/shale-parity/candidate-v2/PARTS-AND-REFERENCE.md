## Target versus historical direction

The former shale packet intentionally rejected relief, perimeter shadows and
busy marks. The user explicitly prefers the richer shaded wall appearance in
the scene concept, so that historical limitation is superseded for this new
sibling candidate. Every prior source and accepted resource remains unchanged.
This is still flat unit-quad native2D; no extruded geometry or 3D lighting is used.

Keep useful material contrast and layered relief. Do not reproduce the
reference's altered camera dimensions, expanded visibility, invented continuous
walls, changed UI, uneven generated pixels or smoothing. Compare actual
registered gameplay regions after root integrates the candidate.

Root measured neutral grade plus ambient1/torch-off: the prior source floor
RGB84,88,92 appeared approximately86,90,94. Therefore this candidate uses
RGB63,68,74, expected near the reference floor's65,70,76 under that experiment.
This is a source choice informed by the measurement, not verified gameplay
color or approval to change global grading. No amber compensation is baked in.

## First-source rejection and correction

`candidate-v1/` preserves the entire first source candidate and builder. Its
broad equal-length ledges read too regularly like staggered masonry; its
98%-quiet floors looked bare against the target. The revised wall fields vary
ledge sizes and interrupt highlights rather than drawing continuous strips.
The material study uses deterministic hashed variants rather than checkerboard
alternation. Each floor now has33nonbase pixels/384 (8.59% detail) in three
11pixel clusters, using two facet tones and one shadow. The four floor variants
move those clusters rather than sharing a centered repeat.

Root viewed the revised study and approved it for the **first actual gameplay
trial only**. Horizontal band repetition, straight boundary strips and the
small floor clusters' possible pile-like read remain explicit actual-view
questions. Do not iterate indefinitely in contact-only space or claim parity
from this source approval.
