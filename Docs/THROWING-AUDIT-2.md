## Findings requiring resolution in the implementation plan

### A2-F01 — 🔴 Irreversible damage/payload can survive a refunded command

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** Throw damage occurs at `ThrowItemCommand.cs:237`; item on-hit hooks follow at253–257; tonic effects occur at184/280/312. The enclosing executor commits only after `Execute` returns success. Its catch rolls back and returns `Success=false` (`InventoryCommandExecutor.cs:52–55`), and native throw input does not pay for false (`InputHandler.cs:2601–2604`). `CombatSystem.cs:1107` commits HP before the attacker `DamageDealt` event at1177, giving an exact fault injection seam.

Minimal reproduction specification: a deterministic penetrating carried-dagger throw; attach a player Part that throws on `DamageDealt` after recording the target HP. Assert HP changed, then inspect exact projectile custody and the native action count. Current source permits HP loss plus restored dagger and no turn. Positive control: the same handler without throwing; prelaunch refusal control must preserve HP/item and remain free. Repeat with tonic `ApplyTonic`/effect callback failure and an existing gas receiver throwing on `GasDensityChange`: gas density is assigned before that callback (`GasPoolPart.cs:61–67`).

**Required rule:** launching is the irreversible action boundary, established only after reversible preparation succeeds. From that boundary the result owes its unit, preparation/cooldown and action even if a later gameplay callback fails. Report partial resolution explicitly; never replay damage to make a failed callback look successful.

### A2-F02 — 🔴 Landing notification precedes cleanup registration and permits nested dual custody

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** `ThrowItemCommand.cs:343` adds the unit, `367` broadcasts landing, and `369–371` registers its removal undo only afterward. Carried/world/split throw paths never acquire the uniform transfer claim. An ItemLanded listener can pick up the unit through a separate command, then throw. Outer rollback can restore the unit to its old owner while a listener owns it, or leave the world unit plus restored inventory. Moving the undo above the broadcast alone does not solve the already-committed nested pickup: removing from the old zone does not revoke another inventory's rightful receipt.

**Required rule:** exact source and extracted-unit claims persist through resolution. Finalize physical disposition before publishing landing; release claims at the defined publication boundary. Landing is an informational notification of a committed result, never a provisional custody transfer. Notify each live observer independently with event release in `finally`; one exception must not prevent other pet listeners. If an observer subsequently picks up/merges/destroys the item, that state stands and later recovery checks it live. The immutable receipt says “landed at time of completion”; it is not a permanent assertion of current location.

### A2-F03 — 🟡 Equipped rollback registers restoration too late

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** The singleton equipped route already claims the item (`UnequipCommand.cs:150`); this protection must be retained. It removes equipment bonuses, detaches the item, fires `AfterUnequip` at187, dispatches enhancement removal at193, then registers equipment restoration at201. A throwing AfterUnequip handler leaves a failed prelaunch action with the weapon detached; the earlier bonus undo can run without restoring the actual slot. An enhancement callback that partially mutates then throws is another window.

**Required rule:** capture exact body/legacy slot and bonus ownership before mutation; register restoration before callbacks that can fail. Use current restoration ownership checks so rollback cannot overwrite independently replaced slots. Preserve normal veto, multi-slot and enhancement semantics. This is reversible preparation, so a successful restoration remains an unpaid refusal. If an unrelated callback intentionally kills/disarms the actor, do not resurrect or re-equip into a dead/replaced graph merely to satisfy the snapshot.

### A2-F04 — 🟡 Empty stacks launch; split construction can lose quantity before undo exists

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** Throw validation never checks positive StackCount. `HandlingService.cs:114–134` checks handling/strength, not quantity. `ExtractItemForThrow` treats only `>1` as a stack; zero/negative current stacks fall through to singleton extraction. In addition, `StackerPart.RemoveOne:124–128` decrements source count and refreshes weight **before** `CloneForStack`; `Entity.CloneForStack:462` invokes `AddPart` initialization. Any constructor/Initialize exception occurs before the throw registers its split undo at413.

**Required rule:** refuse nonpositive quantity, malformed owner/back-reference, or stale part ownership in every source path. Protect source and exact quantity before callbacks. Use a mutation-local snapshot around splitting (including exceptions), not `sourceCount += splitCount`, which trusts mutable split state. A failed split must restore only its own change and preserve independent operations on other items. Clone caches/preparation/recovery marks must not be copied as public mutable references.

### A2-F05 — 🟡 Source/access/range must be revalidated after extraction callbacks

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** DetectSource prioritizes equipped/list membership/zone anchor (`ThrowItemCommand.cs:378–395`); it does not independently check positive quantity, item Physics owner consistency, current actor Inventory/Body identity, or a living actor. `Execute` captures `actorCell` before equipment callbacks and does not recompute source/target legality afterward. An AfterUnequip handler can move the actor to another zone, change its strength/skill, replace its inventory, or alter the target, yet the subsequent trace still uses the captured cell and object.

**Required rule:** validate live actor, exact source, bounds/range, intended owner's actual contact, ability/preparation and payload again after reversible callbacks and immediately before irreversible launch. A changed source/zone/owner cancels safely; a changed blocker recomputes the actual trace. Same-zone movement invalidates an explicitly prepared shot under its chosen lifetime contract. A forged UI context is never authority.

### A2-F06 — 🟡 Deferred skill input and committed partial outcomes cannot fit the boolean result

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** `SkillsPart.cs:303–325` sets cooldown only on true. `ResolveAbilityCommand` resets `_pendingAbility` and input mode unconditionally at3841–3842; false reports failure, true spends or schedules a turn. Returning true simply for opening an item picker charges before a throw; returning false can erase the continuation. Throw failure after launch also cannot be mapped back to false without creating a free effect.

**Required rule:** explicit action disposition shared by the new throw continuation and native/AI action adapters, while preserving existing boolean skill behavior through a compatibility adapter. A pending picker is not an action. Confirmation produces one committed token; double Enter, FX completion, timeout and repeated callbacks settle that token at most once. Passive focus selection itself spends neither action nor cooldown.

### A2-F07 — 🟡 Reentrant save/load can capture or replace a half-resolved throw

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** `RequestQuickSave` and `SaveSlot` guard `MeleeStrikeScope.IsResolving` only (`SaveSystem.cs:672,717`). Save capture is synchronous into owned bytes even when disk writing is backgrounded. `LoadSlot:804` has no throw resolution guard. A damage/unequip/landing callback can request a save after extraction, or replace the graph before cleanup. Normal UI preventing F5 during targeting does not protect these service entry points.

**Required rule:** a narrow shared action-resolution lease covers extraction through outcome settlement, and a pending committed action debt remains guarded while FX waits. Queue a requested save until the action/turn boundary is stable; reject/defer load until then. Do not serialize the live transaction, UI picker, callback delegates or provisional projectile. Test both synchronous SaveSlot and RequestQuickSave; preserve prior checkpoint if capture is refused. Reuse the existing melee guard rather than remove it.

### A2-F08 — 🟡 Height needs a genuine intersection rule, not a center-height filter

Merely adding a height field still misses descending intersections if checked at cell centers. Render-layer order currently changes equal-cell noncreature choice (`Cell.cs:74–84` plus trace enumeration).

**Required rule:** pure throw-specific profile/trace, ordered earliest intersection along the visited horizontal segment, deterministic ties, actual body-contact cells, and a separate floor-rest query. Preserve existing `LineTargeting` default for Lunge and its AI preview. See geometry contract below.

The ordinary throw branch calls it directly at94. New prediction can compound the leak by truncating at an unseen blocker even if its name is hidden.

Compute permitted-known-state prediction with the same geometry kernel; label unobserved continuation uncertain. Actual execution still collides with hidden geometry. ### A2-F10 — 🟡 Existing tonic snapshot protects identity but not current contact after callbacks

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** `ApplyTonicAoe:639–651` snapshots unique owners and `654–673` rechecks zone membership/life/destruction. It does not recheck that the owner still occupies any cell in the original footprint. If the first target's application teleports a later snapshot target across the same zone, the later target is still dosed at658. The same issue matters when geometry adds splash occlusion and cached contact information.

**Required rule:** freeze candidate identities and the original allowed footprint; before each authoritative dose require a current eligible contact in that footprint. Never add a newly arrived owner mid-loop or chase a moved owner. Stable owner order must be independent of insertion/render order. This retains one dose per physical owner, not one per body cell or one arbitrary owner per focused cell.

### A2-F11 — 🟡 Existing finite pouring is not a safe complete throwing-payload template

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** `LiquidVesselService:196–203` conserves transferred pool volume, but211 registers `ApplyContact` in AfterCommit; that callback applies creature effects and immediate reactions at219–229. InventoryTransaction intentionally swallows observer exceptions at158–162. Copying this ordering for new throwing payloads would hide partial authoritative gameplay behind an informational receipt and could skip later contacts/reactions after one fault.

**Required rule:** reuse finite preparation, registry/liquid semantics, material writes and existing contact effects. Execute new authoritative payload/contact/reaction phases in the explicit committed resolver with recorded partial failure. Keep audio/log/diagnostic notifications in observer hooks. This report does not mandate an unrelated rewrite of all vessel interactions, but the new shared primitive must not inherit that unsafe contract.

### A2-F12 — 🟡 Finite flasks can become inexhaustible coats or permanent hazards through reuse

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED reuse hazard, not an implemented new-tonic exploit.** `LiquidPoolPart:124–135` applies `min(Volume,Strength+Toughness)` without reducing Volume; `LiquidCoveredEffect.OnStack:195` accumulates exposure. Current fill/pour can move volume into a normal PouredLiquidPool; its four-turn tile coating does not establish pool expiry. A new six-turn “film” that can be collected into an ordinary vessel and poured again loses its finite lifetime/attribution unless those travel too. Nine cells each receiving a full-flask payload also multiply resources. Natural existing pools are deliberately distinct and should not be silently nerfed.

**Required rule:** opt-in finite spill policy on the existing liquid contact model; debit its budget before callbacks, bounded expiry, explicit collection policy and movement-event dedup across body contacts. For the initial thin-film design, collection is refused because there is no recoverable unit, not because an invisible arbitrary skill restriction blocks it. Natural/poured pools remain their existing category. If collectible spills are later supported, finite state must survive vessel fill/pour rather than become a renewable reset.

### A2-F13 — 🟡 Contact time, reaction time and navigation time are separate

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** Single-cell ApplyFireToTile/ApplyColdToTile only add energy (`ZoneTileStateSystem:241–245,279–283`). Player EndTurn and NPC processing precede material and tile simulation (`InputHandler:1167–1183`). Tile reactions affect current creatures, not future generic entrants (`TileReactionSystem:384–426`). Pool acid coats on entry and damages at the coated actor's next BeginTakeAction, even after exit (`LiquidCoveredEffect:251–279`); preexisting ice slips, whereas freezing occupied water applies Frozen at reaction time. `ForceMoveTo` moves before AfterMove, entry and slip, so a callback exception cannot be a free failed move. Multi-step DragAlong recomputes actual position each step.

**Required rule:** payload deposit and intended immediate reactions complete before the throw returns its committed outcome; navigation sees those facts on the next decision. Do not promise immediate acid HP loss, an automatic freeze from entering old ice, or a guaranteed push-then-flask sequence. Failed weighted-path first-step fallback is a required test, not a claim that AI always avoids hazards. A rational detour is useful control.

### A2-F14 — 🟡 New fields need deliberate save adoption and stack equality

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** Saves reconstruct parts from their saved fields (`SaveSystem:998–1015`), not new blueprint definitions. `ReadPublicFields:2047–2050` falls back to object for unknown names; `ReadFieldValue:2200–2201` returns a sentinel without consuming those value bytes. EntityFactory conversion can silently ignore malformed parameters (`EntityFactory:264–266,281–295`). Enum.TryParse alone accepts undefined numeric values. New geometry/payload state is not in `StackPayloadIdentity`'s seven groups.

**Required rule:** keep field names/types additive; missing profile resolves through verified authored defaults/conservative runtime fallback, while malformed explicitly authored/saved values fail validation. Unknown fields must fail clearly before publication if their bytes cannot be skipped; do not claim forward compatibility. New physical/payload equivalence belongs in stack identity, or stacking is refused for stateful items. Keep dynamic caches private/nonserialized. No new version break or destructive save purge is required by this feature.

### A2-F15 — 🟡 Delayed responsibility is not carried uniformly; merging cannot invent credit

**SOURCE-CONFIRMED.** Liquid coating damage calls source:null (`LiquidCoveredEffect:279`); StatusEffectsPart sets `Effect.Owner` to the affected entity (`StatusEffectsPart:112`). Gas carries Creator and merge keeps existing receiver creator (`GasFactory:87–97`); burning has its own IgnitionSource. Those are different real contracts. Writing a thrower into Effect.Owner is wrong. A new spill merged into an old source cannot blindly adopt the latest attacker for all old matter or preserve the first attacker for all new matter without a stated policy.

**Required rule:** choose a bounded attribution rule for new finite payloads. Suggested: preserve exact source/throw identity within one payload cohort; do not merge finite film owners from different throws merely to reduce entity count; coating combines same-source exposure with source retained, and mixed/unknown-source exposure clears delayed kill credit rather than attributing all matter to either donor. Existing gas/burning rules stay explicit regressions. A source absent from the current graph does not get resurrected by load or lookup. Immediate reactions have their initiating source; passive world reactions without a preserved cause remain environmental. Do not claim perfect causal attribution for arbitrary secondary chemistry.

## Corrected outcome and custody contract (PROPOSED A2-C1)

The smallest reusable addition is an action disposition/receipt plus an explicit irreversible phase in the existing transaction executor, not a second inventory or damage engine.

For backward-compatible adapters, **legacy `Success` is true for a committed partial result**: the launched action was accepted and must not be retried/refunded. `State`/`FailurePhase` exposes that it did not complete normally. Keep `ActionCommitted` independent of generic inventory success: existing `Ok()` for unrelated pickup/trade commands does not itself claim a scheduled throwing action. The throw's explicit boundary sets this flag/token. Cancellation, reversible failure and deferred UI remain unpaid; the new UI adapter handles Deferred explicitly rather than interpreting its legacy boolean. A wrapper returning false after a committed child is normalized by the executor to committed partial. A stricter `CompletedNormally` query can serve consumers that genuinely require every phase completed.

| Result | World/resources | Native input and cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cancelled | No launch; no item or gameplay mutation | Restore source menu/cursor context; no turn, no cooldown |
| ReversibleFailure | Attempted preparation fully compensated within its actual ownership | Explain refusal; picker can remain; no turn/cooldown |
| Deferred | Typed continuation token owns picker and selected ability/mode, but no extraction | Keep continuation alive; no cooldown or action; cancellation remains free |
| Committed | Exact launch/move happened and resolution completed | One action token settles once; applicable cooldown/preparation settled once |
| CommittedPartial | Launch/move happened; a subsequent callback/placement/payload phase failed | Same payment as committed; visible bounded failure, no retry of prior effects, cleanup/disposition still finalized |

Suggested sequence:

1. Enter bounded resolution/reentrancy lease for actor/action; perform pure request validation. Acquire exact source claim and snapshot quantity/source/equipment. Reject a second independently requested actor action during resolution; preserve legitimate child combat reactions and independent non-action item receipts.
2. Prepare reversible extraction. Register compensation before a throwing mutation; register clone/source quantity restoration before invoking cloning initialization. Claim the split unit immediately on creation. Validate source/back-references again after callbacks. Recompute actual actor position/range and trace before launch.
3. Preflight commit predicates and establish the action obligation, exact projectile custody and prepared/cooldown consumption **before** the first irreversible gameplay attempt. Seal reversible inventory preparation; keep claims until resolution cleanup. An implementation may split the existing Commit internals into seal/finalize phases, but must not release the action guard and allow a second throw mid-resolution.
4. Resolve physical accuracy/penetration/damage/item effects or payload through the ordinary authoritative services. Mark each phase entered/completed; on exception do not rerun it. Miss, armor absorption and resistance-zero remain distinct receipt fields. Item effects are not generic melee skill events.
5. In a guaranteed finalization path, choose exactly one terminal disposition: consumed, actual grounded exact unit, valid later owner, or explicitly lost/destroyed after all legal fallback placements fail. Never restore an already-launched unit to its old source. A consumed payload has spent count/tombstone state, so stale references cannot launch it again. Do not fabricate a replacement entity for a lost unit.
6. Finalize committed transaction/resources; release claims; publish immutable outcome and separately isolated informational observers. The receipt includes actor/action serial, original stack and extracted-unit IDs, source kind, aim intent/contact, actual hit owner/contact, physical result/HP delta, payload phases, actual disposition/location, and failure phase. Recovery records the exact unit and invalidates on custody/merge/transform changes. Thrower receives its own outcome directly; existing ItemLanded exclusion remains intentional for pets.
7. The native adapter owes one scheduler action for either committed disposition. FX postpones processing, not commitment; normal completion and hard timeout consume the same token once. An NPC adapter must use the same disposition. Low-level InventorySystem.Throw currently returns bool and spends no scheduler time (`InventorySystem.cs:82–85`); compatibility callers cannot be cited as proof of action settlement.

**Explicitly rejected:** calling `transaction.Commit()` early and later returning false; it still produces a free world outcome under current input. **Explicitly rejected:** moving damage, forced movement, coating or reactions into AfterCommit callbacks whose exceptions are swallowed. Observer isolation is suitable for notifications, not an authoritative gameplay pipeline.

**Nested command compatibility:** `ConnectedReserveGroundThrowBoundaryReviewTests.cs:35–49` intentionally pins rollback after a wrapper calls a real throw then returns false. Once actual launch is the irreversible boundary, that wrapper must surface CommittedPartial and keep taking/witness consequences. Split this pin into a prelaunch-refusal rollback control and a postlaunch-obligation test; do not delete gathering-claim coverage or blindly retain an impossible “unthrow after event” contract. Composition should prepare child commands before commitment; a wrapper cannot silently undo an already-authoritative child.

## Geometry and disclosure contract (PROPOSED A2-C2)

Adopt one numeric vertical interval per owner, separate from movement/sight. Resolve profile from authoritative content/current state; authored presets are conveniences, not enum ordering physics. Extents must be finite and nonnegative within a named sane ceiling; zero height is allowed for explicitly nonintercepting surfaces, never a default for unknown solid walls. Dynamic open-door/barrier state overrides static obstruction. Real Gas+GasPool is permeable; a Gas tag alone is not.

For ordinary throwing choose a **throw-only centerline supercover traversal**, including the touched side cells at an exact grid corner. This is a deliberate narrow change from Bresenham, justified by blocking shots through the sealed point between two corner walls and by obtaining real cell-entry/exit parameters. Preserve LineTargeting's old policy for Lunge/spells. State clearly that this is zero-width XY with a vertical envelope, **not full 3D swept-radius collision**. No renderer mesh, horizontal weapon length, scatter or ballistic lob is implied.

- Parameterize horizontal travel `p(t)=start+(end-start)t`, `t∈[0,1]`, start/end cell centers; for each touched cell keep its interval `[tEnter,tExit]`, including zero-length corner contacts. Set linear `z(t)` from actor launch to aim. Proposed initial launch is base+0.65×actor height; entity aim base+0.5×target height; ground aim is projectile-radius above floor, so its bottom touches the floor. Use a named initial radius0.05 and epsilon0.00001 in canonical units, with tests independent of eventual tuning.
- Solve the earliest t in the cell interval where `[z(t)-r,z(t)+r]` overlaps `[base,base+height]`, using inclusive touching with the stated epsilon. Do not substitute a single center sample or “any overlap somewhere” followed by unordered owner selection. Compare earliest t first. At a true tie use sealed/solid barrier first, then explicitly intended owner if it actually intersects, then creature, then other physical owner, finally stable ordinal entity ID/contact coordinates. This does not let the selected owner beat an earlier wall.
- A selected entity request carries exact owner identity and a visible occupied contact. Revalidate current owner/contact before launch; do not aim at an unoccupied wide-body anchor. Exclude every caster-owned footprint contact, not just start cell. Stable IDs are established before preview; resolving ties must not mint IDs or draw RNG while hovering. Malformed duplicate/missing runtime IDs use an explicit refused-ambiguous intent/conservative blocker outcome, not render order.
- Ground intent carries only the cell/floor, never an arbitrary entity “selected” from it. It does **not** exempt intersected bodies. A descending throw can genuinely contact low objects near its destination; report that collision as such rather than changing aim intent to the first cell occupant. Bare floor, low item target and creature target therefore have different aim heights/outcomes, even when XY agrees. Do not promise that choosing floor can pass through someone occupying it.
- Same-cell ground placement retains feet placement/payload friendly fire and pays when committed. Same-cell explicit entity attacks use a local vertical segment and exclude actor; invalid/self-owned target refuses. Out-of-bounds, negative range and beyond Chebyshev range refuse before extraction. Exact maximum range is allowed. No accidental extra whole-cell horizontal travel from nonunit direction input.
- Keep actual contact cell, payload center and rest cell separate. Barrier contact records its actual cell; for consumable burst and nonconsumable rest choose approach side of sealed geometry. A destructible targeted barrier can receive the physical strike at contact while the thrown unit rests on the approach side. A passable creature receives contact, and the unit normally rests in that impact cell. A physical accuracy miss terminates there; no second target check.
- A cleared low movement blocker is not a valid place to rest inside. Floor-rest candidates are traversed cells backward then original launch cell, checked against current ground obstruction and the exact projectile footprint, while ordinary co-occupancy with a creature is allowed. Recheck after callbacks/destruction. No supported tabletop/elevated platform is implied. If all fail, the committed result says lost/destroyed; item/action are not refunded.
- Preview uses the same kernel with permitted-known-world candidates; unknown segments are uncertain, not secretly truncated by hidden objects. Actual full-world trace is re-run on confirmation. Display only visible known contact owners/cells; footprints with hidden anchors may expose a visible occupied contact without leaking hidden cells. Actual FX consumes the resolved route/heights/contact; it cannot steer simulation.
- Splash occlusion is separate: for new physical liquid delivery, block initial deposit/dose across sealed wall/door rays and double sealed corners. Do not blanket-migrate gas propagation, beams, ground spells or LOS. If existing instantaneous tonic splash is changed to this same physical footprint, document it as a deliberate compatibility change and update the old no-occlusion expectations.

## Skill-event contract (PROPOSED A2-C3)

- Sure Grip, if retained, retries only the first failed physical accuracy die; no extra extraction, penetration, damage, item proc, FX or action. Tonic/gas paths do not gain physical accuracy checks. Agility1/2 impossibility and no-skill RNG remain explicit controls.

## Payload, time and conservation contract (PROPOSED A2-C4)

Prefer an explicit supported payload list plus a default **instantaneous/no-residue** policy for other tonics. Do not infer a dangerous puddle from every status name. Water/oil/acid films, existing gas, and heat/cold/charge tile inputs are candidates, not a demand to invent a universal liquid. Audit 3 owns final numbers/roster; these safety requirements apply to every retained mapping.

1. A flask owns one total residual budget. Distribute it across valid initial cells deterministically; clipping at bounds/walls cannot multiply it. Focus changes distribution, not whether matter exists. Preserve deliberate magical direct splash dose as a separate nonrecoverable effect budget; do not pretend that equal per-owner tonic dose is conserved physical liquid volume.
2. Existing impact owners receive the declared direct dose once and are excluded from same-action residual contact. New entrants later encounter remaining actual film, not another full tonic. Snapshot/filter unique owners across the whole initial footprint. Per-movement dispatch deduplicates film family/throw cohort across multi-cell contacts before reserving exposure; a later genuine reentry is a new event. Reserve/debit before the callback, and do not refund after an accepted effect may have happened.
3. An opt-in finite-film policy on LiquidPoolPart or a narrow metadata Part supplies remaining exposure, source/throw serial and expiry, while existing LiquidCoveredEffect supplies the actual coating behavior and TerrainNavigationWeight sees the same pool. Do not add a second invisible damage-trap engine. Natural pools retain their behavior. A thin noncollectible film must be refused by vessel source selection/execution and examined truthfully; converting it to normal PouredLiquidPool would erase finite policy.
4. Finite expiry uses one defined player/world-round boundary, including zone leave/reenter and save/load. Define whether offscreen time ages it; recommended elapsed world-time reconciliation, so travel does not pause a consumable trap indefinitely. Absolute expiry must use a real shared clock unit, not “six TickCount” if TickCount advances by actor/speed differently. Zero remaining exposure or expiry retires actual owner/projection without removing someone else's newer coating.
5. Acid entry means coating now, typed damage at wearer turn start, and dry-down at wearer turn end. A fresh own-turn effect skips its first EndTurn, but **OnStack refresh does not reset JustApplied** (`StatusEffectsPart:95–105` returns early; `LiquidCoveredEffect:178–199` merges amounts). Test preexisting nearly dry coats separately. Do not promise a next-turn dose that a refreshed coat actually loses during the current EndTurn.
6. Deposit all initial tile inputs, then call one explicit ResolveAfterAbility before NPC decisions where immediate chemistry is intended. Secondary propagation is allowed. A finite film used by an oil/water reaction must consume/transform its **actual remaining source**, not only remove a tile projection that the surviving source immediately reseeds. Unsupported mixed-liquid placement must have a deterministic nonduplicating rule and visible feedback; do not use vessel's pure-liquid restriction to silently skip a thrown dose.
7. Preserve real gas lifecycle/creator/density semantics. Stable=true is not a lifetime solution; it creates a different indefinite hazard. The existing grenade spawns ordinary gas without OpenCloudPath filtering, while the opacity overlay alone checks that path (`GasGrenadePart:78–85,100–106`). Do not claim a new liquid-wall policy automatically fixes gas reach. Record this distinction in previews/tests and change only a deliberately selected family.
8. Source/cause is explicit metadata, never Effect.Owner. Resolve source identity through the loaded world without creating a new actor. Preserve same-source attribution; mixed-source exposure cannot fabricate a single owner's exclusive credit. Existing gas receiver-credit policy is an explicit separate regression. Tile reactions from ordinary world ticks without a preserved source remain environmental unless the implementation deliberately carries cause.
9. Hazard preview/AI weight must use actual remaining/expired state and the same eligibility/immunity/contact facts. Maximum across footprint avoids counting one body nine times. Do not label a coating dangerous merely because its name is acid when no future contact handler exists. Test an available dry detour, expensive detour, no detour, a resisted actor, ranged actor, fast actor and failed weighted first step. AI need not cooperate or always choose the route the designer hoped for.
