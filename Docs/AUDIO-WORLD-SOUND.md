## Goal and boundaries

Give exploration a quiet musical identity, make known places sound distinct, and make committed ordinary actions readable. Preserve the seven approved spell sounds and their outcome/contact contracts. Audio never spends a turn, consumes simulation randomness, changes serialized saves, or delays input. Unseen enemies must not create a new threat cue. No new speech or gameplay hearing/stealth simulation is included.

The user's 50 passes are divided into runtime **01–24** here, asset work **25–44** in [AUDIO-ASSET-RESEARCH.md](AUDIO-ASSET-RESEARCH.md), and coordinator integration/review **45–50**. These are distinct implemented/refined contracts or substantive checks, not fifty subjective listening sessions. Native receipts are complete. ## Implemented contracts

- `WorldAudioSettings` stores finite-clamped Music/Ambience/SFX volumes independently of saves. Defaults are 0.55 / 0.45 / 1.0; explicit zero mutes that channel. Existing local spell gain remains authoritative within the global SFX multiplier.
- `WorldAudioContext` selects organic, dry, underground and visible-danger music IDs. Organic field, wet and cave ambience have explicit mappings; dry locations are silent on the ambience bus. Danger uses a currently visible living hostile in a bounded radius-six neighborhood, excluding party allies, then a four-second release. Between samples only the already-seen witness is revalidated; no fresh enemy scan runs every frame.
- `WorldAudioLibrary` loads the prepared catalog once per lifetime. Missing/invalid clips or cue IDs fail quietly. Three complete music tracks and three ambience beds use streaming importers; sixteen short cues preload. The library's thirteen semantic cue IDs include three prepared UI cues with **no production emitters** (`UiConfirm`, `UiCancel`, `Page`).
- `WorldAudioPlayer` owns sixteen reusable 2D sources: two music, two ambience and twelve action voices. Two-second equal-power transitions keep free-running track positions. A fade reversal reuses the outgoing waveform; a same-clip danger gain change ramps without restarting. Gains are independent, pitch stays one, cue cooldowns and a full pool refuse excess work, and mute clears transient sounds without later replay. Missing owned sources fail closed and the runtime can reconstruct its bounded lifetime.
- `WorldAudioEventRouter` uses additive callbacks and exact current-zone/owner checks. Only the player's ordinary committed movement emits footsteps; forced relocation, blocked/unchanged movement and hidden owners stay silent. Real liquid, material tags and the exact stone-floor family choose water/stone/soft cues. Visible accepted melee swings and actual melee damage have distinct cues. Successful known inventory commands, real door changes and existing self-use hooks supply the remaining sounds.
- `WorldAudioRuntime` is attached by GameBootstrap and owns the library/player/router. It samples context after renderer FOV on zone, actor, map, turn or world-resume changes. Load/new graph clears transient cues and threat hold. Repeated initialization and disable/destroy remove only its own subscriptions. It never creates an AudioListener. Exposed counters are presentation diagnostics, not gameplay state.
- Pause gains Audio after Graphics. Its five rows are Music, Ambience, Sound effects, Audio credits and Back. Left/right lower/raise by ten percentage points; A raises; B returns to the Audio row; Menu closes all. The footer explains left/right, so zero-volume mute is discoverable. Credits use the existing paginated announcement reader. Existing save/load/control/graphics actions retain their indices except Quit moves after the new row; the intentional Alpha pin was updated.

Asset gains are conservative engineering starting values: music 0.30 (danger 0.40), field/water/cave beds 0.35/0.20/0.55, steps 0.40 and ordinary action cues 0.50. These numbers have not been called a subjective listening result or an all-combinations headroom guarantee.
