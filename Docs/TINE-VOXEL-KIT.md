`CavesOfOoo.Editor.TineVoxelKitBuilder.Run()` writes `Assets/Resources/TineVoxel3D`. Meshes are combined once per variant, existing mesh/prefab asset identities are preserved on rebuild, and all use `SpawnRing3DLibrary.WorldMaterial`. `TineVoxelKitLibrary.Load/Find/Validate/Family/ModelId` matches the established strict library API; invalid family/variant inputs fail clearly. Alias matching is exact and deliberately excludes MirePool, BoatFrame, Scribe and foreign floor IDs.

The fixture checks mesh/prefab/material/metadata references, identity transforms, one renderer, no scripts/colliders/lights, triangle/bounds/clip metadata, four distinct variants, horizontal cell bounds, quiet continuous surface, playable wall/board height and real plank/support ray intersections. Thirteen malformed-library controls retain the unmodified source library.
