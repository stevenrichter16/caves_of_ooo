# Secure field sites and collection routes

November 28, 2026. Design proposal in response to the player’s direction. A home or field installation can earn a dependable safety guarantee and become the source of real trade. A protected site and a viable route serve different purposes. Site investment keeps the belongings safe. Route work lets a buyer reach them and bring useful goods back to town.

## What the player would do

1. Find a reason to work a particular spot. At a Stump cascade, fish rings point to a live basin with shared stock. At a Sodden bank, a dry shelf beside a useful mire pocket could make a workshop worthwhile.
2. Put equipment near the source and choose a safe layout. The site reads **Defense 4 / 6** and identifies the exposed approach. Place a barrier, cover that approach with a suitable defense, or choose a position where the terrain already does part of the work. The place reads **Secure 6 / 6** once the actual arrangement meets the requirement. These numbers illustrate the rule. Tune them in play.
3. Choose how to use the output. Keep it for the next outing, carry it to town yourself, or agree to a named buyer’s finite order. The buyer tells you what they will take, when they might come, and what they will leave.
4. Walk the route or watch the buyer try it. A dry detour may already work. A shorter line might need a cleared obstruction, drained step, sound boards, or a safer crossing time. The buyer responds to the resulting ground and their real capacity.
5. Go elsewhere. When you return you can find actual stock, payment from a completed pickup, or a clear account of why a visit paused. If a local enemy committed an attack and the defense killed it, its recoverable remains are there to inspect and loot. An uneventful stretch is an ordinary outcome.

The player can use the same site for their own expeditions, one buyer, or neither. The recurring pleasure is returning to a specific place and seeing an outcome produced by that place, its equipment, and the people who used the route.

## The protection guarantee

**Secure means a hostile attack or theft attempt causes zero loss to the enrolled machine, its installation, the enrolled containers, their contents, and the protected payout.** The named defensive pieces that provide the guarantee suffer zero wear from those attempts. The guarantee lasts indefinitely with zero upkeep and succeeds deterministically at the displayed threshold. Normal equipment, routes, and terrain beyond the protected assets have their own physical rules.

An enrolled site has a known, bounded threat profile. That profile defines which approaches and enemy capabilities count, and the required defense for each. The score shown to the player is the weakest eligible approach against its requirement. A closed, sound boundary can remove an approach only when the supported threat actually cannot use it. An open service gate still needs coverage. A wall, placement on genuinely difficult ground, and a defense overlooking a corridor can contribute different capabilities. Count each physical piece once per covered approach, cap repeated contributions, and allow different combinations to reach the same guarantee. Show one short reason for each contribution and missing condition.

The first profile supports a single local threat type and a small authored set of valid approaches. Further threats need deliberate admission and a balanced response. A saved site never silently attracts a stronger tier or loses certification because world time advanced. If an external change leaves its physical plan unrecognizable, put production and new appointments on **Safe hold** and preserve the registered assets and contents. A player who deliberately removes coverage sees the consequence before confirming. Below threshold, a site can still be used manually, and starting unattended work remains disabled until the player restores protection. The first version does not need an option to risk uninsured production.

Authorize protection for exact installed assets and exact directly enrolled storage. Nearby loose objects, unbooked ground loot, and anyone walking through remain ordinary. Confirm a new boundary after substantial relocation, and pause scheduled work before moving a machine with reserved stock. Put an agreed loading point at the protected edge when possible. Its lawful admission must preserve the same measured site coverage.

The placement preview must separately identify independent dangers such as existing fire or rising water. Preempt an attributable hostile strike, ignition or transfer before it launches. Keep a final gate at the protected mutation for splash, delayed fire, collapse, displacement, absorption, theft, and changes with missing hostile provenance. Existing fire spread can change the source to the burning object, so checking the original attacker alone misses that case. A supported non-hostile environmental action has its own validated path. Blocking collateral damage does not start an incident or kill its unrelated originator. The threat profile limits which attackers the system can resolve lethally. Unsupported effects trying to harm enrolled assets cannot bypass protection. Safe hold allows lawful owner withdrawal and cancellation while pausing further work and harmful mutation. Report discrepancies in invalid saves and preserve provable custody. [Heat propagation](../Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/Materials/MaterialSimSystem.cs#L149)

### Attacks and loot

**Player-approved rule, November 28.** Choose a site for its materials, access, and nearby customers. Loot from a stopped attack is an occasional find. A location that attracts more attacks should never be the best way to make a workshop pay. Safe, quiet sites must offer a rewarding loop through their actual production and trade.

Give a finite pool of real, eligible local actors periodic chances to attempt an attack. A nearby hostile, passing animal, lawful visitor, or enemy pursuing the player is insufficient. Recognize the attack when the exact actor begins a valid action against the specific site. Use one saved incident identity tied to that attacker and site. Once a supported attack commits, a secure defense kills the committed attacker and the ordinary terminal death path supplies its eligible drops. Enemies that turn away before committing leave no loot. A resolved attacker supplies one result through leave, load, repeat visits, and every view of the incident.

Keep the first encounter roster finite. A site does not spawn prey or yield an encounter bounty or player experience. The recovered goods come from the actual slain entity’s supported drops. Qualify a first attacker with one eligible recoverable drop. Keep it at a safe interception point, separate from the harvest bin and paybox. Checkout and encounter resolution do not depend on picking up earlier remains. An actor that already reached a terminal state cannot die or drop a second time. Offer a quiet summary such as **One attack stopped. Machine and stock intact. Remains by the east approach.**

Before expanding the encounter roster, compare two similarly productive and accessible sites over the same game-time absence, one quiet and one exposed. Record output, paid pickups, and usable drops. The exposed site can yield a memorable find. If repeated drops make it the obvious economic choice, change which nearby threats commit to attacks or how they are replenished. Keep the zero-loss guarantee and the actual drops from enemies killed in committed attacks.

## The route is a separate player project

Draw a real approach to the loading point and a real exit to the buyer’s destination. Quote the buyer’s available capacity, load, approximate trip, and any known obstruction. Start from actual locations and learned or walked paths. The initial version can use the existing bounded same-zone path. The route must be retested for that courier, that load, and current cells when it acts. A route note is a useful observation. An unseen route segment remains uncertain.

| The player notices | Ways to respond | What the game measures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One finite pool cuts the short route to a working site. | Take the dry detour, draw into flasks or pump to a legal outlet, or later install a genuinely walkable raised crossing. | Footing and passability for the courier. A pump’s outlet can block a different part of the path. At Peat Works the existing pump visibly opened a step for the player, and the same outlet’s mire became clay and torch oil. A buyer crossing that step is a new qualification. [Actual pump and walk](Verification/MachineSystems/PeatPumpedMire20261128/README.md) |
| A loose oil film makes a short step unsafe. | Apply compatible grit, wait for the film to expire, or pick a dry path. | Real coating, grit duration, and whether the courier can cross while it lasts. A settled route should offer a lasting option so the player need not regrit on every visit. The prior matched porter test supplied the film and grit. |
| A tight approach blocks a person, or a load exceeds their capacity. | Move the obstruction, take a dry lane, split a smaller permitted order, or move the loading point. | Walkability for the actual actor and carry weight for the actual pack. Sled or cart clearance needs its own movement support. |
| A hunter is using the same lane or exposure is bad at that time. | Wait for a safer departure, use a different route, address the real local danger, or choose a different buyer and work site. | Actual observed danger and applicable actor tolerances. The report can say **Last seen by the bend**, and the decision reacts to the current world. |

Show outcomes in behavior. If the safe detour fits the terms, the buyer uses it. Once the shortcut passes the same footing check, the buyer can arrive sooner on the same order size and price. If neither way fits the current trip, the buyer waits or turns back and reports the observed reason. When an approach becomes viable, the outstanding order can proceed. A dry detour, a pump stroke, and a suitable raised crossing can all unlock the same successful service.

Site certification applies to the enrolled site. On the road a buyer may pause, detour, retreat when injured, or be attacked. Before pickup, goods stay under site control and money stays in buyer-side escrow. After a paid pickup, the buyer owns the load and its travel risk. If cargo is lost later, the receipt and actual remains follow the physical shipment. The merchant receives no stock that failed to arrive. A missing buyer or parcel can be an optional lead using the existing overdue-search and witness rules. Future orders wait for a real replacement, route, or renewed demand. There is no obligation to supervise each trip.

A useful corridor can serve other real walking trips when their endpoints and loads fit. A short crossing can shorten a buyer’s trip and leave time for another appointment within an agreed quota. A dependable lane might justify a larger contracted lot or a second collection site if someone actually wants the goods. Let the player earn those advantages from delivery capacity and observed outcomes. A project flag or higher defense score does not grant an unrelated route bonus.

Give sites different reasons to invest. A nearby lane on sound ground may already support small orders. A richer deposit beyond a wet crossing could earn the cost of an installed pump or a permanent walkable crossing. A remote site with no dependable buyer can still reward a traveler who hand-carries occasional high-value loads. The first route comparison should hold source, buyer, load and price constant so the difference is visible in travel. Later worlds can vary those conditions and reuse the same acceptance rules.

## Harvest, sale, and physical custody

- **Production.** Enroll one eligible installed machine, its exact source and receiving container. Limit admissible start, source draw, capacity, and total output per game-time interval. Held count and staged unclaimed output share the same capacity. A full receiver pauses before spending the source. A player and a buyer performing an equivalent take draw from the same ledger. Initial automation can be a small supported catch, then extend to another feed when it has a real receiver and consumer.
- **Quote.** Name the buyer, accepted material and grade, maximum weight and units, interval, minimum lot if any, method of payment, and visible pause conditions. Agree to one funded appointment first. Later standing terms can authorize one outstanding order at a time with a finite buy quota, a given receiving need, and a choice to reserve some output for personal use. Skipped visits do not stack into debt or draw past stock.
- **Reserve.** Fund one appointment, then reserve the exact lot when it is ready. Different participants may not consume, move, split or substitute a reserved unit through other actions. Show the reservation. Cancellation or expiry before pickup releases the lot and returns the unused escrow exactly once. Access grants apply to the named buyer and selected output only. Stock, owner payout, and recovered loot have different permissions even if shown at the same fixture.
- **Collect and settle.** At the admitted standing cell, revalidate buyer, route arrival, capacity, site revision, package and funds. Transfer the exact package to the buyer and payment into the protected on-site compartment as one transaction. For drams, use an on-site balance backed by the buyer’s debit and an exact withdrawal receipt. For barter, move the specified real item from escrow. A full paybox or vanished participant pauses before either transfer. The buyer cannot withdraw the player’s payout. The player may collect the sum when they choose.
- **Deliver.** The buyer physically carries the load to an admitted destination and transfers it once into real stock or a real consumer. Keep in-transit units out of ordinary restock, sale and consumption. A full, locked or missing destination retains the paid state and actual cargo for retry or a recoverable close. A new order evaluates market demand, destination capacity, real funds, and the outcome of the prior cargo. An abstract shelf refresh cannot count as this delivery or fund an unlimited loop.

The installation reader should offer **Assess defense**, **Certify site**, and **Pause work**. The collector conversation offers **Quote a pickup**, **Agree or revise** and **Cancel pending pickup**. At the site, the ordinary local actions show **Collect payment**, **Take available stock** and where to find any remains. Map and journal summaries show saved results and label unverified route conditions as last seen. Looking at a reader neither books work nor transfers anything.

Use the saved [world clock](../Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/Turns/WorldClock.cs). The current scheduler removes inactive zone actors. Phase one therefore demonstrates the actual buyer trip in a loaded zone. Away collection is a distinct implementation requirement for the full fantasy. Reconcile at most one eligible pickup and one limited attack opportunity per actual departure interval in the first version. For an away attack, the exact existing actor still has to reach a valid commitment through the supported approach rules. A long absence, a reload, or a clock jump cannot enqueue a backlog. Cap work and visit attempts together across both legs. Safety itself never expires.

Use the real elapsed travel steps and validate the site, source and path revisions for the effective times of the work. Present conditions cannot retroactively authorize a past catch or trip. If the route depended on grit that expired before passage, it was blocked. If a pond or route changed and later returned to its original state, reconcile at the saved change times where that history is available. Uncertain history, unresolved danger, or a blocked destination pauses at the last established position for a real on-map step. Keep cargo and payment under the existing receipts until the next lawful action.

Order transitions at the same tick deterministically. Commit due production before checking ready stock. Resolve a committed safety event before settlement. Apply at most one pickup receipt on that order. All paths check the same identity and revision. Refreshing readers, Continue, walking across an edge, or changing world time via rest does not replay events. Saved absences retain and reconcile finite state. Wall time is irrelevant.
