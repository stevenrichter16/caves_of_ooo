## Player opportunities

1. A traveller carrying brine can spend fuel at an existing still to recover
   useful clean water, or retain the brine for electrical ground effects and
   proofing. Fresh water and collected rain remain convenient alternatives.
2. Clean carried water can rinse existing acid from oneself or a nearby willing
   companion. This gives the same stored water another emergency use alongside
   drinking, tending plants and dousing. It supplies no immunity or healing.
3. A traveller changing an ambush or route can recover an unsprung loaded spring
   trap and lay it elsewhere. Triggered traps remain spent, and a trap can still
   spring on its own layer. Recovery does not grant a reusable ammunition loop.
