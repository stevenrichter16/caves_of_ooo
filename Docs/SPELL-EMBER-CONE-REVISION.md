# Ember Spit — flowing projectile and restrained impact

Status: new Blender candidate imported; Unity regression and native motion verification in progress, 2026-09-11. The user approves Flaming Hands but finds Ember Spit too similar. This revision gives Ember a conspicuous flowing cone-shaped projectile tail and a smaller, subtler impact. It supersedes only Ember's earlier large fire-crown direction; the accepted R23 pack and its receipts remain historical evidence.

## Design and scope

A compact incandescent ember leads a long, tightly filled plume. Swept ribbons and fine embers form a cone that is broad near the projectile and tapers into the trailing wake. The tail grows out of the travelled distance, flows forward and contracts into contact. The hit produces a small warm puff/splash and a few fading embers, visibly quieter than Flaming Hands. No large radial crown.

Keep Flaming Hands and the other five spells unchanged. Preserve their complete normalized exported mesh/pose/material data and six FBX byte hashes. Keep the shared palette, current camera, gameplay, actual hit cells, renderer schema, ordinary timing and existing hitch recovery. The proposed implementation is Blender/export data only; runtime changes require a demonstrated defect and separate RED gate.
