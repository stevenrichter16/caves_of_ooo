# Quest-free exploration: where to expand next

Follow-on assessment requested during implementation. Read-only source review; no new gameplay, art, Unity execution or regional coverage claim. Proposals below are targets, not shipped behavior.

## Recommendation and current boundary

Deepen the Spread first, then pilot **one Beating resource/salvage situation**, followed by one Sodden access situation. Reuse the ownership, finite-resource and saved-aftermath rules; author regional sources and decisions separately. More generic hostile owners are not the missing ingredient.

E6 remains conditional on successful Spread acceptance (`Docs/QUEST-FREE-EXPLORATION-PLAN.md:297`, `:351`, `:359`). The current E2 tranche covers one variant of four families, not all twelve. The three E0 ordinary walks stopped at a moving-owner replan cap; none traversed twenty chunks. The latest full native run completed 20,967 cases with six failures; the subsequent focused correction selection passed 122/122. This is not a clean full-suite acceptance result. The first local state witness stopped before any paid input because no grazer passed the bounded source/view qualification. That report collapses absence, role/food rejection and camera/safety rejection into a null owner; it does not yet establish why feeding was unavailable. Revised observation is pending. Neither imported art nor source tests establish ordinary discovery or player awareness.

### Why these are real extensions rather than labels

**Spread sources already support useful combinations.** Tier 1 supplies BerryBush 1–3 and Beehive 0–2 plus a single Viper-or-Marlback encounter group of 1–2, not independent extra bands (`PopulationTable.cs:350–383`). The actual bushes and hives harvest finite berries/honeycomb; no authored bee retaliation or smoke pacification was found (`Objects.json:25213`, `:25323`). Design around the existing Viper and harvest, or explicitly scope a new mechanic. Avoid claiming a hedge blocks sight: current AI checks `Cell.IsSolid`; the ordinary Hedge's physical solidity alone does not satisfy that (`AIHelpers.cs:59–93`).

The grazer offers a different choice only while two actual reachable unspent field rows exist: one can be eaten, the other remains. Its exact placement and source authority must survive optional refusal and callbacks (`SpreadExplorationActorPlacement.cs:72`, `SpreadGrazerPart.cs:16–68`). Do not substitute a fabricated plant or attach the part to arbitrary regional/quest creatures. Diagnose source-compatible selections versus committed placements versus observable/acted-on sites before increasing frequency. The current local census with no feeding site is a reason to inspect that funnel, not to promise a herd simulation.

**Exchange is available through a different lifecycle.** `WorldTravellers.OnZoneEntered` creates real stock after current player entry, consumes a per-address roll and allows at most three successful encounters per player/world-seed record; the sample is one in eight before route/space/stock refusals (`WorldTravellers.cs:8–81`). It is not a cold-generation owner or an offscreen caravan simulation. Preserve stock, price and timed restock semantics; reserve a verge, then adopt only the actual newly created merchant. Do not manufacture a trader to satisfy a scene count.

**Scavenging is not yet data-only.** `AIHoarderPart.HandleBored` starts a fetch-and-return goal with a real item, home and inventory (`AIHoarderPart.cs:51–83`); it does not perform a persistent deposit into a home container. A collector family needs one-owner pickup/deposit/capacity/death rules, return/load tests and carried-item visibility. Keep this after the simpler slices. A separated work gang similarly needs the existing two-owner allowance and genuinely distinct cooperating roles, rather than adding enemies around every retained cache.

## Regional identity, resources and art

**Beating.** Current Tier 1 has SunStriker, Scorpion, Saltbriar, DryBrush, Bones and a possible WaterTonic (`PopulationTable.cs:435–462`). Saltbriar already yields 1–2 sprigs (`Objects.json:29576`). Desert container forms are urn, sack, crate, bone cache and strongbox, populated through real loot tables (`ContainerPlacementService.cs:59`, `:140`). Reposition an exact rolled source within its allowance; do not add a free second cache. `ComputeBudget` already bounds total containers to 1–6 based on kind/tier/jitter (`:236–260`). Brine/oil/acid hazards are not water supplies: `WaterVesselService.FindSource` requires real usable water (`WaterVesselService.cs:111–131`). A WaterTonic is also not three stored drinks. Any new draw source needs its own explicit resource budget, regional full/empty object, consumption/readout and saved depletion.

**Sodden.** Its authored wilderness avoids human trouble in favor of patient fauna and hazards (`PopulationTable.cs:533–590`); relevant containers are WovenBasket/HollowLog/Crate/Sack (`ContainerPlacementService.cs:87–96`). Reedfrog corpse harvesting can yield FrogOil under existing chance gates; it is not a guaranteed supply. A causeway/log decision can reuse current interactions while preserving dangerous water and the real hazard layer. Wet coating alone does not authorize vessel refill. Do not add explanatory dialogue or hidden systems to Gin Frogs: `Lore/MYSTERY-LEDGER.md:110–118` protects their non-explanation.

**Grovelands and exclusions.** Natural logs and left-behind woven goods fit the actual container policy; crates/chests/urns do not (`ContainerPlacementService.cs:97–106`). FruitingBody has material/destructibility but no Harvestable part (`Objects.json:30890`), so “fungal forage” would be a new action/content contract. Protect Woven Doll, Tenth Fire, authored POIs/lairs and Overwrit's intentional emptiness (`OverworldZoneManager.cs:110–174`, `:749–764`, `:807–828`; `Lore/MYSTERY-LEDGER.md:57–82`). Stump already uses habitat-specific population bands (`OverworldZoneManager.cs:833–845`); later expansion should follow those rather than impose lowland grazing.

**Art readiness is limited.** The Beating library contains 12 static families ×4 variants; Sodden has six ×4 (`BeatingVoxelLibrary.cs:31–38`, `:103–114`; `SoddenVoxelLibrary.cs:31–38`, `:99–105`). Those are useful terrain foundations, not proof of every regional actor/weapon/corpse. The new grazer and finite draw forms are explicitly gated to the actual managed Spread (`QuestFreeSpreadArtLibrary.cs:105–147`, `SpreadPresentationScope.cs:19–48`); other new visitor/portable styling is similarly scoped. Reusing an asset does not enable or validate it in a new biome. Audit visible native owners, all material pieces, clips, attachments, corpse mappings and dynamic effects in each proposed pilot. Preserve regional silhouettes/palettes instead of recoloring every area into the glade. Use existing Examine/action/readout surfaces before adding UI; new animation is required only for a genuinely new physical act.

## Staged expansion contract

1. **Close the current source/visibility uncertainty and accepted Spread slice.** Preserve first failed observations. Record selected, source-compatible, committed, visible and acted-on counts separately. A staged local activation frame stays separate from ordinary discovery. Do not call E5 or E6 complete to shorten this gate.
2. **One narrow Spread slice, then a conditional regional pilot.** Keep exact producer receipts, finite attempts, protected-owner exclusions and final callback revalidation. Reuse primitives through a region-specific admission/budget contract; do not broaden the current exact Spread guards into “any creature/container.” Preserve original owners on optional refusal. Quiet places remain intentional.
3. **Persist aftermath without backfill.** New family assignments require deliberate manifest versioning and fresh-world availability. Current missing metadata takes the legacy path; accepted graph retention prevents reward regeneration (`SpreadExplorationPlan.cs:205–277`). Never reinterpret an absent graph or depleted source as unused budget. Test partial take/draw/feed, death, leave/return, unload refusal and full save/load; actual inventory ownership must survive, not merely an address tag. This is a persistent RPG (`Docs/PROJECT-IDENTITY.md:7`).
4. **Measure choices and costs.** Freeze deterministic metadata cohorts before inspecting generated content. Use the planned multi-seed contiguous routes; record partial/refused walks, not cherry-picked successful pockets. Count distinct observed approach/action/payoff decisions, voluntary bypasses, earned supply use and revisits. An informed automation path cannot prove human awareness. Compare current scene/settings/seed in bounded 60–90-second movement and active-actor captures; retain dirty-redraw, allocation, generation, save-size and retained-graph measures (`Docs/PERF-FOUNDATION.md:13`, `:40`; plan `:363–405`). Old noncontemporaneous samples are not causal evidence for a new feature.

The practical next decision is therefore **more distinct use of current Spread sources**, followed by **one regional source-adoption pilot with its own art and save audit**. This assessment authorizes no implementation and makes no Qud parity claim. Canon/voice authority is the root `Lore/` corpus, not superseded `Docs/Lore/` material (`Lore/README.md:15–34`; `Docs/BIOME-LORE-FIRST.md:11–30`).
