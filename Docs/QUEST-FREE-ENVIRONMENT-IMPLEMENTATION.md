## Goal and sequence

Make the existing landscape support choices that matter outside quests. First, turn selected existing hedge segments into operable field passages with a genuinely longer alternative route. Keep a hauled object's visual identity stable. Then compose useful hauling situations from actual generated props, add explicitly finite cooking sources, and audit predator/prey opportunities. Do not call the whole E4 roadmap complete when its first slice ships.

1. **Field passages (F12):** use one exact generated Hedge, replacing it with one closed, unlocked timber gate. Preserve the boundary and physical bypass. Opening, closing and breaking it use existing actions and persist normally. No additional creatures, stock, rewards or keys.
2. **Hauling presentation prerequisite (F9):** an actual FallenBeam currently chooses its model from coordinates. Reproduce the changing-model defect with real grab/movement, then stabilize only the moving object's choice. Keep static terrain variation intact. This repair alone does not create a hauling encounter.
3. **Heavy salvage and cooking:** inspect and compose actual haulable producers; keep a separate opt-in finite cooking contract so established authored stations retain their intended behavior. New supplies must be explicit budgeted additions, never hidden replacements for refused sources.
4. **Predator/prey and Spread acceptance:** follow the parent plan's separate source/behavior audit, then examine ordinary walks, quiet spaces, repeated decisions, consequences and costs before regional rollout.

A fixed private corpus of twenty metadata-selected chunks per seed (1, 64, 1729) generated 22 ordinary haulable objects across 60 chunks. Thirteen were starting-character-compatible beams/barrels. Only one candidate offered a usable existing gap and parking path: three real drag movements, Speed 100→76→100, ten blocked movement steps versus three hauling and three cleared-route steps. The witness is a marginal roomy-corner case near an edge, not two demonstrated variants. The stable-hash standalone maps are not native Unity seed evidence.

Therefore F9 is not registered, and no unused receipt implementation is added. Its private failing source tests and negative census remain evidence under `Verification/QuestFreeExploration/E4/HaulingFeasibility/`. Preserve protected routes, arrival lanes, the ordinary haulable roll and saved versions 2–5.
