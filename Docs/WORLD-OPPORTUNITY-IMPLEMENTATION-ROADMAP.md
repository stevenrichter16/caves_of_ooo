# Replayable work through compatible physical outcomes

November24,2026 (America/Chicago). **Implementation roadmap, not acceptance or
a promise that every example exists.** This expands the direction in
[Composable world systems](SYSTEMS-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md) and
[Local porters and visible shipments](WORLD-LOCAL-PORTERS-AND-SHIPMENTS.md).

The intended experience is that one run makes repeated workshop deliveries
worthwhile, while another favors portable tools, small loads and different
projects. Local materials and seasonal surplus suggest useful choices. Ordinary
exploration and baseline services remain comfortable. Compatible methods earn
the same resulting service benefit because of what they accomplish, rather than
which tool or menu command the player used.

This requires shared rules for needs, material suitability, work and receiving
goods, followed by varied physical opportunities. More road projects or recipes
alone cannot establish it. Implement small complete connections; a universal
particle simulation, job scheduler or autonomous economy is unnecessary.

workshop access. Fixed public rain collectors offer optional finite water.

The bounded hand-sieve operation and coarse-grit consumer have Editor evidence,
with their documented acquisition limits. G2 now has guided heap harvest200,

passing at210 and250. G2 is published as `7ecee70b3` after Editor qualification
and independent review. The current Mac package includes the change and passed
startup verification. Wider mixtures and discovery remain open. Its finite
size manifest is a useful foundation, not a general material-grade system.

Cinderhold already gives the same future paid sounding-rod availability after
either a stable/improved pump or the existing sealed-seep condition. This is an
accepted bounded authored alternative, not a general physical-outcome service.
All twelve whole-machine acceptance gates remain open. One paid local porter
route and one selected NPC shipment have working candidates. Wider seeded
logistics opportunities remain proposals.
Existing seeded Spread roads and bounded hauling situations are useful starting
points; they do not establish the full proposed logistical experience.

Most place-site and structural repairs still name materials or tools, and
registered processes name their sources and extra ingredients. A first shared
structural payment accepts explicit lining-clay suitability for two recipes.
Receiving and shipment demand still need that suitability boundary.

## Priority 1: make needs and benefits independent of method

1. **Describe one practical need in physical terms.** Start with a bounded need
   such as a sound well lining or suitable bracing stock at a workbench. State
   quantities, access, accepted condition and what usable service follows.

2. **Introduce a shared suitability matcher.** Let a receiver request material
   capabilities and required grades instead of one blueprint. Keep existing
   exact requirements supported. The same matcher must drive previews, payment,
   player handover and later shipment demand.

3. **Separate supply from completed work.** Receiving compatible clay establishes
   stock at the destination; it does not repair the well. A separately validated
   work action consumes actual materials and establishes the supported result.

4. **Commit a common functional result.** Supported repair methods must converge
   on the same authoritative saved condition when they achieve equivalent work.
   Retain independent consequences: sealing a seep need not erase its grove or
   faction effects merely because another method earns the same smith benefit.

5. **Have services read their real prerequisites.** Benefits depend on committed
   function, usable storage, traversable access or measured work, as applicable.
   A tool name, animation, project button or completion message cannot substitute
   for that state. Read-only checks never create missing sites or goods.

## Priority 2: make compatibility truthful and preserve material differences

6. **Define only the material facts needed by real consumers.** Initial examples
   might require repair-clay suitability or sound bracing strength. Particle
   size, dryness, purity and workmanship are distinct facts; coarse material is
   not automatically strong, clean or suitable for masonry.

7. **Separate reusable declarations from altered instances.** Content declares
   supported capabilities and defaults. Saved item Parts retain meaningful
   changes such as wetness or damaged workmanship. Missing or contradictory
   declarations refuse rather than acquiring permissions from descriptive prose.

8. **Select exact payment units through the matcher.** A compatible substitute
   must satisfy the whole requirement, including quantity and condition. Capture
   actual selected owners and revalidate them before commitment; do not count the
   same unit twice across overlapping requirements.

9. **Preserve differences through ordinary handling.** Stacking, splitting,
   containers, trade, processing and save/reload must retain relevant material
   state. Introduce explicit adapters where current payload rules cannot safely
   preserve it; do not broadly permit unknown Parts or flatten unlike grades.
   The two original Gleaners’ cellar measures now form one valid stack. In
   Editor play the exact marked stack was taken, reloaded, carried back above
   ground and spent to reopen the well while two purchased prepared measures
   stayed in the pack. Focused native checks cover split, merge, processing,
   scoped reversal and unrelated fatal interruption.
   [Evidence](Verification/MachineSystems/OriginalClayHandling20261125/README.md)

10. **Prove substitution and refusal together.** Use at least two explicitly
    qualified commodities with one consumer, plus plausible incompatible
    controls. Screening separates declared sizes; it cannot identify unknown
    substances, purify equally sized mixtures or invent recoverable stock.

## Priority 3: give methods distinct costs and useful portable work

11. **Offer genuinely usable ordinary alternatives.** For a chosen load, support
    personal carrying and explicitly quoted smaller batches before advertising
    another mode. Retain local hauling/sled work only where existing load and
    route rules admit it.

12. **Compare complete costs.** Show actions, total carried mass, capacity,
    consumed material, discarded fractions, fuel/water and fees where relevant.
    Include container shells and gear. A portable method may save travel while
    requiring more handling; neither method needs to dominate every circumstance.

13. **Extend work providers through explicit capabilities.** Add a narrow provider
    contract only when a second real supported operation requires it. A sound
    capable alternative tool can qualify; combat damage types, item names and
    equipment ownership alone do not authorize material work.

14. **Make source-side processing physical.** Consume an eligible finite batch,
    produce declared fractions and account for any loss. Leave unwanted material
    in an actual accessible store or ground location. Portable processing cannot
    silently delete the heavy fraction to grant free transport.
    At the finite Morrowfast heap, the one-action portable choice reduces
    carried weight by one pound and leaves exact recoverable grit. The clay
    and grit both have ordinary uses that worked after fresh Continue.
    The original all-in-pack choice remains available.
    [Evidence](Verification/MachineSystems/GroundScreening20261125/README.md)

15. **Keep installed advantages earned and bounded.** A workshop can offer a
    supported process, storage convenience or larger admitted batch. Portable
    tools retain their supported work elsewhere. Current forge supervision and
    own-camp-box limits remain until a specific replacement adapter is qualified.
    An adjacent installed own box now lets the carried sieve work on its
    exact direct stock, with three pounds of real output capacity and one
    inventory transfer saved. Both carried destinations retain
    their ordinary actions elsewhere.
    [Evidence](Verification/MachineSystems/CampBoxScreening20261125/README.md)

## Priority 4: make storage and lasting projects optional investments

16. **Provide real receiving storage.** A shelf, bay or bench needs an actual
    endpoint with entry limits, mass rules where applicable and lawful access.
    Being labelled a loading bay grants neither capacity nor ownership.

17. **Persist stored work without unpaid upkeep.** Goods and completed supported
    improvements survive departure and reload. Do not introduce routine decay,
    maintenance bills or settlement deterioration merely to keep the player
    performing deliveries.

18. **Tie each improvement to an observable change.** A cleared approach may
    shorten a valid route; a larger receiver may admit another load. Recalculate
    the actual relevant constraint. A Road tag or project flag cannot make an
    impassable corridor usable.
    At Gleaners’ ordinary structural work removes a real walking obstruction.
    In seed 64 the same parcel quoted 25 steps and eight drams before work,
    and 23 steps and seven drams after the gap opened. An agreed fare stays
    fixed when a later obstruction changes the live path. The marker also
    explains ordinary loose carries for 11–12.
    [Evidence](Verification/MachineSystems/PorterCrossing20261125/README.md)

19. **Preserve baseline convenience.** Ordinary porter service, shops and useful
    exploration work without completing a new project first. Investments become
    attractive through repeated chosen use; do not place a mandatory loading-bay
    or road-repair step in front of every run.

20. **Keep lasting benefits independent of repeated repair.** Once equivalent
    durable work qualifies, apply the same stated benefit at its ordinary
    boundary. Preserve current merchant lots and prices; a future-stock benefit
    is neither an immediate free item nor evidence of physical production.

## Priority 5: add actual local carrying, then selected NPC shipments

21. **Implement the first paid local delivery.** Start with one real packed
    portable package, lawful same-zone endpoints and a carrier's actual free
    capacity. Quote route, fee, loading terms and interruption before payment.

22. **Give the order one durable authority.** Save reservations, custody, escrow,
    participants, phase and settlement independently of the walking goal. Ordinary
    pickup, unpacking, trade and consumption must respect booked cargo; hiding
    menu actions alone is not a reservation.

23. **Move the same goods through visible ordinary actions.** Loading removes
    the package from its source. Walking obeys doors, hazards and capacity.
    Handover checks the exact receiver and settles once. Full storage or a blocked
    doorstep pauses; neither counts as successful delivery.

24. **Make failure and time honest.** Yield to danger and incapacity. Cancellation,
    theft, death and destroyed endpoints preserve surviving goods and refund only
    unearned fees under stated terms. Initially pause with an unloaded zone;
    reload never manufactures completion or replacement cargo.

25. **Reuse that service for selected ordinary NPC work.** One opted-in supplier
    reserves finite actual stock and budget for a compatible receiver. Physical
    imports use real separate storage and cannot also trigger abstract restock.
    Watching or ignoring a shipment creates no compulsory quest or food shortage.

26. **Compose circumstances, not mandatory project lists.** Vary eligible source
    quantities, load size, destination, existing access and service availability.
    Keep regional identity, lore, protected geometry and mandatory entrances
    authoritative. Generation does not inspect the player's tools to force a build.

27. **Generate an accessible baseline first.** At selected admitted sites, optional
    bends, forks and loading approaches can create different tradeoffs. Preserve
    an ordinary entrance and convenient services. If an optional layout fails,
    omit it without clearing unrelated owners or removing the original site.

28. **Validate routes for the advertised actor and load.** Check standing space,
    width, turns, doors, hazards and endpoint access. A package that a person can
    carry does not prove a beam can be dragged or a sled can turn there.

29. **Give movable goods provenance and finite ownership.** Place real quantities
    from a declared source budget, with supported custody and recovery rules.
    Destruction, consumption and player relocation persist. An exhausted site
    stays exhausted; new orders need goods that still exist.

30. **Save the generated opportunity itself.** Use a deterministic world/site/
    version stream independent of cosmetic randomness. Revisits, seasons, reloads
    and generator updates never reroll an existing site. Promise multiple
    approaches only after validating at least two there.

32. **Expose material state through observable cues.** A suitable tool acts on

    A calendar label changes opportunities; it does not supply work permission

## Priority 8: explain choices and prove the combined experience

36. **Use one truthful preview across entry points.** Explain accepted result,
    selected stock, output, loss, cost and exact blocker in player language.
    Material, tool, shop and receiver readers must agree. Reading and cancellation
    remain free and cannot replace a still-valid selection or reserve goods.
    The finite-source Focus hint now uses the same configured label as the
    world action menu. Editor play confirmed the pallet label, free opening
    and free cancellation. [Evidence](Verification/MachineSystems/TimberOpening20261125/README.md)

37. **Let discovery suggest connections.** A found tool names a few supported
    uses; a stock description explains its useful fractions; a recipient explains
    why it accepts them. Show actual nearby work and observed shipment labels
    without exposing sealed contents or unexplored live positions.

38. **Record causal diagnostics.** Trace exact source units, selected method,
    committed result, receiving owner and benefit qualification. Distinguish
    quoted, paid, paused and completed work. Diagnostics help establish
    equivalence; they do not become hidden reward or capability flags.

39. **Validate transactions, saves and generation together.** Cover aliases,
    partial stacks, competing claims, callback veto, changed grades, independent
    injury, full receivers and exactly-once settlement. Test mid-phase saves,
    fresh Continue, old sites, retained lots, finite budgets and omitted layouts.

40. **Compare complete runs in actual play.** Use ordinary acquisition and normal
    carrying limits, not only supplied fixtures. Measure whether choices differ,
    equivalent results earn equal service and ignored bonuses remain comfortable.
    Preserve useful screenshots and independent review; tests alone do not prove
    discovery, pacing or replayability.

## Additional requirement: overdue shipments have social consequences

41. **Agree and save an expected arrival date.** A recipient knows when the
    package is due, using the same game clock and an explicit grace period.
    Reconcile this with local unloaded-service pause before enabling deadlines;
    time passage cannot fabricate offscreen travel, injury or discoveries.

42. **Let a missed delivery open one saved inquiry.** Record the actual order,
    recipient, known route/reports and noticed lateness. A missed date alone
    does not reveal death, theft or a culprit, and reload cannot dispatch twice.

43. **Use actual people and resources for searching.** A capable recipient may
    search personally; a wealthy/staffed recipient may send available real
    followers or employees with finite expenses/provisions. Ordinary movement,
    threat/retreat behavior, bounded search effort and essential service
    availability still apply. No spawned replacement army.

44. **Make searches meet the player through the world.** Searchers follow known
    leads, inspect the route and learn from supported observations. The player
    who killed a porter can encounter those actual people. Recognized cargo,
    a body or a witness can matter; proximity and global hidden flags do not
    automatically prove guilt. Accusation/justice systems remain separate work.

45. **Close or revise cases through real news and events.** A living delayed
    porter, committed late delivery, recovered goods or recipient death changes
    the case once. Searchers receive reports through supported communication,
    not omniscience. Preserve assigned actors, evidence and budget on save/load.
    Timeliness terms never secretly change the physical benefit of equivalent
    completed work or turn every missing shipment into a compulsory player quest.

## Improvement pass after all 45 are verified

Revisit all 45 requirements and the systems that implement them after the
initial acceptance gates are met. Check the actual player experience using
contrasting routes, builds and regions. Record the concrete evidence behind
each finding and implement the most valuable improvements in connected slices.

- **Content and variety.** Look for useful materials, tools, recipes, regional
  work, customers and lasting projects that give existing mechanics a longer
  life. Check whether each opportunity teaches itself through the world and
  whether different playthroughs support meaningfully different decisions.
- **Quality in play.** Revisit discovery, action previews, controls, pacing,
  consequences, interruption and recovery. Check that the alternatives remain
  practical and that skipping optional work leaves exploration comfortable.
- **Expandability and modularity.** Trace new content through shared Parts,
  capabilities, events, receipts, saves and readers. Identify where adding a
  material, tool, NPC job, region or station requires unnecessary special
  cases. Improve extension points where a concrete second use proves value.
- **Verification and delivery.** Keep what works, rank findings by player
  impact and cost, and verify changes through the same native, actual-play,
  reload and independent review gates. Continue the improvement work through
  the scheduled task after recording the initial 45-item audit.

## Smallest useful slice and contrasting runs

G2's bounded qualification is complete. Next implement one receiver requirement
for suitable repair clay, retaining today's exact
FireClay route and adding one separately authored, genuinely suitable commodity
only after its material state and acquisition are supported. Prove both with the
same matcher and functional repair result, plus incompatible controls. Reuse the
requirement at a second receiver so this is not a special-case substitution.

Make carrying a small batch and one portable preparation useful choices, with
actual remaining fractions. This establishes the outcome/material foundation.
Next qualify P1 paid delivery and P2 selected NPC shipment through that receiver.
Only then add one optional storage/access investment and two admitted seeded
arrangements. Seasonal material work follows with its own complete contract.
These are priorities, not permission to call the entire statement implemented
after the first slice.

In the workshop-oriented run, a lawful nearby source, reusable receiver and
repeated chosen work make paid packages or existing eligible hauling worthwhile.
An optional improvement earns measured storage or travel value. In the portable
run, scattered lighter stock and different accessible work favor small carried
batches, supported source-side preparation and a different lasting investment.
Both runs retain comfortable shops and exploration. Compare one common repair
result directly: suitability and resulting service must agree despite differing
sources and methods. The other projects may legitimately differ.

Later scope includes cross-zone freight, NPC sled/heavy-beam adapters,
multi-trip scheduling, live/hazardous cargo, adjustable meshes, nested mixed
fractions, automatic production and dynamic markets. None is a prerequisite for
the bounded experience above, and none inherits acceptance from it.
