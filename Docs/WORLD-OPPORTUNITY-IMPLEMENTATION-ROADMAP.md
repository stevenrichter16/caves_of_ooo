# Replayable work through compatible physical outcomes

November24,2026 (America/Chicago). **Implementation roadmap, not acceptance or
a promise that every example exists.** This expands the direction in
[Composable world systems](SYSTEMS-DESIGN-PRINCIPLES.md) and
[Local porters and visible shipments](WORLD-LOCAL-PORTERS-AND-SHIPMENTS.md).

The intended experience is that one run makes repeated workshop deliveries
worthwhile, while another favors portable tools, small loads and different
projects. Local materials and seasonal surplus suggest useful choices. Ordinary
exploration and baseline services remain comfortable. Compatible methods earn
the same resulting service benefit because of what they accomplish, rather than
which tool or menu command the player used.

This requires shared rules for needs, material suitability, work and receiving
goods, followed by varied physical opportunities. More road projects or recipes
alone cannot establish it. Implement small complete connections; a universal
particle simulation, job scheduler or autonomous economy is unnecessary.

## Priority 1: make needs and benefits independent of method

1. **Describe one practical need in physical terms.** Start with a bounded need
   such as a sound well lining or suitable bracing stock at a workbench. State
   quantities, access, accepted condition and what usable service follows.

2. **Introduce a shared suitability matcher.** Let a receiver request material
   capabilities and required grades instead of one blueprint. Keep existing
   exact requirements supported. The same matcher must drive previews, payment,
   player handover and later shipment demand.

3. **Separate supply from completed work.** Receiving compatible clay establishes
   stock at the destination; it does not repair the well. A separately validated
   work action consumes actual materials and establishes the supported result.

4. **Commit a common functional result.** Supported repair methods must converge
   on the same authoritative saved condition when they achieve equivalent work.
   Retain independent consequences: sealing a seep need not erase its grove or
   faction effects merely because another method earns the same smith benefit.

5. **Have services read their real prerequisites.** Benefits depend on committed
   function, usable storage, traversable access or measured work, as applicable.
   A tool name, animation, project button or completion message cannot substitute
   for that state. Read-only checks never create missing sites or goods.

## Priority 2: make compatibility truthful and preserve material differences

6. **Define only the material facts needed by real consumers.** Initial examples
   might require repair-clay suitability or sound bracing strength. Particle
   size, dryness, purity and workmanship are distinct facts; coarse material is
   not automatically strong, clean or suitable for masonry.

7. **Separate reusable declarations from altered instances.** Content declares
   supported capabilities and defaults. Saved item Parts retain meaningful
   changes such as wetness or damaged workmanship. Missing or contradictory
   declarations refuse rather than acquiring permissions from descriptive prose.

8. **Select exact payment units through the matcher.** A compatible substitute
   must satisfy the whole requirement, including quantity and condition. Capture
   actual selected owners and revalidate them before commitment; do not count the
   same unit twice across overlapping requirements.

9. **Preserve differences through ordinary handling.** Stacking, splitting,
   containers, trade, processing and save/reload must retain relevant material
   state. Introduce explicit adapters where current payload rules cannot safely
   preserve it; do not broadly permit unknown Parts or flatten unlike grades.
   The two original Gleaners’ cellar measures now form one valid stack. In
   Editor play the exact marked stack was taken, reloaded, carried back above
   ground and spent to reopen the well while two purchased prepared measures
   stayed in the pack. Focused native checks cover split, merge, processing,
   scoped reversal and unrelated fatal interruption.

## Additional requirement: overdue shipments have social consequences

41. **Agree and save an expected arrival date.** A recipient knows when the
    package is due, using the same game clock and an explicit grace period.
    Reconcile this with local unloaded-service pause before enabling deadlines;
    time passage cannot fabricate offscreen travel, injury or discoveries.

    recipient, known route/reports and noticed lateness. A missed date alone
    does not reveal death, theft or a culprit, and reload cannot dispatch twice.

43. **Use actual people and resources for searching.** A capable recipient may
    search personally; a wealthy/staffed recipient may send available real
    followers or employees with finite expenses/provisions. Ordinary movement,
    threat/retreat behavior, bounded search effort and essential service
    availability still apply. No spawned replacement army.

44. **Make searches meet the player through the world.** Searchers follow known
    leads, inspect the route and learn from supported observations. The player
    who killed a porter can encounter those actual people. Recognized cargo,
    a body or a witness can matter; proximity and global hidden flags do not
    automatically prove guilt. Accusation/justice systems remain separate work.

45. **Close or revise cases through real news and events.** A living delayed
    porter, committed late delivery, recovered goods or recipient death changes
    the case once. Searchers receive reports through supported communication,
