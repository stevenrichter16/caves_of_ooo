# The other advertised routes — Gathered and Kept

**Status:** planned, 19 September 2026. Release roadmap step 4, second sentence ([RELEASE-VISION](RELEASE-VISION.md) §A dependency-led roadmap: "Add the other advertised ending routes through the same robust state architecture"). Follows the ending spine ([ENDING-SPINE](ENDING-SPINE.md), ES.1–ES.6 shipped). CoO-original; no Qud parity claim.

## Goal

The Palimpsest's page of the Three Dispositions advertises three ends for the world's slippage — GATHERED, KEPT and RENEWED (`Palimpsest.json` `ThreeEndings`) — and only Renewal can be reached. Build the smallest complete route to each of the other two, at the Root, through the state the spine already owns: one enactment ever, persisted on the player and as a narrative fact, its costs stated before the choice, the epilogue announced, and every act taken on toward it recorded on the closure-ledger and endable by a spoken no. No ending is best; the distinction between them is what each keeps and what each costs.

## Canon (what the code must serve)

- **Gathered (Consume)** — "The Choir reaches the Root through the substrate, and Selen ascends to sole divinity … every self untied and its threads re-woven … Nothing is forgotten; no one remains." ([10_Bible](../Lore/10_Bible.md) §V). *The only ending in which nothing is ever lost* ([11_SecondSpine](../Lore/11_SecondSpine.md) C3). The Choir carries it in the v1 triage ([V1-DramaticCore](../Lore/Design/V1-DramaticCore.md) §II). The Choir's own voice already tells the player of the First Root — "asleep beneath the stump", "deeper than the snapjaws dig … below the deepest of our cathedrals", "we could show you the way" — and that walkers who went down "came back as choir" (`RotChoir.json` `FirstRoot`, `FindFirstRoot`, `ReachFirstRoot`).
- **Kept (Preserve)** — "The Root is sealed with Tepuibone, Memory-Marble, and Mute-Stone — the three name-holding stones — and the seal must be tended forever. The Thinning halts … nothing decays, nothing heals." *The only ending in which the Six survive as themselves.* Reached by working with the Salted (Pale Curation) and the Reader (the Palimpsest and its Recension) separately, since they cannot cooperate ([06_Plot](../Lore/History/06_Plot.md) §Preserve, [01_Spine](../Lore/History/01_Spine.md)). The three stones are the anti-Unsaying materials, "the substances that hold their names hardest" ([05_Spirits](../Lore/History/05_Spirits.md), [08_MaterialCulture](../Lore/History/08_MaterialCulture.md)).
- **The Root** is the narrative centre, deliberately not the traversal hub: world cell (3,3), the Tree's sleeping taproot beneath the petrified stump ([FELLING-WORLD-DESIGN](FELLING-WORLD-DESIGN.md) §0; `WorldMapAuthoring` reserves the cell). What it dreams is Mystery Ledger entry 2 and stays unresolved.
- **Three verdicts on grief** — keep it (Kept), dissolve it (Gathered), finish it (Renewal); the epilogues carry the costs without adjudicating them (C3). No document may call any ending best, true or canonical-preferred.
- Firm constraints ([GAME-STATE](GAME-STATE-2026-09-17.md) §15): Urqu is pressure, not an actor; protected questions stay unresolved; the climax is an enactment, not a fetch turn-in; optional content must not silently become an obligation.

## Semantics (the contract, in player terms)

- **The Root is a place.** On the world map, at the stump's crown north of the Felling-Site, a tier-5 cell named *the Root*: a cleft with a way down, and below it one chamber where the taproot's face is exposed. It dreams; the examine text says so and no more.
- **Two enactments at the face**, offered with [C] and a direction while no ending has been enacted, each with its cost on the menu: *let the Choir in* (gathered: nothing lost, no one left) and *seal the Root* (kept: nothing heals, the Six stay). Each is refused, with the reason, when the player does not carry what it needs: a cutting of the Choir; tepuibone, memory-marble and mute-stone. Refusal consumes nothing.
- **Once and for all**, with the seventh position: any of the four endings ends the offering everywhere. After a Root ending the seventh offers nothing and its exposure has ended; after a Renewal the Root offers nothing.
- **Acts on the ledger.** Taking the cutting from a tendril undertakes *Gather the world*; taking the first stone from the Searcher or the Indexer undertakes *Keep the world*. The enactment closes the act; if the act had been refused, the enactment takes it on again and closes it in one motion (a carried-through act is closed, whatever was said before). Every giver has a `[Release]` line that ends the act aloud. A giver who is gone makes the act lost, and [R] renounces it. While either act is open, naming the world at the seventh names it, with where to end it.
- **No clean-ledger gate** on Gathered or Kept; only the practice path reads the ledger. Other open acts stay open after a Root ending and remain visible; nothing is written off.
- **Costs before the choice.** The giver states the ending's cost when handing over the cutting or a stone; the menu label states it again; the epilogue witnesses it.

## Scope and divergences (recorded, not implied)

- Selen, her name and the Cathedral name-scene are not built; Gathered is enacted through a tendril's cutting. The lore's Reader-Salted reconciliation and Dohren's gate are not built. These belong to the deep arcs, after step 4.
- Consequences after a Root ending are stated in the epilogue and the face's examine text, not enacted by NPCs or the world (bio-light going out, the *sari… sari…* stopping), as with the Renewals.
- Kept is canon-"hard"; here its difficulty is the journey to three givers and the Root, not a puzzle. Recorded so no one reads a lock that is not there.

## Canon guardrails

No ending text resolves a Mystery Ledger entry; the Root's dream is named as a dream, never as content. No "best" wording on any menu, giver line or epilogue. Urqu never appears as an actor. The Choir's tendril "sings all six and takes no side" on Naro. Each cost is stated before the choice.

## Performance and observability

No per-frame work; builders run once per zone; the Root chamber is an ordinary zone. `ending/Enacted` with `path` = `gathered` / `kept` and the reading's counts; `ending/Rejected` with reasons `not_the_root`, `no_actor`, `already_enacted`, `not_adjacent`, `no_cutting`, `missing_stones` (payload lists what is missing); `worldgen/RootPlaced` / `RootRefused`; `closure` records for both acts through `StoryletPart`.
