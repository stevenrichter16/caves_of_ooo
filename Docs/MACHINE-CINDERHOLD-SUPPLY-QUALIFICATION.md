November23,2026. Source/design qualification only. No new shop profile, pump

## Concrete player problem

The existing Drowned Level concern says the smith sells iron spikes, an oak
haft and a leather binding. Its lasting pump repair consumes2IronSpikeComponent,
1OakHaftComponent and1LeatherBindingComponent. The actual WeaponsmithStock
catalogue has no IronSpikeComponent entry; oak haft and binding are optional60%
rolls. Its only guaranteed direct line is Dagger. The promise therefore lacks
dependable catalogue support, independently of whether another local NPC can
provide an occasional spike.

Existing finite reclamation is a useful partial alternative, not a complete
repair kit: ReleasedExtractionTool→1RecoveredIronBlank+1SalvagedTimber, then
that blank→ONEIronSpikeComponent. Dismantling instead preserves a Cinderhook
axe head and FieldHaftComponent; a field haft is not an oak haft. The released
tool is optional fresh placement after dependency validation, never an earned
cached-save item or renewable spawn assumed by this proposal.

## Preserve the real project and its alternatives

Lasting repair is distinct from a2FireClay/600turn temporary collar patch,
four-clay sealing with its separate consequence, field combat and contracted
work. ConcernRegistry's Restore trigger requires StableRepair and excludes
Contracted. Existing PumpRunning consequence grants a5%price improvement and
Quartermaster IronSpikeComponent2 supply; do not invent a SoundingRod production
chain or import route merely to add another reward.

CinderholdCompositionPlan guarantees a Weaponsmith in the workshop.
Quartermaster absence is NOT established: VillagePopulationBuilder attempts
one always and is part of the Cinderhold pipeline. Actual census and placement
can establish whether that recipient exists in a particular retained world.
The pump object and saved repair state do not implement a flooded level,
physical drainage or recurring mining output. Canonical material culture
supports Concord portable, weighed and priced goods; no new lore authority,
universal battery, hidden mine contents or Urqu intent is introduced.

## Smallest candidate and required baseline

First read an actual pump/concern, current genuine smith shelf, Quartermaster
and finite reclamation source state. Establish the missing material through
real menus. Candidate: dependable local smith repair inputs alongside Dagger,
using the existing staple/special machinery. Preserve all original optional
catalogue access and existing prices/custody; do not remove the ordinary way
to buy tools simply to make reclamation compulsory. A local profile and a
global Weaponsmith rollout are different scopes; qualify before choosing.

The meaningful loop is one recovered spike plus one bought spike, or ordinary
purchased supplies, into a lasting pump repair and existing commerce benefit.
It must demonstrate actual consumption, saved StableRepair, concern completion,
visible stock/price consequences and fresh reload. Do not repeat accepted
reclamation branches without this new consumer purpose.

Independent bounded source/design critique recommends the availability gap
over an invented pump reward. Before native RED: audit every complete optional
special cycle and its exact payload permissions; confirm current merchant
declaration/zone eligibility and old-save policy. Countercases include altered
resident goods, reservations, callback rollback, regional opening grants not
duplicated and temporary patch/seal/combat not treated as a lasting repair.
