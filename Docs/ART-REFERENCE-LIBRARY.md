# Art reference library — 16×24 and regional atmosphere

Status: curated and visually inspected on 2026-10-22. Eight external image references supplement the existing imagegen concepts. They are art-direction references, not imported game assets.

## Direction

The user's approved Seed Keeper, cook, tatterjay, grazer and beetle jar define the current scale, palette and simplification. Real photographs inform physical shapes and working arrangements. Official Qud imagery informs readability and use of empty space. Imagegen explores original combinations; Blender and the native Unity capture determine whether they survive at the actual cell size.

Keep the flat top-down presentation and dark green ground. The existing 3D presentation remains recoverable. New props should explain what happens in a place through their arrangement and state, while quiet wilderness remains visually quiet.

## Current clearing references

### R01 — Qud: open ground and readable clusters

![Qud: open ground and readable clusters](https://clan.akamai.steamstatic.com/images/7472163/e27a2d73beddda370475e40a7701edb78a9c2c36.png)

[Source and context](https://freeholdgames.itch.io/cavesofqud/devlog/809524/tutorial-beta-out-now) · [Image](https://clan.akamai.steamstatic.com/images/7472163/e27a2d73beddda370475e40a7701edb78a9c2c36.png)

**Observed:** Sparse vegetation, separated building fragments, dark open ground and a few bright focal colors remain legible together.

**Use in Ooo:** Keep the clearing floor quiet. Group workstations and beds around paths; reserve bright warm pixels for characters, useful objects and readable state changes. Translate visual hierarchy into original silhouettes.

**Application:** Clearing composition; all future biome readability reviews. **Avoid:** Copying Qud sprites, characters, named creatures, dialogue or the screenshot's ground noise.

**Credit / rights:** Freehold Games; Caves of Qud official developer post. Reference only; no asset reuse license established.

### R02 — Ornavik: sheltered earthen oven

![Ornavik: sheltered earthen oven](https://www.ornavik.fr/images/four-a-pain/20120811_140446.jpg)

[Source and context](https://www.ornavik.fr/four-a-pain) · [Image](https://www.ornavik.fr/images/four-a-pain/20120811_140446.jpg)

**Observed:** A clay dome has a deeply dark opening, sits above a woven base, and is protected by a pole-and-thatch structure.

**Use in Ooo:** Retain a broad dome and unmistakable dark oven mouth. Place wood and ash on the working side, with a small shelter or windbreak only where it helps the composition. Use three or four large color masses.

**Application:** Cook's workspace and practical settlement construction. **Avoid:** Photographic surface cracks, elaborate thatch noise, and a wholesale medieval-village theme.

**Credit / rights:** Ornavik historical park; individual photographer not identified on the source page. Reference only; source site reserves rights.

### R03 — Seed Savers Exchange: stored seed batches

![Seed Savers Exchange: stored seed batches](https://seedsavers.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/seed_storage_two-1024x653.jpg)

[Source and context](https://seedsavers.org/learn/seed-saving/) · [Image](https://seedsavers.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/seed_storage_two-1024x653.jpg)

**Observed:** Clear containers expose differently colored seed batches and different fill heights; lids provide a strong repeated top edge.

**Use in Ooo:** Use a few seed vessels with distinct contents/fill silhouettes, and connect storage visually to the existing sorting table and drying rack. The accompanying guide supports a harvest–dry–separate–store workflow; it does not authorize inventing new gameplay states.

**Application:** Seed keeper sorting/storage props and environmental explanation. **Avoid:** Modern brand marks, tiny illegible labels, or making every stored jar look like the approved beetle jar.

**Credit / rights:** Seed Savers Exchange; individual photographer not identified on the source page. Reference only; no image reuse license established.

### R04 — Garden Organic: dry seedhead structure

![Garden Organic: dry seedhead structure](https://gardenorganic-assets.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/images/_1920x1080_crop_center-center_60_line/September-seed-saving.jpg)

[Source and context](https://www.gardenorganic.org.uk/news/new-seed-list-2024) · [Image](https://gardenorganic-assets.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/images/_1920x1080_crop_center-center_60_line/September-seed-saving.jpg)

**Observed:** Dry seedheads curl inward on spoke-like stems, with small clustered terminals and substantial holes between branches.

**Use in Ooo:** Make stored seed bundles recognizable with three terminal clusters and bent stems. For future original flora, distinguish upright shoots from bent dry stalks by silhouette; fit art to each actual owner's growth rules.

**Application:** Drying rack bundles; crop-state concept studies. **Avoid:** Drawing every fine branch, inventing an EmberwheatCrop ripe stage, or treating all crops as the same seedhead.

**Credit / rights:** Garden Organic; article by Alice Whitehead; image photographer not identified. Reference only; no image reuse license established.

### R05 — Sundew: biological strangeness

![Sundew: biological strangeness](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Drosera_capensis_Luc_Viatour.jpg)

[Source and context](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drosera_capensis_Luc_Viatour.jpg) · [Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Drosera_capensis_Luc_Viatour.jpg)

**Observed:** An elongated green leaf has outward-pointing stalks with red tips and bright droplets.

**Use in Ooo:** Try one olive paddle, alternating short teeth, and three restrained red/cream tips. This is a candidate silhouette for an existing adhesive or insect-trapping plant, after confirming its mechanics and biome.

**Application:** Unsettling flora; meaningful biological hazard cues. **Avoid:** Specular sparkles everywhere or implying a hazard on a harmless plant.

**Credit / rights:** Luc Viatour / https://Lucnix.be. Source offers CC BY-SA 3.0 among its licenses. Kept as a linked observational reference; no photograph is incorporated into game assets.

### R06 — Salt crust: preservation made visible

![Salt crust: preservation made visible](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c6/Dead_Sea_salt_crystals.jpg)

[Source and context](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dead_Sea_salt_crystals.jpg) · [Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c6/Dead_Sea_salt_crystals.jpg)

**Observed:** A thick white crust sits above charcoal rock, with an uneven pendant lower edge and some dark rock still exposed.

**Use in Ooo:** Use a pale upper mass with irregular two-pixel lobes, a dark seam and exposed muted material beneath. Apply only to lore-appropriate salt deposits, Curation preservation objects or troughs.

**Application:** Curation objects and salt-preserved surfaces. **Avoid:** Snow-like uniform caps, generic glitter, or adding salt to unrelated surfaces.

**Credit / rights:** Petar Milošević, Dead Sea salt crystals, 2023. CC BY-SA 4.0 on source page. Kept as a linked observational reference; no photograph is incorporated into game assets.

### R07 — Mangrove roots: wetland rhythm

![Mangrove roots: wetland rhythm](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8f/Mangrove_pneumatophores.JPG)

[Source and context](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangrove_pneumatophores.JPG) · [Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8f/Mangrove_pneumatophores.JPG)

**Observed:** Pale tapered root spikes interrupt dark water in sparse groups; spreading trunks bridge some groups while open water remains visible.

**Use in Ooo:** Use three asymmetric spikes of different heights, muted teal bases and occasional bent roots. Place uneven bands around water edges with passage gaps consistent with collision and pathfinding.

**Application:** Sodden banks, crossing edges and quiet wetlands. **Avoid:** An even carpet of identical spikes, hidden blockers or borrowing a real plant name as unsupported game lore.

**Credit / rights:** Shao. Source offers CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL. Kept as a linked observational reference; no photograph is incorporated into game assets.

### R08 — Reclaimed ironworks: old purpose beneath growth

![Reclaimed ironworks: old purpose beneath growth](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Duisburg%2C_Landschaftspark_Duisburg-Nord%2C_Sinterpark_--_2026_--_1491.jpg)

[Source and context](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duisburg,_Landschaftspark_Duisburg-Nord,_Sinterpark_--_2026_--_1491.jpg) · [Image](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2e/Duisburg%2C_Landschaftspark_Duisburg-Nord%2C_Sinterpark_--_2026_--_1491.jpg)

**Observed:** Rigid steel beams retain their mechanical structure while ivy accumulates around joints and hangs from ledges.

**Use in Ooo:** Build a charcoal squared frame, one rust seam and olive growth concentrated at a joint. Preserve evidence of a machine's original function and an accessible service side.

**Application:** Sodden works, irrigation equipment and repurposed settlement structures. **Avoid:** Random rubble, vines covering every surface, or phototexturing original models with the reference.

**Credit / rights:** Dietmar Rabich / Wikimedia Commons. CC BY-SA 4.0 on source page, with explicit derivative/AI attribution guidance. Kept as a linked observational reference; do not transform the photo into a distributable texture without addressing those terms.


## Use during each art iteration

1. Choose at most two relevant reference IDs. State the visible feature to carry forward and the actual game owner/state it serves.
2. Follow the [live brief's playable-greybox gate](LIVE-16X24-ART-DIRECTION.md#required-production-pipeline-and-agent-handoff). Inspect the current native sprites and actual Unity greybox capture. Keep approved actors unchanged.
3. Use built-in imagegen on that capture to explore the observed shapes with the project's palette and cell proportions. Record the reference IDs and exact prompt alongside the concept. Use original project art/screenshots as image inputs; the external photos above are linked observational references.
4. Extract the selected concept's sub-parts and map them to real owners/states. Produce editable native sprites and animation frames. Judge silhouettes first at native resolution, then reintegrate into actual gameplay at 1280×800.
5. Check palette restraint, open paths, state legibility and actual owner semantics. Reject decorative detail that confuses collision, harvest readiness or danger.
6. Keep the concept, final asset and actual Game capture clearly distinguished. Record what changed and why.

When the opening's crop assets are reached, use R04 for silhouette separation and R03 for surrounding work props. Follow the crop audit in [the overnight plan](CLEARING-16X24-OVERNIGHT.md): EmberwheatCrop has seed/sprout then drops produce; MarlrootCrop has a persistent ripe stage. A visually attractive reference never overrides these mechanics.
