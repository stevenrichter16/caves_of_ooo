Later factories cannot replace the Waterskin with an equal-valued Part, empty its charges or disable the policy and have that change accepted as the promised source. The existing complete final-packet proof still protects the committed actor graph. Failed staging leaves the original population receipt unconsumed; missing optional content follows the original working-site path.

- 🟡 **Resolved before implementation:** creating the shell after the loaded actor would introduce a factory callback that could rewrite the already prepared recipient before any complete snapshot. Shell-first creation plus its detached proof keeps the new callback boundary narrow.

- 🟡 **Resolved by identity checks:** equal-valued replacement Parts must not silently become accepted stock. The receipt retains the exact shell Part sequence, physical/Waterskin references, InventoryPart and TacticalSupplyPart.
