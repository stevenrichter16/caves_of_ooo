## Two authored roles, no universal upgrade

**Dousing pilot: the cindercaller at the tempering shelter.** Use the existing actual cindercaller generated at `Overworld.12.10.0`; give that freshly authored owner one SunbladderShell and opt it into emergency self-dousing. Its existing FireMoss, cudgel and EmberSpit remain. The story is direct: a worker carrying fire also keeps a little emergency water. When genuinely burning and able to act, it spends its complete next opportunity using one water on itself. This removes the real flame, cools the body through the existing implementation, and leaves Wet. The shell survives empty. Wetness creates the player's electrical follow-up, while the lost NPC attack creates room to retreat. No autonomous refilling, ally rescue, preemptive soaking or scavenging in this milestone.

**Cover pilot: the soursprayer at the trapper's store.** Use the existing soursprayer at `Overworld.11.11.0`; give only that fresh authored owner one VeilpuffBladder and a retreat threshold of40% for a readable escape window. This is carried trade stock, not a claim that Veilpuff grows in the Spread. It uses cover only while actually retreating, with a currently observed hostile pursuer4–6 cells away. Choose a traced empty impact cell two cells toward that observed contact, so the 3×3 burst is between the two bodies without initially covering its own feet. Require the actual impact, not the requested cursor cell, to match the safe candidate. Refuse if a visible ally or the thrower is in the burst, if cover already blocks the relevant ray, if no traversable retreat step exists, or if stock/control/handling fails. The throw consumes this turn; movement follows on a later turn. A cornered actor keeps current fallback behavior instead of wasting its only tool.

These are proposals to enrich two existing native owners during fresh worksite generation, with exact site/role receipts and no added population. The current builder already creates these roles but does **not** guarantee them in every failed-placement case: preserve its refusal and source-owner budget. Do not retrofit saved generated graphs or change the global creature blueprints as a shortcut. The carried items must be actual factory owners in their native inventories and survive the normal save stream.

## Integration boundaries and mandatory prerequisite

1. Add a small saved opt-in Part whose policy queries actual owned supplies. It owns no independent stock, recharges or global enemy planner. A successful action returns immediately from Kill/Flee; the scheduler alone charges energy and begins the action. Priority: active emergency dousing, existing medicine, retreat cover, current recovery/skills/movement. If a source or transaction refuses, preserve ordinary fallback rather than spending an empty turn.
2. Expose an explicit self-dousing command construction/execution seam with the existing exact origin, target, quantity and transaction checks. Do not query every player menu or directly delete Burning. Keep real `BeforeInventoryAction`, transaction rollback and final-state validation. NPC feedback must be actor-aware and visible-only; failures stay in diagnostics, while existing player refusal feedback remains. The command/executor itself does not charge a second AI action.
3. Reuse `ThrowItemCommand` with the brain's injected RNG and exact inventory owner. Keep real first-impact tracing, extraction and `GasGrenadePart` detonation. Verify payment/refusal/rollback with native whole-goal tests before relying on the existing transaction class name: gas side effects currently occur inside execution. Do not call `Detonate` directly or spawn free mist for the AI.
4. **Before shipping retreat cover, fix Flee's hidden live-position read.** Kill already remembers observed contacts, but Flee reads the live target cell each action. Give retreat a bounded last-observed position; six opportunities is a small compatible starting budget, saved with the goal. Use `TryGetVisibleTargetCell` to update it independently of player FOV. After cover blocks sight, hidden player movement must not steer retreat. This is a required fairness seam, not a broad threat optimizer.

## Acceptance and stopping point

- Native RED→GREEN whole-goal: dousing consumes exactly1 water, ends the existing flame and leaves Wet, deals no simultaneous attack, leaves the same empty shell; empty, foreign, stale, frozen/stunned or vetoed sources preserve stock and fallback. Control: a non-opted-in opponent behaves unchanged.
- Native RED→GREEN cover: a real bladder vanishes exactly once, actual gas plus four-turn opacity blocks distant NPC sight, adjacent contact/projectiles keep existing semantics, and the thrower cannot also move/cast/attack. No safe ray, nearby ally, adjacent pursuer, hidden threat or already-covered ray must not waste the tool. Offscreen observer FOV flags may suppress messages but never change the decision.
- Save/reload and native death before/after use prove literal finite stock and last-observed retreat memory. The same hidden player detour produces the same retreat choice; a visibly reacquired player may change it.
- Native fresh-generation test proves both exact sites/roles receive only the intended additions, placement failure is clean, other owners remain unchanged, old saved graphs gain nothing. Actual Examine reports stock and empty state; a visible use animation/message exposes the cost without a map marker.
- One Play encounter for each role: deliberately ignite the cindercaller, then observe the paid douse; wound and pursue the soursprayer at range, see the mist, circle it, and verify it cannot track the hidden detour. Show useful recovered inventory after one unused and one spent case.

Stop after these two fights are observably different. Do not add universal supply shopping, refill searches, new grenades, extra spell families, ally coordination, NPC crafting or a broad medicine refactor. If timing makes cover practically unreachable at the first site's real HP/range, revise that one authored retreat window and demonstrate it; do not count a fixture-only use as completion. Player-visible appeal and clear animation still require the live review.
