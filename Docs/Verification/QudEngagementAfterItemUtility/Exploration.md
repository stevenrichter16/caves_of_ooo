# Exploration after the item-utility tranche — source investigation

**Status:** read-only investigation and recommendations, 2026-10-08. No gameplay changes. Qud evidence is the available local decompiled source snapshot at `/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project`; this is not a claim about the latest retail version or a tested Qud play session. CoO evidence is the current working tree after the 35 item passes. These recommendations reuse CoO's characters, factions, assets and mechanics; no Qud enemy identities, lore, text or code are proposed for copying.

The strongest next step is to make existing places and actors combine into different problems. CoO already has substantial machinery and authored examples. Another collection of unrelated interaction commands would add less than giving those interactions changing context, useful advance information and consequences for later destinations.

The gaps below are narrower than “no ecology,” “no destinations,” “no persistence” or “no reputation.” None of those broad claims would be accurate.

## 1. Compose two compatible pressures at selected ordinary places

**Qud source fact.** Its generic population builder separately recognizes liquid and wall habitat requirements, placing entities into compatible cell groups. Faction encounters make independent rolls, choose members near the zone's level, equip leader and followers separately, establish their allied relationship, and place party objects and zone objects. This establishes a mechanism for coherent groups overlapping other zone content; it does not prove a complete simulated ecosystem or that every overlap is enjoyable. [Qud Population.cs:20](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/Population.cs:20), [habitat placement:47](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/Population.cs:47), [FactionEncounters.cs:47](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/FactionEncounters.cs:47), [party ownership/equipment/props:69](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/FactionEncounters.cs:69), [independent rolls:121](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/FactionEncounters.cs:121).

**CoO's actual limit.** The modern Spread exploration manifest assigns one primary family per address and the builder dispatches one family. That is a good safety baseline, not evidence that every chunk has only one system: ordinary terrain, population, loot and older situations still coexist. Current source receipts and finite population replacements must remain authoritative. [SpreadExplorationPlan.cs:79](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/SpreadExplorationPlan.cs:79), [SpreadExplorationBuilder.cs:27](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/Builders/SpreadExplorationBuilder.cs:27), [SpreadExplorationPopulationPolicy.cs:12](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/SpreadExplorationPopulationPolicy.cs:12).

There is already a strong composite prototype: the connected satellite deliberately combines a wet lane, cover, two original opponents and a cache, with a separate heavy-frame variant. The gap is a bounded way to combine different existing role families around shared resources or routes across ordinary eligible places, not the first multi-system vignette. [ConnectedSpreadSatellite.cs:16](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/Builders/ConnectedSpreadSatellite.cs:16), [geometry:112](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/Builders/ConnectedSpreadSatellite.cs:112).

**Proposed player situation.** A predator is pursuing a grazer beside a claimed draw point. Waiting for the animals to move, frightening the grazer toward cover, drawing water during the distraction, laying the existing cord snare, and choosing the longer unclaimed approach are different solutions. Interference can bring the predator toward the player or carry a frightened animal into the watched ground. Another seed produces the same useful draw point beside workers guarding a salvage load, changing the obstruction and social risk. In the Sodden, pair a finite salvage opportunity with an actual wet crossing and a patrolling existing predator, while keeping the preparation shelter separate and reliable.

**Bounded implementation.** Use the existing wet-crossing satellite as the control, then pilot two compatible pairings: water + hunt and claimed passage + salvage. Keep one primary family and at most one secondary pressure, a single combined hostile/loot budget, protected arrivals, a traversable bypass and explicit habitat/ownership checks. Use existing actor roles; adapt their configuration contracts only where a pairing needs it. Do not make every existing role eligible everywhere. Persist the selected pair and literal owners once. Keep quiet addresses and refused placements quiet. Expand the wet-crossing prototype only if its added role changes actual decisions; its present cache, cover and opponents do not count as new content.

**Payoff and tradeoff.** This produces more outcomes from the newly useful items without inventing more verbs. It is also the highest generation/AI interaction risk: arbitrary pairings can create unavoidable fights, consume player supplies offscreen or make every zone busy. Acceptance should compare identical seeds with the secondary pressure disabled, prove each resource is charged once, and demonstrate at least two materially different ordinary-player solutions. Start here, ahead of adding another biome-wide catalog.

## 2. Give a small cluster a coherent, mechanical identity across runs

**Qud source fact.** `SultanDungeonArgs` stores distinct lists for walls, primary/secondary/detail templates, objects, encounters, halls and cubbies. Historical properties select matching population tables; the dungeon clones/mutates arguments and uses them for physical rooms, passage furnishing and loot niches. These are semantic generation inputs with mechanical content, not just different names. [Qud SultanDungeonArgs.cs:84](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/SultanDungeonArgs.cs:84), [history-to-content mapping:146](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/SultanDungeonArgs.cs:146), [SultanDungeon.cs:248](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/SultanDungeon.cs:248), [room/cubby/hall placement:1024](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.ZoneBuilders/SultanDungeon.cs:1024).

**CoO's actual limit.** Sodden already varies formation, water condition, geometry and residents. Its authored three-stop circuit nevertheless keeps the same destination purposes and main role owners; the seed variation is a five-way offset of specified layout elements. Spread has encounter-family diversity but does not use this cited manifest to give a small neighboring cluster a shared material/hazard/reward identity. This recommendation extends those systems rather than replacing them. [SoddenCompositionPlan.cs:49](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/SoddenCompositionPlan.cs:49), [SoddenDistrictPlan.cs:29](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/SoddenDistrictPlan.cs:29), [SpreadExplorationPlan.cs:132](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/SpreadExplorationPlan.cs:132).

**Proposed player situation.** Two optional works outside the established Sumphold circuit share one saved regional condition. An old flooded repair yard has connected wet floors, recoverable conducting gear and a dry bypass: insulation, grounding and wicking matter. A dry resin-processing remnant has limited oil, wooden obstructions and useful repair stock: lighting a route is tempting but risks burning supplies. The recognizable regional material and environmental evidence let a knowledgeable player infer what equipment to bring to the second site, while the particular layout and occupants remain uncertain.

**Bounded implementation.** Author three original profiles, each selecting existing compatible terrain/materials, one or two encounter groups, a small existing reward-table bias, and truthful exterior clues. Apply one profile to two eligible ordinary chunks and optionally an already planned lair approach, using cold generation and existing budgeted loot. Preserve the reliable first-hour route, the three Sumphold anchors and existing saves. A profile is an input to builders, not an opaque runtime state manager, and the implementation must not add unsafe random traits to every creature.

**Payoff and tradeoff.** The second destination rewards inference, while another run can reward different preparation. The risk is producing three new repetitive templates or inflating loot. Require the profile to change at least two real tactical decisions and one useful discovery, with unchanged aggregate rewards. No automatic weather timers, offscreen respawns or forced “return every day” loop is needed.

## 3. Extend existing travel notes into evidence for particular expeditions

**Qud source fact.** Reading an object can reveal pre-existing journal entries by secret ID; arrival can reveal/create the corresponding location record; conversation can reveal map, recipe or historical entries while tracking that finite disclosure. The connection is a shared identity across finding, reading, conversation and destination. [Qud RevealSecretOnRead.cs:39](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Parts/RevealSecretOnRead.cs:39), [SecretRevealer.cs:38](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Parts/SecretRevealer.cs:38), [WaterRitualBuySecret.cs:31](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Conversations.Parts/WaterRitualBuySecret.cs:31). The proposed CoO design does **not** copy Qud's ritual or require an equivalent system.

**CoO's actual limit.** Reports already exist and intentionally avoid remote omniscience. The cited Spread ledger stores one record per family and reads eight known families; learning a second site in the same family overwrites that family's prior record. Current report records contain destination, informant and formation, not a source-bound equipment/material clue or a separately dated first-hand observation. Regional request receipts already distinguish historical outcomes from current stock, providing the right honesty model. [SpreadDiscoveryNotes.cs:43](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/SpreadDiscoveryNotes.cs:43), [family-key write:51](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/SpreadDiscoveryNotes.cs:51), [read catalog:60](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/SpreadDiscoveryNotes.cs:60), [RegionalSituationNotes.cs:25](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/RegionalSituationNotes.cs:25).

**Proposed player situation.** A salvage tally in a visited works mentions insulating equipment taken to a particular nearby store. A resident confirms that a flooded approach was passable when they last went there. The player chooses to fetch cold or electrical protection before visiting, then records what they actually saw. After leaving to prepare and returning, the same door damage, depleted container and changed local owners remain. The journal separates “reported equipment” from “I emptied this cache” instead of advertising an omniscient live stock list.

**Bounded implementation.** Add an instance key and source/observation fields to a bounded new-version discovery ledger, retaining existing eight-family records. Pilot three clue chains tied to genuine generated destinations and already-budgeted material/equipment finds: one readable scrap, one conversation lead, one physical observation each. No claim may synthesize an item or force a remote graph into existence. A frozen site plan may support an unconfirmed report; a current item claim must come from the locally observed real owner. Let the player retain multiple useful same-family destinations. Reuse existing journal UI and local examination, adding modest inspectable physical cues rather than objective arrows.

**Payoff and tradeoff.** Equipment searching becomes an expedition the player chooses, and scouting has lasting value. The main risks are misleading guarantees, excessive note volume and duplicate reward creation. Limit chain length to one or two leads, do not make scraps mandatory keys, and test destroyed/moved/looted targets and saves. This is a comparatively small, high-value companion to recommendation 1; it should not become a new quest framework.

## 4. Make exceptional loot owners socially consequential

**Qud source fact.** An exceptional individual's saved related-faction records are generated or authored, exposed through examination, and used for specific reputation consequences on death. The result is that appearance, possessions and affiliation need not all imply the same decision. [Qud GivesRep.cs:271](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Parts/GivesRep.cs:271), [kill consequences:117](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Parts/GivesRep.cs:117), [examination:483](/Users/steven/qud-decompiled-project/XRL.World.Parts/GivesRep.cs:483).

**CoO's actual limit.** CoO already has faction reputation and kill rewards/penalties. `GivesRepPart` derives them from the victim's faction and globally hostile factions. Exceptional lair keepers already have saved identity and a real enhanced carried item, but this system opts into one keeper blueprint, two reward templates, two biomes and tiers2–3; it explicitly avoids traders/conversation owners. The missing part is individual political context and a noncombat decision around that real possession, not “implement legendaries” or “implement reputation.” [GivesRepPart.cs:22](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/AI/GivesRepPart.cs:22), [LegendaryLairEncounters.cs:54](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/LegendaryLairEncounters.cs:54), [real item proof:87](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/LegendaryLairEncounters.cs:87), [carried-item readout:18](/Users/steven/caves-of-ooo/Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/World/Generation/LegendaryLairEncounters.cs:18).

**Proposed player situation.** An original named salvage leader carries a desired enhanced tool, has a documented relationship with a useful local service faction, and is unwelcome to another. Killing them obtains the actual item but can worsen the next town visit. Keeping them alive preserves trade or passage; bargaining transfers that same item, and disarming or stealing it must not cause a replacement to appear. The relationship reason is visible through observation or a reliable local account before the player commits.

**Bounded implementation.** Pilot two authored relationship patterns on a small cold-generated subset of existing exceptional encounters, then seed their selection. Add at most two saved per-person faction relationships and a truthful examination summary; compose their one-time consequence with existing reputation handling instead of paying both systems accidentally. Introduce one limited peaceful transfer option on an appropriate original encounter owner using the existing trade/ownership paths. Do not retrofit every hostile keeper into a merchant or grant universal diplomacy. Preserve actual item identity, causal death attribution and already earned reputation.

**Payoff and tradeoff.** A valuable find becomes a strategic choice involving later destinations, rather than an automatic kill-and-loot instruction. This is the largest social-design step here: opaque faction losses feel punitive, and repeatable gifts or cloned deaths can become reputation farms. Defer it until the first two recommendations demonstrate clear ordinary-play value; require pre-action readable stakes and once-only save-safe consequences.

## Recommended order and evidence gates

1. Implement a small pilot of recommendation 1 together with one recommendation 3 clue chain. Reuse the new 35 item uses in the situation rather than expanding their command list.
2. Evaluate three ordinary builds over a fixed sample of fresh seeds: record actual decisions, unavoidable engagements, distinct effective solutions, resource use, and what remained on return. Native assertions can establish outcomes; a human playtest is still needed to establish surprise, comprehension and replay appeal.
3. If the pilot is perceptibly different across seeds, add recommendation 2's shared regional profiles. If it merely adds actors to the same fight, revise layout, incentives and roles first.
4. Add recommendation 4 only after its faction stakes and peaceful transfer rules are designed against CoO lore and existing reputation behavior.

All implementation would follow the repo's RED → GREEN → adversarial → native-play workflow, preserve ordinary spawn/arrival safety and finite source budgets, and keep literal saved worlds intact. New repeated-run variety must not be confused with regenerating the player's cleared destination. No implementation or runtime verification of these recommendations is claimed by this investigation.
