# Two ordinary routes to rebuild Tine’s fishing weir

November 26, 2026 UTC. A fresh seed 2 game began in Gleaners’ Glade at
40 HP with the ordinary starting kit and 50 drams. Both branches continued
from the same tick 360 save beside the elder. By then the player had found
the gapped weir beside the well, read its repair bill and asked the elder
about The Gapped Weir. The elder identifies the contract and its catch
tithe. Navigation was guided, and all gear, stock and money came through
ordinary GameBootstrap movement, world actions and trade.

The paid route used the starting purse and left the site ready quickly.
The hand route was a longer investment. Tine’s quartermaster sold its
current oak haft. In Gleaners’ Glade the warden defeated the two marauders
near the settlement. The player recovered the loose sword and armor from
the pile. A villager died in that fight. At Peat Works the collapsed drying
frame yielded four timber, and the unlocked cutters’ locker supplied the
second oak haft. Back in Tine the player sold the exact sword to Vellin and
bought his stocked hatchet. The object menu’s lasting repair used two
timber and both oak hafts in one action. Ordinary merchant trade worked
before the weir was fixed.

The selected shelf had no perch immediately after either repair. It
stocked four on ordinary return. A second Tine merchant also received
its own lot. These are physical goods for sale. The two branches retain
their different method records, market rates and the contract’s stated
tithe. The mason’s announcement describes a day’s work. The saved
contract repair takes effect at payment tick 360. That clock and prose
alignment remains open.

Both actual branches reached 40 HP and preserved the bought fish and
remaining stock through fresh Continue. Independent review checked the
saved entity identities, quantities and purses. This qualifies one
ordinary acquisition comparison for #20, #37 and #40 using the existing
runtime. Shop availability, elapsed time and settlement fighting can
vary across starts. Wider player-led discovery, seed variety and full
