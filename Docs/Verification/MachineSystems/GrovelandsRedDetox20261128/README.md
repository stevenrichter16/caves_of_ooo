# Grovelands red growth to mending tonic

An earned seed 65 Continue began in the Fen with 38 of 40 HP. Three
ordinary steps reached the visible red growth. Harvest at tick 4060
spent the original source 51714 and yielded two grove-red in one stack.
The usual atlas route brought the same material to Sill still 7412.
No goods, time, movement or healing were supplied by the harness.

Near the still, the Pack Recipes page showed the exact one-fruit price
and the cleansed pulp. One action paid a fruit and left the original
stack with one unit. The pulp had Vital 2 and no Toxic property. The
usual Brew page made a mending tonic with a 2d4 healing payload from
that pulp. The raw fruit's mix preview offered no healing. Previewing
and closing kept the same material amounts and game time.

Fresh Continue preserved the exact unspent fruit, new tonic, position,
turn and 38 HP. Selecting Drink on that tonic removed it from the pack
and restored the traveler to 40 HP. The second fruit stayed available.

recipe and brew already show their costs and outputs. This pass verifies
