# Rain exposes loose clay at Morrowfast

A fresh western Morrowfast allotment can place one low seam beside its
existing clay bank. On the normal map a firm line changes into a bright,
loose cluster in rain. It breaks into smaller marks as the rain ends and
leaves a scrape after gathering. The cue follows the local clock and the
same saved stock as the collection action. Fog conceals it. The other
clay sources keep their separate stock.

The player continued the ordinary seed 2 save with the sieve they had
already made from timber, cord and a bought spike. They travelled west by
world map and walked to the field. These images show normal exploration
from (58, 11). The seam is one cell southeast of the avatar. The time of
day, log and nearby creature positions vary. The firm and loose images
are from the first branch. Waiting beside a shambler killed that player
before the shower ended. The retry loaded the native arrival save and
defeated the nearer shambler with ordinary spells before returning to the

At tick 3000 the reader confirmed one packet was still available. The
player gathered it at 3010. The damp packet offered no screening action.
It dried on the ground through ordinary waits, was picked up at 3280,
and screened at 3290. Two fire clay went into the pack and one coarse
grit stayed at the player's feet. Both outputs were put to use. The
player repaired the original catch well at 3330 and drew cool water.
The one grit made firm footing on the well approach. The original sieve,
ordinary bank and dry spoil remained.

A fresh Continue at 3340 kept the exact spent seam, repaired well,
sieve and eleven turns of grit. The two earlier clay sources were still
present. The final generation order places the seam after the existing
catch frames and stand so it cannot displace them. A second fresh visit
kept all three placements and the same seam cell. The player gathered
one damp packet during rain at 2410 and saved all four files.

The playtest proves one layout and one useful wet-weather
deposit. Existing tests also cover the grace deadline, refusal in a
later shower after collection, and controlled flat and ASCII appearances.
This receipt does not qualify other condition-driven sources or a Mac
package. The native fixed-map presentation uses glyphs.

passed 154 cases with one opt-in test skipped. It includes a

inventory, presentation and saved-Part checks. The exact archive and
