# Visible marsh gas warns about exposure

Look and Interact now recognize a visible, live poisonous gas cloud
and identify it as a hazard. The detailed line points to a wearable
filter. The visible cloud and the poison behavior must belong to the
same current entity. Empty, hidden and stale clouds give no warning.
The distinct veil mist keeps its sight warning.

## Ordinary Brine Camp play

The traveler carried the Filter Hood from the original Peat Works
locker. Ordinary travel recovered the previously purchased torch
from the ground in Sill. The same held torch lit natural Brine Camp
mire 11384 at (44,12). This exposed an existing environmental source
using an ordinary action. At tick 3000 the player stood on the dry
western approach at 40 HP and was already burning. The target cloud
had 60 density.

The old Interact menu named the cloud and reported bog mire. The
revised menu gives the poison warning and filter advice their own
line, with bog mire on the ground line below. Looking spent no turn.

Two runs from the same approach save took one ordinary east step.
Both experienced an entry dose and the round’s surviving-cloud dose.

| Gear | Intake per dose | Total gas damage | Separate damage | HP after |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Hood carried | 100 | 10 | 1 acid and 1 fire | 28 |
| Hood worn | 50 | 2 | 1 acid | 37 |

Both acquired lingering poison with two base damage per turn. Duration
was rolled independently. The gas reduction accounts for eight HP of
the difference in the matched baseline. The extra fire hit is reported
separately. The revised replay repeated the worn two-hit gas result
and fresh Continue kept the exact hood, source, torch and effect.
This later worn run had 35 HP after one acid and two separate fire
damage in addition to the two gas hits.

## Checks

The old native fixture passed four controls and failed the new
visible-cloud expectation. The first compact layout then failed the separate-lines case, which
also reproduced the live cropped mire text. The final native results
passed 243 cases and skipped one explicit save-contract generator. It covers the live

empty and split owners, a separate veil, existing gas cadence and
saved effects. Independent source and recorded gameplay review.
