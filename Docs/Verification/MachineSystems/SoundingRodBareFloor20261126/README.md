# Sounding on dry bare cave stone

The sounding rod and trail marks share the check for a dry, visible
piece of natural cave floor with no separate floor object. A valid
bare spot uses the existing depth palette to determine the rod's range.
The rod's ordinary floor route still works. A roomed gap, protected
column, wet tile, obstructed cell, hidden tile or replaced active zone
does not gain the new allowance.

## Actual play

The private Cinderhold continuation held the exact previously bought
rod `30387`. The starting character inherited 412 HP and 10 drams from
that audit. Ordinary movement crossed the surface, took two stairs and
walked out of the authored column into ordinary depth two. The authored
approach refused a sounding. The natural cave's entry supported its
usual sandstone range of 12 paces.

At tick 11800 the player stood at `(38,2)` in `Overworld.6.7.2`.
The tile was visible, dry, interior and contained only the player.
Before the change the real pack menu refused the rod on that spot.
After Continue from the same save, the same menu offered **sound the
ground** at 12 paces. The strike cost one action and made the usual
noise. The rod recorded open ground southeast and a bore south as
bands in its notebook. The ordinary log action read the entry for free.
The player stayed on the same bare tile. Fresh Continue matched the
same rod, one notebook entry, noise timestamp and bare ground exactly.
The capture shows the guide before a small wording clarification.
An independent review matched all eight archived files. The before and
after observations at tick 11800 matched the same physical state after
sorting the ordinary stair list. The usable range was the changed result.
