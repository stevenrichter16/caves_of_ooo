# A bought lake perch becomes a field meal

The pack lists Cook for raw food with a recipe. When no usable fire is
within reach, the action stays visible and explains why it is unavailable.
The item's details keep the healing and handling information and show
the cooking guidance in its own section. Raw Eat remains available.
The pack and the guide use the existing station rules.

## Ordinary trade and a field fire

The trial began from the prior seed 2 Tine purchase after the repaired
weir restored the fish to the shelf. The traveler had paid 15 drams
for one perch. Continue restored that exact raw item at tick 2900 with
40 HP. Away from a fire, the unavailable Cook action kept the fish,
health and tick unchanged.

The traveler used the ordinary atlas and walked to dry ground at
`(41,9)` in the Gleaners' Glade. The original finite cooking fire
`2137` was diagonally adjacent, at `(42,8)`, with a temperature of
463.1 and 200 fuel. Both the Cook row and the item guide showed the
action was available. One Cook took the game from tick 3050 to 3060,
changed the one raw catch into one roasted perch `7511`, and kept
the traveler's 40 HP. The fire cooled during that turn and still
held 200 fuel. Fresh Continue restored the exact roast and fire.
The restored meal's details show that eating it heals `2d4+2` HP.

## The same fire after cooling

A separate Continue from tick 3050 restored the exact raw perch and
the same ready fire. Ordinary waits cooled it to 125.3 at tick 3780
while its fuel stayed at 200. The Cook row and the item guide agreed
that no usable fire was within reach. Selecting Cook kept the same
raw fish, fire, 40 HP and tick. At the captured text size, the
current availability line is on the second page of the item guide.

## Verified scope

The first native reader omitted the cooking guide. Two focused reader
cases reproduced it, and six earlier cases reproduced missing readiness
action or guidance. All 272 targeted native cases passed on the final
source. Coverage includes the existing legacy station rules, a finite fire
at its heat threshold, exhausted fuel, multiple adjacent sources, stale
actions, and the item details from both the row and the action popup.

Independent source and gameplay reviews checked the action and guide,
the exact carried items, and all three four-file archive hashes. The
Editor stopped and restored the checked input and save settings.
All 876 checked normal Quick files and 87 unrelated tracked edits matched.
