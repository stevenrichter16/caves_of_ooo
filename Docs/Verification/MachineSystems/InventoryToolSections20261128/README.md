# Tools by use

The Tools tab groups visible equipment by its primary work. Gathering &
processing includes the scraper, hand sieve, cutting tools and settling
tray. Other sections cover Crafting & repair, Brewing & treatment,
Water & liquids, Fire & light, Camp & hauling, Exploration & surveying,
and Traps & fishing. A tool has one stable section and keeps all of its
operations in the detail pane. Empty sections stay out of the list.

Category labels are separate from item rows. The first visible item
keeps its heading during scrolling, and a heading always has a row
under it. Matching copies retain Pack grouping. The compact rows keep
the exact item, count, current state, readiness and location. The right
pane carries the longer explanation and the ordinary action button.

In the same continued settling-tray save used for the original Tools
tab, 11 tool rows fit at once, up from eight. Home and End kept the
beginning and late sections available. The two trays shared the
gathering heading with separate carried and placed rows. G browsed
13, then 21, then 13 rows at tick 3610. Selection reached placed tray
2134 and its normal Gather action produced one vent scale at 3620.
Vent 2105 had three catches left. The other vent was unchanged.
Browsing and gathering kept 343 HP.
