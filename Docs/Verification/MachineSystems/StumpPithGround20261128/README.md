# Prismreed pith clears a thin slick

A ground Examine names an available cleanup when a visible temporary
film is within reach and the traveler carries usable pith. The same
ground actions spend one pith to clear one chosen film. The physical
pool, recurring sources and other ground layers retain their own
rules. A thin slick can also expire in ordinary game time.

## Ordinary play

The seed 65 Continue starts in Stump at tick 7580 with 38 HP. The
traveler walked to the ripe prismreed at (10,2) and gathered the
original crop into the pack at tick 7900. It gave two pith and one
seed. The neighboring young crop remained at its original stage.
The return through Stump cost four HP.

The traveler went to Sill and paid the ordinary provisioner 49 drams
for the original stack of three lamp oil. One unit spread onto grass
at (30,19). The player-laid slick was visibly marked and had seven
turns remaining at tick 9150. The original Examine reported a 40%
slip chance and fire hazard without a cleanup lead.

The revised Examine named one carried prismreed pith and the ground
action. Opening the reader spent no time. Wick spent one pith and
one turn and removed the exact film. Two adjacent original puddles
each kept 120 water. The destination reader lost the slippery
warning. An ordinary step then stood on the cleared square at
tick 9170 with 34 HP. Fresh Continue preserved the position, the
cleared ground, the remaining units and both neighboring puddles.

A separate replay spread one more oil directly over puddle 7141.
Examine still showed the thin oil and its slip chance. The action
menu and reader offered no Wick. The puddle kept all 120 water.
This branch was discarded after the control.

## Checks

The action and clue share carried-item and exact ground eligibility.
The reader uses the current ground authority before offering the
lead. Fog, unexplored and distant cells, no carried pith, inactive
and replaced zone instances, and oil over a stocked physical pool
are covered. Native baseline had five passes and one expected
missing-clue failure. The first revised source reproduced two stale
zone failures. Final native run passed 218 cases and skipped only
the explicit save-contract regeneration helper. One test build
needed the public detached fixture constructor before it could run.

Independent source and gameplay reviews matched the final changes,
raw receipts and screenshots. The dry crossing verifies the removal
and movement. It leaves the untreated slip roll unmeasured.

This was native Editor play on Apple M5, in the existing Flat view.
The current Mac app predates the clue. No performance benchmark
or new package was run for this slice.
