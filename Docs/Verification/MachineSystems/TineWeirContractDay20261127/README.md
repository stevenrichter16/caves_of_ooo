# The Concord takes one day to rebuild Tine’s weir

November 27, 2026, America/Chicago. A fresh Classic seed 2 traveler
started in Gleaners’ Glade with the ordinary kit, 40 HP and 50 drams.
The traveler reached Tine by the atlas, examined the same gapped
fishing weir and hired the Concord through the elder’s dialogue.
Navigation was guided. All travel, waits, reading, dialogue, payment,
saving, Continue and trade used the normal game inputs.

| Observation | Tick | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booked | 600 | Paid 45 drams once. The elder and weir show day 2 at Dusk. |
| Fresh Continue and early return | 600 and 620 | Same broken weir, 5 drams and original deadline. No perch at either merchant. |
| Last ordinary action before the deadline | 1790 | Weir still fouled. No tithe, report or perch. |
| One more wait | 1800 | The same weir became holding and working. The tithe took effect. |
| Normal departure and return | 1820 | Both original merchants stocked four perch each. The elder could take the report. |
| Elder’s report | 2170 | Earned 15 drams and 60 experience once. |
| Trade and fresh Continue | 2900 | Paid Vellin Mosswick 15 drams for one perch. The exact carried fish, his remaining three, the closed report and the repaired weir survived. |

The game records the promised deadline at payment. Ordinary actions,
travel and Continue use the same saved clock. The Concord reserves
the site while the work is booked. Any existing patch can expire
during that time. The story report becomes available on the next
normal narrative update. The perch follow the eligible arrival
boundary that both lasting repairs share. Both merchants retain their
identities and their separate stock. No elapsed computer time is
used.

Hand repair spends the authored timber and oak and keeps the hatchet.
It remains immediate before booking. Its prior guided acquisition
trial used ordinary merchant stock and Peat Works salvage.
