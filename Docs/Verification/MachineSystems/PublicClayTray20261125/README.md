# Public loose clay storage at Gleaners’

November 25, 2026 UTC. One eligible newly generated Gleaners’ district
can have a public tray at (42,9). It holds up to two physical measures
of compatible lining clay. A person can put one pound there, walk away,
and come back later. Public access means anyone beside the tray can
collect the stock. Repair is the ordinary separate action. The original
one-box receiving shelf remains available for packed deliveries.

The tray extends existing container admission and transfer. It counts
units rather than entries, preserves both original clay marks through
ordinary splits and merges, and rechecks exact location and stock at
commit. Full, unsuitable, unreachable or conflicting stock is refused.
Only enabled cold generation at Spread manifest v25 can place the optional
fitting. Its spot must be clear and reachable from the existing cellar
exit. Existing saves retain their world and container modes.
