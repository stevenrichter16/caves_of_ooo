# Sill’s settling pocket can yield whole bank gravel

Clear the original pocket, then either hand-pick one measure of silver
sand or take the whole two-pound packet. Both choices spend the same
saved deposit. A hand sieve separates the packet into one silver sand
and one coarse grit. The fixture records no extra stock. The usual
partly covered and spent states still apply. The captured material
manifest is checked at the source and again on the actual output.

## Ordinary gameplay

I continued the retained seed 65 traveler at West Morrowfast at tick
520 with 50 drams and neither tool. In Morrowfast, Orrit sold exact
sieve 9240 for 29 drams and Sella sold exact flask 9244 for 20. I
walked to the Sill pocket and filled that flask with all twelve water
at tick 1520. That exposed the original deposit 12284. Its old menu
had one harvest choice. The same saved state in the updated runtime
had both choices and still had just one unspent deposit, 32 HP and
one dram.

The whole-gravel choice took one turn, produced the exact packet
12314 and left the scrape. Screening at tick 1540 used that packet
and the original sieve. It yielded sand 12315 and grit 12316 and
kept the full flask. A soot gremlin injured the player on the approach
and during the work. In this occupied cell, the shared popup heading
named the gremlin even when the player had selected the deposit. The
harvest choices and their receipts bound the exact deposit.

The player scattered the one grit onto the west bank step `(41,17)`
and stepped onto it at tick 1560 with active traction. The grit later
expired. The player learned about well work in ordinary dialogue,
then used the sand to leave an `exit` mark on stone at `(72,11)`.
That action consumed the exact sand at tick 2050. The well remained
fouled on this trip. Fresh Continue kept the original spent pocket,
the exact route mark 12318, both purchased tools, the flask’s twelve
water, and the traveler's 18 HP and one dram.
