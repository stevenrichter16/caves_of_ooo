# From a found quarry packet to a useful mineral

An ordinary shallow cache supplied one five-pound Quarry Sweepings packet.
An earned hand sieve separated it into one pale-salt and two silver sand,
discarding two pounds of rock dust. The pale-salt enhanced the player’s
original dagger at Cinderhold. The other branch used one sand to mark the
actual cave return path.

## Find and screen the packet

The guided seed 2 route started at an earned version 26 save at tick 1150.
It carried the original sieve 9271 and starting dagger 2716. The player
walked to Cinderhold’s stairs and descended twice. The first level contained
no ore cache. The opened cache on the second level contained one Glow-Quartz,
which the player left. A native walk across the southern edge reached the
neighboring Spread cave at tick 1770. Its open ore cache 19765 at `(16,6)`
held one packet and a separate pale-salt. The packet’s reader explained the
fractions, the lost dust and how to make a sieve before the player took it.

The native take put packet 19767 into the pack at tick 1890. The cache’s
original pale-salt 19766 stayed inside. One ordinary screening action at tick
1900 consumed the packet and put pale-salt 19768 and two silver sand 19769
in the pack. The sieve and both healing tonics remained. The route followed
observed cave geometry with map and position checks. One cached find leaves
the odds, regional breadth and player-led discovery as separate questions.

## Two outcomes from the same screened supply

The native return walk reached bare, dry floor beside the north exit at
`(7,1)` at tick 2000. On the previous runtime that floor offered grit rows
but no marking action. Strata generation can leave exposed floor without a
terrain object. The updated action accepts that support in an active ordinary
natural cave. Existing walls, fixtures, coatings, gas, liquid, visibility and
one-mark limits still apply. A changed floor before commitment refunds the
material while preserving the independent change.

The original-runtime branch went back across the boundary and up both stairs
to Cinderhold’s actual forge. An ordinary action spent only the screened
pale-salt at tick 2640, enhancing the exact dagger 2716. Fresh Continue kept
the pale-salt-edged dagger, both sand units, the sieve, 40 HP and three drams.
The later Spivenor continuation records a successful landed bonus and
its saved effect. [Ordinary field use](../QuarryPaleSaltFieldUse20261126/README.md)

The updated-runtime branch continued the exact tick 2000 save. The player
used one sand at tick 2010 to mark the north exit from the existing floor.
One step back let the player focus the note in the world. Fresh Continue
kept the same marker 19770 at `(7,1)` and the player at `(7,0)` at tick 2020.
The other sand, pale-salt and sieve stayed in the pack.

## Verification scope

Two focused tests first failed because the cave offered no action. Five
additional controls cover a detached or inactive floor, a fixture, a wet
cell and a hidden cell. The payment test adds a real coating during the
transaction to check rollback. The full save case verifies the same marker
and note on the same carved floor. All 77 final route-work, sieve and
nearby ecology cases passed with no compilation errors. Independent review
checked the source and the exact archived play. The Mac package predates
this update.
