Peat Works north-entry save at tick 420, with 40 HP, 28 drams and the
empty flask bought at Sill. The same world contains the actual mire
shortcut used in the earlier flask and detour comparison.

The local approach, stroke and crossing took 17 actions. The original
comparison from the same northern geometry needed 23 dry walking
steps or 17 actions with a bought flask. The flask contains the mire
for transport. The pump keeps the flask empty and leaves a physical
mire pool at the chosen outlet. The destination is a real workshop
the player can inspect, haul, repair or dismantle through its usual rules.

Wildlife shaped the price of the outing. The initial optional salvage
fork died immediately after taking four timber from the drying frame.
Native Load resumed the original 420 save. On the successful branch,
snakes also prevented resting at the town fire. The whole route spent
two tonics and two pieces of dried meat. The 17-action result measures the crossing
after acquisition, and the previous branches remain the local route
comparison. This is guided play on a retained layout with existing runtime.
