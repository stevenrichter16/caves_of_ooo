# Late-cycle frost lichen at North Works

Fresh version 31 North Works can offer one shallow lichen crust.
Its optional placement requires two dry approaches, a safer route than
the deep yard and the original worksite geometry. The alembic, mendleaf
and ordinary lichen remain. The shallow crust yields one ordinary frost
lichen late in the existing four-phase cycle. Harvesting it removes
the source. The saved approach and depletion survive unload and reload.

## Actual play

A fresh seed 64 Duelist arrived from the Reference Glade at tick 50 with
40 HP. Two ordinary steps reached `(40,10)`, alongside shallow crust
`6957` at `(39,9)`. At tick 70 the usual Examine action explained the
late-cycle harvest and the deeper ordinary patch. The world menu offered
no harvest.

The private audit advanced only the shared clock to tick 28870 to reach
phase three. It kept the same terrain, player and source. Native **gather
one frost lichen** earned exact item `6958` and removed the shallow source
at tick 28880. The ordinary lichen, still and mendleaf were present.

The audit supplied a single canonical flask with two units of water to
exercise field use on a safe shoulder. Native **pour one water northeast**
moved one unit into puddle `6960` at `(41,9)` and kept one in the flask.
Native **freeze wet ground northeast** spent only the gathered lichen.
At tick 28900 the tile had three turns of ice and the puddle still held
one unit. Health stayed 40. This branch verifies an actual earned lichen
and ordinary actions using a supplied flask and a controlled calendar.
Fresh Continue kept the same three-turn ice, puddle, flask volume, used
lichen and absent shallow source. An independent review matched all 12
archive files and the native screenshot. The previous version 30 scout
was a separate game with the same site layout in this comparison.
