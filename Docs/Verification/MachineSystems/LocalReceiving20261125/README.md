ordinary carrying, and receiving a local parcel. This is the supply and

beside the cellar landing and an open one-box shelf by the lined well. The box
accepts two measures of fire clay, prepared lining clay or a mix. Its actual
contents add to its weight. The shelf needs a full qualifying box that weighs
no more than eight pounds. Use normal inventory actions near the box or shelf.

leaves the well alone until somebody uses the ordinary repair action with the
clay. Older loaded sites retain their saved fittings and ordinary repair paths.

Receipt checks enforce exact physical custody, access, material grade, finite
capacity, and local identity. Repeated provenance bits cannot go into the same
box. For these fittings, informational observers run after the transfer commits.
A failed observer cannot reverse the transfer after the new owner dies and
spills the box. Existing unrelated container actions retain their ordering.
