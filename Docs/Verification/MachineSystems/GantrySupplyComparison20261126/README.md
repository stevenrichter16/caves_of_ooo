# Two ordinary sources for Gantry's grain

Both fresh version 31 games began with the ordinary kit, 50 drams and
40 HP. The seed and the travel guidance were controlled. Actions used
the normal keyboard, world menus and saves. No materials, stats or
positions were supplied.

| Route | Physical source | Completion | Reward and real stock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seed 64 | Two marked rows north of Gantry, at `(3,3)` and `(4,3)` | Tick 810, 25 HP | Six drams and one tonic. Brinna `6930` owns exact sheaves `9339` ×2. |
| Seed 2 | Public Gleaners' rows `2150` and `2157` | Tick 420, 40 HP | Six drams and one tonic. Kesset `6953` owns exact sheaves `2728` ×2. |

In seed 64 the read-only merchant action named the northern field, two
sheaves and the reward before acceptance. The player followed that local
direction and cut the two rows. Three wildlife hits cost 15 HP. The
Gleaners' public rows stayed uncut. Seed 2 cut two public rows before
visiting Gantry and left the third uncut. The merchant accepted that
already-carried grain. The existing acceptance action generated the
marked field offscreen. Both marked rows remained ripe with zero
explored cells in that zone.

Both ordinary handovers moved real inventory into stock. The purse rose
from 50 to 56 and the original two-tonic stack became three. Both
four-file save archives were verified before exit. Fresh Continue kept
each completed request, reward and exact stock. The seed 2 Field Notes
show the settled receipt and its once-only payment. Independent review
matched both raw saves, backups, harvested rows and actual recipients.

This check exercises existing runtime. The routes and world seeds differ,
so the observed time and injury are specific to these journeys. It adds
one current cross-seed comparison for items 37 and 40. General discovery,
balance, regression and the complete machine gates remain open.
