# A provisioner's satchel and ordinary supplies

The provisioner profile includes an empty field satchel. Its existing
identity check refused the container parts, which stopped publication
of the entire profile. Existing legacy shelf goods could still be sold.

The merchant check now admits the specific ordinary empty satchel.
Changed or filled containers remain distinct. A normal empty shell
preserves its own identity and can enter the existing finite stock and
restock rules. The correction keeps the transaction's existing payload
and custody checks.

The native Sill route continued from the restored watch lantern. Before
the player's first trade visit, Kesset Mosswick 18164 had one lamp oil
on his legacy shelf and no saved stock profile. The normal trade screen
established the profile, brought the exact oil stack 18177 to three,
and kept the original six-slot satchel 18182. The player bought that
empty satchel for 22 drams, then used its normal Stow action to put
their exact four-seed stack 2720 inside it.

F5 and fresh Continue at tick 4760 kept the bag and its contents in
the pack at 7 drams and 26 HP. Kesset's three oil and saved profile
remained. The purchased bag stayed absent from the shelf. The route
used the earned oil-repair save and ordinary trade, movement and
inventory controls.

The same trade screen offered a camp hearth as a current special for
123 drams. That is an observed stock source for a later field heat
trial. This player bought only the satchel.

The old runtime failed all nine required-stock cases in the focused
check. The corrected run passed 246 cases across provisioner and
merchant stock, saved item state and the related I–R fixture checks.
Independent review checked the identity and payment safeguards and
matched all eight archive hashes, the exact bag and seed custody, and
the recorded price against the native evidence.
