# Read the full compatible repair bill

Lining-clay actions now show the amount in a short row, with both accepted
grades and “in any mix” in the highlighted hint. The same bill appears in
F1 and Examine. Timber and other fixed-material repairs retain their
ordinary labels.

These were isolated continuations of previously earned root saves. The
rig trial was a new reader check. The mixed well repair used one bought
grade and one original measure. An older body of flavor text and the
Gleaners’ sign still point to fire clay by name. The live bill gives both
options. Fresh discovery copy has a separate scope to preserve earlier
saved sign text and the local cellar directions.
