# Ginmere’s rope anchors show the work and the way

Before rigging, Examine tells the player to check the other landing
by the ordinary stairs. Once both ends can take the rope, the action
shows its cost of two knotflax cord and one turn. A short pack or
unavailable landing gets a reason before selection. The two rigged
anchors give their own directions and explain that the landing needs
to be clear.

## Ordinary play

The earned seed 704860946 checkpoint started on Ginmere’s first floor
at 40 HP and 50 drams. The player took the original stairs down and
walked to the lower anchor at (23,18). On the original version the
lower reader still pointed to the lower terrace and the menu offered
to rig with no cord in the pack.

Ordinary stairs, atlas travel and a conversation took the traveler
to Orrit Coilstitch’s shop in Morrowfast. One purchase moved his
exact three-unit stack of knotflax cord (22711) to the backpack for
26 drams. Back at Ginmere, one rigging turn spent two from that stack.
The remaining cord stayed in the pack. The original stairs stayed
available, and ordinary stair input used the rope to reach (23,18)
on the other floor and return.

The revised reader was compared from the same three saves. The
unvisited-end and zero-cord choices explain their refusal before any
payment. Selecting either disabled row kept the same turn, money,
cord, position and stairs. The ready replay spent two cord once,
then the upper anchor said to descend and the lower said to climb.
Fresh Continue on the upper anchor kept the route and exact leftover
cord. A new down-and-up trip used both original landing coordinates.

## Native checks

The reader uses the existing cached world and reciprocal owners. It
checks both stair parts, tags and exact registered connections before
describing an installed route. An unavailable landing has a general
reason. Inspecting never opens another zone. The old generated
instruction is removed from the displayed text while the original
saved prose stays intact.

Six focused tests failed on the old source. Two initial candidate
assertions were adjusted to check the requirement’s amount and
material separately. The final run passed 181 cases with one explicit save-contract
generator skipped. It covered the exact cord payment,
ground and unusable items, incorrect reciprocal connections, both
directions, adjacent reader behavior and saved part contracts.

The real playtest ran in the Unity Editor on Apple M5. This slice
has no new standalone package or performance measurement.
