# Ordinary work before investing in local projects

November 25, 2026 UTC. Two separate Editor GameBootstrap forks used the
same earned Gleaners’ sign save at tick 30, seed 64, generation v26. Both
started with 40 HP, 50 drams, 50 Ink and the ordinary pack. Map travel,
movement, menus, waits and actions used native input. We followed known
coordinates and inspected live state to guide the route.

## Sodden’s public ripe bed

Direct travel reached Sodden at tick 130 and the dry approach at 250 with
40 HP. Ordinary waits brought the first shower at 600. The channel menu
offered one usable portion for the dry bed. Closing the menu left the
clock and allowance at their original values. Three steps west led to
the independent ripe plant. One **gather into pack** action produced
two sumpsieve pads and one seed at tick 640. The character kept both
starting tonics, all currency and full health. The dry crop still had
zero moisture and the treatment bench required repair. Fresh Continue
matched all selected fields, exact products and the unused shower portion.

The two branches establish ordinary harvest during an available shower
and an earlier optional use that helps a second crop.

## Route countercase and verification limits

A first fork took a detour through Sill. Its arrival event changed wildlife
in subsequent zones. After borrowing and studying the book, the player left
Quillhold at 2 HP. One starting tonic restored 16 HP at Sodden. Another
snake hit and bleeding ended the run at tick 620. The last autosave records
the 2 HP arrival, and the native log records the later death. This fork
shows a route hazard separate from the shower choice. The successful forks
both travelled directly from Gleaners’ without the Sill detour.

Independent review checked the original and forked save bodies, route
flags, currency, skill, exact items, crop state and native logs. The earlier
focused native test results remain linked from the related work. This pass
adds actual play evidence for existing actions. The two routes share one
seed. Unassisted discovery and wider generation comparisons remain open.
