# The still-grown sumpsieve pays for a field dressing

The traveler continued the rested seed-65 branch with the exact two
sumpsieve pads grown with portable still water. Ordinary world travel
and twelve dry steps from the Peat Works arrival reached the collapsed
drying frame. Harvest spent the frame and put four salvaged timber
in the pack.

At Sella's shelter, I used two timber to brace the existing bench.
The repair took one action and left two timber. With Sella visible
beside it, the repaired bench then offered to prepare a field dressing.
The service took one grown pad, the remaining knotflax cord and two
drams. The player kept one pad and the seed. Sella received the two
drams, and one exact field dressing entered the pack.

Fresh Continue at tick 6950 restored the braced bench, living Sella,
both purses, the packed still, remaining water and the dressing.
The spent Peat Works frame stayed absent. The traveler finished at
42 HP. The spare pad offers a separate portable treatment choice.
This outing verified preparing the dressing through the existing
service, with the finished dressing kept for later use.
