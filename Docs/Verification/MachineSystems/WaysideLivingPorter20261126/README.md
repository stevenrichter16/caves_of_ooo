# A search returns with news of the living porter

When a searcher on the wayside route sees the assigned porter alive at
close range, the sighting becomes part of the saved search. The searcher
still checks the loading point. News reaches the shipment shelf when the
cook returns or when the paid hand reports in person. It describes what
the searcher saw. An independently observed body takes precedence. The
parcel stays under the original delivery and settlement rules.

## Personal search

The real seed 64 version 18 outbound save already contained one controlled
Rooted delay on the porter. At tick 300 Orven stood at `(27,16)` and the
live porter `6893` was at `(25,17)`. One ordinary wait recorded the exact
sighting and moved Orven to `(26,16)`. The shelf still showed only the
overdue notice. The private tick 310 save survived a fresh Continue.

Ordinary waits let Orven reach the marker and walk back to the kitchen.
After he returned at tick 510, the same shelf said he had seen the porter
alive while checking the route. The case remained open. The porter still
held parcel `6896` with its original two measures. Orven still had 35
drams and his six opening stock entries. The player stayed at 40 HP
and 50 drams. Fresh Continue matched the returned report and custody.

## Paid search and late receipt

The separate seed 64 version 22 outbound save came with the two-dram wage
already paid and the same controlled delay on its original porter. The
hand saw porter `6893` at tick 330, checked the loading point and reported
at tick 470. The shelf described this report at tick 490. Orven stayed at
the kitchen with 33 drams and the hand still had the original two-dram wage.
The same porter still held the box and both measures.

Ordinary waits let the delay wear off and the original porter finish the
delivery. The real receipt closed the notice at tick 770. At tick 790 the
reader showed the arrival. The original box was on the shelf, its two clay
measures had entered Orven's stock as one extra lot, and the opening goods
and player purse remained. No extra search wage was paid. The report and
late receipt each matched their fresh Continue snapshots.

## Native checks

The compiling RED had two intended missing-report failures and 44 passing
controls. The final connected run passed 146 cases. The opt-in saved-part
contract generator was skipped. An intermediate expiry test advanced the
clock object from before reload. The final case uses the loaded clock and
asserts that the deadline actually passed. The suite covers retained
sightings, an unrelated or hidden porter, an expired employee report, a
separately seen body, an unseen death and the existing cargo settlement.

The native route used retained saves with a disclosed controlled delay.
No additional effects, money, actors, cargo or positions were supplied
in this run. The two guided scenes establish a local search behavior.
Additional settlements, player witness dialogue and current Mac testing
remain open.

Independent review verified the source, both personal reloads, the exact
reported paid encounter, 16 save bodies and the three screenshots. The
late receipt and its fresh Continue were checked separately in that same
private series. The scoped 86 foreign files and 861 normal save and
preference files matched their existing hashes. The full regression and
packaged performance remain open.
