# Morrowfast pays for ore the player already has

In an ordinary seed 2, world generation 31 save, the player left
Cinderhold with the spare choir-iron ore `17132` and the spike bought
from the earlier job. The world-map journey brought the player and
the exact ore to Orrit’s rope shop at Morrowfast.

At turn 3180, Orrit’s trade screen offered **six drams** for that ore.
His work request separately offered **24 drams and one fire clay**.
The player closed trade, accepted the request and delivered the
same ore without visiting or mining the suggested deposit at `(1,5)`.
Orrit’s stock acquired the exact ore. The purse rose from eight to
32 drams, and the carried fire clay stack rose from one to two.

Fresh Continue preserved the purse, spike, two clay, exact ore in the
shop and the completed receipt. Orrit’s normal conversation remained
available. His action menu offered no repeat delivery.

This one guided comparison measures the two real offers at one shop.
It adds ordinary Morrowfast verification to the earlier [iron reward
change](../RegionalIronRewards20261126/README.md). The same runtime
had already validated alternative ore sources, once-only settlement and
rollback in focused native tests.
