## One crop, two finishes

mire in flask `9873`. I set the same still down at `(61,14)` in Sodden,
two cells from steam vent `17799`. One action settled the mire into
three clean water and reported nine discarded measures. The source
pool was already at 48 after the earlier draw. It stayed at 48, the
carried torch kept its fuel and the separate flask kept 12 brine.

I packed the still and entered Sella’s shelter after the shower passed.
Only the clean-water flask was offered for the dry bed. The first
ordinary dose left two water and 39 wet rounds after the action turn.
The crop grew while I was away. The matched fork was tick 5430 on the
world map, with the original dry bed `18823` still in the saved zone.
