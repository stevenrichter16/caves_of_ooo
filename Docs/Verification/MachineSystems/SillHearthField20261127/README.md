# A bought camp hearth becomes portable field heat

The ordinary seed 65 traveler found a 14 lb camp hearth in Yevven
Reedfall’s physical stock at Sill. The quoted price was 102 drams. The
17-dram branch tried the offer, which kept the hearth, currency and
clock in place.

From the same starting Sill save, another branch still had 31 drams
from earlier work. The traveler gathered four fire clay at an uncovered
bank west of Morrowfast and dug one choir-iron ore near Cinderhold.
The dig cost Rot Choir standing. Cinderhold’s Drowned Level work took
all four clay and paid 50 drams after a wait and a report. Brinna
Lowbank then bought the exact ore through her work request for another
24 drams and one separate fire clay. The balance rose from 31 to 105.

Two dried rations and one dram bought one board of salvaged timber.
Opening a basket west of Morrowfast supplied one existing fire-moss.
The route back to Sill cost 12 HP. At tick 4110 the seller transferred
the exact hearth `9886` and took 102 drams. The player had two drams and
32 HP, and carried the empty hearth, timber and moss.

A ripe public row in Gleaners’ Glade yielded one emberwheat. The traveler
continued into nearby wild terrain and set down the paid hearth. The
relight action named salvaged timber and dry knotflax chaff. Trying the
unavailable action kept the cold hearth, pack contents and clock in place.

One timber added 10 fuel to the cold coals. One fire-moss lit them.
The action and the following turn left 9.75 fuel. The hearth cooked the
sheaf in one action at tick 4290, leaving one toasted emberwheat and
9.5 fuel. Taking up the hearth at tick 4300 extinguished it and kept
that fuel. A sari-snake hit the traveler for 6 HP during pickup.
Fresh Continue kept the exact hearth, 9.5 fuel, the toasted meal,
two drams and 26 HP.

This complements the existing free cooking fires and burning scenery.
Owning the kit lets the traveler choose where to cook.
Placement, fuel and ignition have their own costs and choices.
