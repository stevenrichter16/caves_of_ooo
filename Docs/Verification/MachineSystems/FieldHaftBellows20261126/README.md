# Rough field hafts at the forge

The Cinderhold forge can make the ordinary hand bellows with either one
rough field haft or one oak haft. Each bill also uses one iron spike and one
leather binding. Both produce the same tool for the same one-action cost.

## Ordinary play

The seed 64 branch carried original Sumphold timber `7472` and the
rough haft `7473` shaped from the other original timber unit. The player
walked through the intervening map and into Cinderhold, found weaponsmith
Ostrel, and paid 29 drams for one offcut and one spike. The carried dagger
cut that offcut into binding `12115`. The old forge offered the oak bill
as unavailable with these same supplies. The fresh Editor loaded the
revised catalogue on Continue and offered the rough haft bill.

The native inventory action at forge `11948` advanced tick 1905 to 1915.
It spent `7473`, `12115` and the bought spike
`b57a087b5c064e82aa1b1efac6786ac8`. Canonical hand bellows `12116` entered
the pack, and exact spare timber `7472` stayed. The purse remained at
21 drams and health at 40. The four-file checkpoint archived those earned
results before the air test.
