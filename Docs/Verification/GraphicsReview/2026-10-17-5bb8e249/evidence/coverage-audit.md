## Why the other four biomes look flatter despite native flags

### Direct observations

GRO-01, STU-01, BEA-01 and OVE-01 show simpler, flatter colored surfaces, less detailed actor silhouettes and a different light treatment from the Spread/Sodden images. Their metadata records nativeRing=true, voxel=true and fullReveal=false. These statements do not contradict one another. The flags establish the active presentation path, not that every entity has the same art family or mesh fidelity.

Their real time conditions also differ: GRO-01 is Dusk, STU-01 and OVE-01 are Dark, and BEA-01 is Dawn. Consequently the screenshots do not isolate biome art as the only variable. The bright region around the player in several frames is actual recorded lighting, not added annotation.

### Source-verified explanation

- `SpawnRing3DPresenter.cs:85` defines `VoxelPresentationActive` as visible presentation plus a non-null voxel bridge. It does not count models or promise every owner is modeled. The presenter separately exposes per-owner `IsAuthoredEntity` and `IsRenderedEntity` at697–704.
- `VoxelWorldPresentation.cs:300–320` expressly supports all six wilderness composition scopes, as well as eligible ordinary caves and authored sites. A blanket claim that Grovelands/Stump/Beating/Overwrit are still2D is incorrect.
- `SpreadCommonTerrain.cs:11–12` applies its richer common-terrain refinement only to `SpreadPresentationScope`. Grass, reeds, bushes, walls and several objects become the reference-glade families through lines30–40. This is a scoped style choice, not the universal recipe for all biomes.
- `SpawnRing3DPresenter.cs:158–214` loads the Spread style catalog and Sodden's separate native art plus shared actor palettes. At217–239 the default surface is built with exposure2.2; Sodden receives its1.35 exposure/lighting/probe/mottle profile, and the glade/Spread path receives1.45 and its own profile. Actor sizing at419–460 and actor paint at879–884 are also scoped through these loaded libraries. Gas/element volumes are created only for Spread or Sodden at238. Thus same global Full/100% settings do not imply identical material, lighting, actor scale or effects implementations.
- `SpreadBiomeHumanoidLibrary.cs:82–84` explicitly scopes humanoid refinement to Spread or Sodden. `SpawnRing3DRecipes.cs:224–230` likewise gates its authored animal forms. Other zones use the baseline catalog unless a more specific recipe applies.
- The baseline catalog `Assets/Art3D/SpawnRing/Definitions/catalog.json` contains real model bindings for Grass5311, Bush5132, Tree5634, VineWall5644, FruitingBody5274, Shambler5490 and Player5445. These are the principal types in GRO-01. Their simpler appearance is not evidence of missing native geometry.
- `SpawnRing3DRecipes.cs:488–512` maps real Overwrit owners to Overwrit voxel art. `OverwritVoxelLibrary.cs:13,117–118` provides the deliberately small ground/growth/waymarker/bench vocabulary. OVE-01 metadata contains2000 OverwritGround,31 OverwritNewGrowth,one stairs and the player; it contains no bench/waymarker. Its emptiness is real sampled content, not proof the renderer lost a forest.
- `SpawnRing3DRecipes.cs:524–546` maps Stump terrain through StumpVoxelLibrary. Its exact families include TepuiStone, TepuiWall, SprayPool and DescentLedge (`StumpVoxelLibrary.cs:123–132`), matching the dominant STU-01 owners. CascadeFather and GlasspaneFrog have baseline native catalog bindings at5149 and5284.
- `SpawnRing3DRecipes.cs:548–560` maps Beating scenery through its regional voxel library and explicitly changes Sand's visual family to pan in a SaltPan formation. The wide flat pale field in BEA-01 is consistent with that actual recipe. `BeatingVoxelLibrary.cs:13,127–129` lists the corresponding sand/pan/crust forms.
- Genuine individual fallback remains possible: recipe resolution returns `unmodeled-native-blueprint` when no binding exists (`SpawnRing3DRecipes.cs:631`). ReconcileOwner drops that owner's recipe when its ModelId is null (`SpawnRing3DPresenter.cs:356`). ZoneRenderer wires native-owner predicates to the sprite/animated paths at414/423 and1914–1927, so a native world can coexist with fallback owners. This is intentionally per-owner, not whole-biome fallback.
- Voxel conversion also preserves an unmapped original source mesh rather than fabricating geometry (`VoxelWorldPresentation.cs:333–343`). A non-null voxel bridge therefore does not establish all submitted meshes were replaced.

### Limits and inference

The sources explain why these regions can look like different generations of art while all use native3D. They do not prove which exact visible figure fell back, how many meshes were replaced, or whether a specific draw failed in the captured frame. The manual JSON lacks per-owner ModelId/rendered status and missing-mesh counters. Those counts require current runtime inspection while the exact zone is active. It would be inaccurate to label all small dark actors in BEA-01 glyph fallback from appearance alone.

The supported finding is **regional presentation inconsistency in these samples, with source-confirmed scoped art/lighting differences**. The unsupported stronger claims are **all four biomes are2D**, **all entities have voxel models**, or **all variation is caused by palette rather than actual time/light**. No fix is proposed or made as part of this evidence assignment.
