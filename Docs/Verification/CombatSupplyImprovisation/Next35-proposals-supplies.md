# Next item-utility passes — supplies, tools and equipment

2026-10-08. Brainstorm input, not a shipped-feature claim or a commitment to all twelve ideas. Sources were checked against `Docs/ITEM-UTILITY-CATALOG-2026-10-08.md` and the named implementation files. Excludes the already shipped grease/grit/veil and current cord/clay/water tranche. These are original CoO proposals, not claims about Qud behavior.

## Preferred compact additions

1. **A lit torch can touch off a nearby oily floor or combustible object.** The ordinary Torch is sold by the Morrowfast mender and in cave/camp/provisioner supply routes. Today it supplies light and qualifies as an ignition source for supported station actions, but the player cannot deliberately touch its flame to arbitrary supported nearby fuel. Add an explicit paid ignition action using `IgnitionSources` and existing `ZoneTileStateSystem.ApplyFireToTile`/`FireDose` contact, with a small real fuel cost. Keep one-tile reach and ordinary fire spread; reject an unlit, stowed or wet torch. Verify the selected ground actually has combustible material; do not grant a torch free ranged fire magic. This turns prior grease preparation into a choice without needing a fire spell.

2. **Gripfrond wraps give an actual secure grip.** Renewable Stump Gripfrond crops yield the currently obtainable handwear; today it is ordinary AV1 armor. Add a bounded resistance to weapon disarm while actually worn on a hand holding that weapon. The tradeoff is the handwear slot rather than free protection from a carried wrap. `Cudgel_Disarm` currently directly unequips the target and only emits `WeaponDisarmed` afterward: add a pre-disarm equipment defense hook, do not intercept the too-late notification. Test NPC and player symmetry, severed hands, unworn wraps and forged weapons. A visible failed disarm should explain the grip. Priority depends on verifying ordinary enemies actually perform disarms, otherwise player defensive value is illusory.

3. **Groundwire screen can discharge the wearer into the floor.** Its finite Sodden works source and current hand-slot ElectricResistance50 are real. Add a stationary paid action which removes/reduces an existing Electrified effect, leaves actual electrical energy on the current tile, and cannot be used stowed. Holding the screen occupies a weapon hand; the discharge may endanger adjacent actors on conductive ground. Use `ElectrifiedEffect` and `ZoneTileStateSystem.AddCharge`, with explicit conservation/capping and post-commit reactions. Never describe charge on ordinary dry ground as safe without checking current reactions. Strong tactical reason to carry the tool, but requires tests proving its electrical aftermath.

4. **Spurgrass spines become low, visible caltrops.** Beating crops already yield recoverable throwable spines. Consume a small number to scatter a single finite tile hazard. Prefer a brief accuracy/footing disadvantage through existing `HobbledEffect` over another hard root; Hobbled currently means -3DV, not slow movement. Allies/player also suffer, and sturdy footwear can give a explicitly defined counter. No infinite recovery/replacement cycle. Distinct from cord because it softens a choke point without stopping attacks/movement. Needs new floor model and clear exact-duration readout.

5. **Lampvein fan can disperse a little real gas.** Renewable Cave crop, already a portable luminous object. A deliberate adjacent fanning action should reduce finite gas density or shift one bounded portion into a legal neighboring tile, not erase an entire cloud. Spend an action, preserve gas identity/level, and make the neighboring risk visible. Reuse `GasPoolPart`/`GasFactory` after checking their actual unit/merge semantics. Do not move a stationary opaque veil automatically merely because its name says mist. The input is already obtainable; the gas-displacement mechanism is new. Prefer a modest density reduction if conservation becomes disproportionate work.

6. **Dagger or a proper cutting edge releases physical bindings on a companion.** Daggers are starting equipment, found in early loot and sold by menders. The scene is an ally held by a rope/plant snare while the player chooses to spend an action cutting them free. Current `RootedEffect` does not identify physical bindings versus ice magic, so this is gated on a source-specific binding effect/record: never silently let a knife dispel Frostbind. The action should require an actually available cutting tool, adjacency and a real physical restraint; it should not also damage the ally. Good interaction once restraint provenance exists, not an immediate generic cleanse.

## AI and environment additions requiring a wider design

7. **Extend meat diversion to a small authored set of ordinary carnivores.** Raw/DriedMeat already have world/merchant/corpse sources and throw handling. Current diversion belongs to eligible configured `SpreadPredatorPart` encounters; it is not a universal bait system. Introduce an explicit diet/temptation policy for two or three existing species, preserving direct combat threat priority and bounded sight-based interest. One stack unit is consumed per feeding, no healing/taming/free friendship. A hungry predator may take the detour; an enemy already being attacked should not repeatedly abandon combat. Use the existing predator behavior as evidence, not a broad keyword-based Beast exemption.

8. **Berries draw grazers away from a route or disputed crop.** WildBerries are harvested from current BerryBushes and sold in ordinary baskets/farmer stock. A small dedicated ground feeding target lets a player redirect a grazer before entering its area. `SpreadGrazerPart` currently targets a configured `RipeCropRow`, and threat flight takes precedence. Preserve that safety priority, bounded approach budget and one-unit consumption. This differs from predator meat by being a way to reorganize a crowded environment and protect cultivated food; it does not pacify hostile humanoids. Do not add dog-fetch work to this pass.

9. **Ink vial briefly disrupts a seeing creature's distant aim.** Ordinary scribes have two vials; shelves and ink production supply more. Close-range splashing trades valuable book/rental ink for a retreat opportunity. Requires a real short-duration observer-sight limit integrated with player FOV, shared AI and ranged aim, plus exact restore/cleanse behavior. No existing Blind effect or universal eye metadata was found. Melee contact and launched projectiles remain dangerous. This remains attractive but should not be squeezed in as a renamed Confused effect or a player-only enemy disable.

10. **Old world pipe is a lever for a specific physical obstacle.** FindWeaponT1 and RuinScavenger equipment sources already exist. First inspect jammed/locked world objects and choose one native obstacle whose existing kick/repair/open action does not already solve the same need. The pipe could pry an authored buckled shutter open for a turn/action/durability cost, yielding an escape route. Use actual object state, ownership and persistence; never announce a universal lockpick or grant arbitrary wall removal. This is a useful environment pass only if there is a concrete repeated obstacle population, otherwise defer as decorative scope.

11. **An equipped shield can prepare against a committed physical strike.** Bucklers and iron bucklers are ordinary obtainable gear, currently armor/DV pieces with no independent ShieldPart or block command in the audit. A stationary guard consuming the next physical hit and then ending offers an alternative to stepping aside during a telegraph. It must cost a turn/occupied hand and reduce or end on movement/attack; elemental environmental harm bypasses it. Reuse an existing guard effect if the current combat code already has an equivalent rather than stack a second defense system. Verify enemy/player usage and native telegraphs before selecting this pass.

12. **A long implement safely tests a known pressure trap from reach.** Spear and CurationSaltRake have concrete equipment sources; the rake is in the receiving hall tool cabinet. Most traps currently require an actual `EntityEnteredCell` actor; spoofing that event would incorrectly hit the probe's wielder. Design a separate explicit trigger-without-creature payload before adding a reach action, keeping fire/area outcomes dangerous and making simple traps spent. The cost is a paid action plus possible tool wear, with no guaranteed protection from a blast. Timber already permanently jams supported traps: this alternative is justified only if reusable testing at distance offers a materially different risk, otherwise prune.

## Selection rules

- Choose distinct verbs, not twelve interchangeable status bottles. Proposed priority: torch ignition, genuinely active equipment property, one gas/environment manipulation, then one narrowly authored bait expansion.
- Every selected pass needs an ordinary acquisition → actual inventory/equipment input → world consequence test, and the same setup with the item missing/invalid.
- Names alone do not establish abilities. Mineral names, gas names, creature names and flavor tools must receive explicit behavior/source contracts before the player help can promise them.
- Save/load, payment, normal input turn cost, physical contact, hostility and visible feedback are part of each pass, not additional counted improvements.
- Do not enable tinkering, redesign dog fetching or add a general hearing/stealth system merely to make this list longer.
