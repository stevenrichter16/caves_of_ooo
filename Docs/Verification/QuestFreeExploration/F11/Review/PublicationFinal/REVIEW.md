## Ordinary arrival finding

A normal fresh world-map descent uses the actual center or normal fallback resolver (`WorldMapTraversal.cs:162–189`), while the Play witness used a disclosed observing transfer. The original hunt geometry only established a safe border crossing. In a private fixed18-address, composer-on/off comparison, two new hunters immediately targeted Player and moved toward them on their first real TakeTurn. One was newly introduced by composition: seed1729/Overworld.14.8.0, hunter(36,20) versus player(40,12), actual LOS and distance8; the original Viper was at(39,24), distance12, and did not acquire Player. There were no first-turn melee hooks or HP losses in any of the36 observations. Private stable-hash maps are not native Unity maps.

The roadmap's literal wording was “Avoid initial attacks on the player at entry.” These observations do not falsify a narrow no-first-melee claim. Root expressly approved the stronger player-experience improvement: a newly composed hunt should remain available to observe on an ordinary default descent instead of immediately changing into a player chase. The private candidate requires the default center to be walkable and the initial hunter to be outside its authored sight8. It preserves existing hostility, player interference, bounded source geometry and literal saved layouts. It does not promise safety at arbitrary border entries, saved return cells, after time passes or from unrelated creatures.
