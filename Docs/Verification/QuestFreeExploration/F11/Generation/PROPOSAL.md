## Topology candidates

Prefer Fallow as the first formation if its actual source geometry passes. It currently has only WorkGang at current version7; introducing a second family there adds more difference than adding a third FieldStrips family or a fifth Hedgerow family. FieldStrips is the comparison candidate, not a fallback chosen after cherry-picking successful addresses.

For planning feasibility, freeze all currently placement-eligible Fallow and FieldStrips addresses for seeds1,64,1729 before generating any. Capture exact Trees immediately after SpreadComposition's successful build and observe source/geometry immediately after ordinary population at4001, before stock. Later source census will separately validate final geometry after the complete pipeline. Disable only existing exploration composition and population rewriting in this private source census, recording that boundary. This census estimates source/geometry availability; it is not the later prospective-v8 release denominator.

Two genuine variants are proposed, selected before source search by a stable `hunt-variant` salt:

- Covered flight: initial hunter/prey separation3–5, mutual real LOS, both at least4 cells inside the border; one actual opaque original Tree is responsible for a reachable loss of sight within a radius4/at-most4-step eight-direction route. No route step reduces separation below the initial safety minimum. Demonstrate the cover dependency by a virtual Tree-removed ray counter-check. Preserve actual protected approach/exit/stair connectivity and prove an ordinary boundary crossing outside the initial hunter's sight8. This preflight is only geometric opportunity; actual healthy escape under interleaved native turns is the mechanics acceptance gate.
- Open pursuit: a genuinely open local grazing lane with initial mutual LOS and a reachable at-least4-step continuation that remains visible, while an ordinary player crossing stays outside initial hunter sight. No claimed cover escape or forced winner. Real speeds, attacks and finite budgets decide catch, abandonment or player intervention.

Try existing cover in place first: it consumes no terrain budget and needs no relocation. If this is geometrically too scarce, separately propose relocating at most3 exact original Tree receipts into one short opaque island, with vacated-source and destination geometry both included. Never append new trees, convert hedges silently, seal a region, or lower admission limits after seeing the corpus without recording the change. User authorization allows meaningful terrain changes, but does not require them.

Limit candidate source packets to32 and geometry trials to256, deterministic order with eligibility applied before the source cap. Admission rejects protected/owned/quest/unique/damaged/liquid/hazard/interior sources, wrong factories/revisions, duplicate IDs and party/service creatures. No offscreen ticking, live reconstruction or repeat source scan on entry. Save the actual new actor aliases, role state and changed terrain graph through existing persistence.
