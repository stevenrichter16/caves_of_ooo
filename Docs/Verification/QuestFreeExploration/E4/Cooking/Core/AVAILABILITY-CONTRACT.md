## Activation, cold and refueling story

Player-facing idea: a ring of stones still holds cooking heat. While current finite heat>=150 and Fuel>0, the description names the actual inventory flow: carry raw meat or a starapple, open inventory, choose Cook. It does not say `C,direction Cook` because that real key opens the world action menu and CampfirePart only offers rest. No hidden recipe/stock guarantee.

When too cool, it remains a cold site and Cook refuses without consuming food; Examine and the approved cold model lose glowing coals/flame particles/crackle/hot light. Ordinary rest policy is unchanged. `FiniteCooking` defaultfalse preserves old fires in towns. Revisit/save does not reset the heat. Fuel exhaustion independently refuses even if heated.

There is no general AddFuel/Refuel/LightFire inventory verb in current source. TorchLightPart lights the torch **from** an already hot source (`Items/TorchLightPart.cs:186–198`); it is not a campfire relight command. KindleFlame rejects a Thermal FlameTemperature>=250 (`Skills/Pyromancy_KindleFlame.cs:52–54`), so it cannot currently warm ordinary Campfire threshold300. Stronger existing real spells apply heat and may reignite scenery, but source survival/fuel/fire consequences are ordinary mechanics, not a promised free starter reset. Do not add refuel/ignite controls, automatic reheating, general fuel processing or new particle fire plumbing merely for this opportunity. A cold or spent finite source is an honest persistent aftermath.
