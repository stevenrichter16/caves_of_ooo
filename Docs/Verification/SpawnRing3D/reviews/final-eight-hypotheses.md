## Sustained native profile: constraints and likely blockers

Source read of the adopted profile companion and its real base audit found no unbounded coroutine wait or deliberate suppression of native mechanics. The center-nearest lane search is finite; readiness is bounded at12 seconds, input-rate waits3 seconds, captures8 seconds, whole audit840 seconds plus the Editor900-second watchdog. All nested iterators are explicitly disposed on failure/abort/destruction. The profile finally disposes recorders and restores runtime low-detail; ordinary teardown restores preferences including absence, input settings/device and the private save root.

The eight-zone workload is **sixteen sustained5-second segments** (idle/walk), totaling80–90 measured seconds. Bind/settle/capture gaps are explicitly excluded. Each walking segment requires multiple successful real keyboard movements and native clock advancement; every attempt retains coordinates, HP, state and tick. Every idle segment must have unchanged position/tick. This is representative local redraw/turn work, not an uninterrupted cross-world route or combat stress.

Natural blockers are meaningful: an NPC can occupy the fixed lane, a hostile can hurt/kill the player, entry effects can slide/displace it, or a modal/status may prevent input. The run then records incomplete work and fails; no AI/HP/faction/RNG/terrain repair is performed. A dead or blocked run must be preserved and reported as incomplete, not used for a successful performance conclusion. Native movement performs the whole synchronous turn, so a legal but unusually expensive turn can also overshoot the90-second maximum and invalidate this intentionally bounded capture. That is a workload limit, not permission to truncate samples.

Cold-binding samples include normal fixture membership transfer, transition binder, autosave/readiness and its settling wait. They are separately reported and **not process-cold asset loading**; all zones and some resources are cached. The ordinary binder also refreshes NPC RNG natively. A shared world generation seed does not make all actor/trader/ambient/combat workloads equal across fresh full/low runs.

Required actual Unity timing/GC recorders must be present and measurable in every steady phase. Missing optional render/GPU/draw counters remain NA, never zero. Recorder samples are latest completed values and can lag their labelled frame/phase; boundary/first-frame outliers remain in the raw evidence and must not be quietly removed. GC.CollectionCount deltas are separate from per-frame allocated bytes. P95/p99/max must be recomputed from the gzip frame file, and raw hash/run/root/teardown checked.

The200000-frame cap fails on overflow instead of dropping history. Screenshot and summary allocations are outside measured steady phases; recorder/observer cost remains included. The full screen is1920×1080, while low detail intentionally reduces only the3D render target and shadows. Whole-zone source triangle totals are not viewport draw counts; fog masking is not mesh LOD. No source read can establish stable60fps, production-build budgets, full-vs-low causality or a speedup. Those require the actual completed receipt and appropriately bounded analysis.

One operator constraint: supply `-spawnRing3dProfile full` or `low` with a value. A bare trailing flag is ignored by the current paired-argument loop and therefore cannot count as a requested profile run. The intended launcher invocation is explicit; this is recorded rather than expanding the production change for malformed manual CLI input.
