# Blind local-dialogue review input

These are drafts for local residents. Attribute each set to a speaking culture,
describe its register, and flag any line that violates that culture, invents a
service or fact, resolves a protected mystery, or sounds like design-document
prose. Place/contact placeholders will be filled from live game state. The water
alternatives correspond to stable, temporary and fouled actual site states.
Do not infer approval from this file.

## Set A

- There is shade here. Rest. This is {settlement}; your business can wait.
- When you have rested, {contact} is here to speak with. You need not hurry.
- The well has been repaired. That is what I can tell you about it.
- The well is clear for now. I will not promise you more than that.
- No, not water from that well. It needs tending first.

## Set B

- Local contact: {contact}. Inquiry may be taken there.
- Well condition: repaired. Status: holding.
- Well condition: cleared. Duration: provisional.
- Well condition: fouled. Drinking: not advised.

## Set E

## Set F

- {settlement}. Ask the rate before you agree to anything. It saves both sides a quarrel.
- Ask {contact}. What they can offer, and on what terms, is theirs to say.
- The well repair is holding. Better to keep it that way than pay for another.
- The well is clear for now. A temporary clearing is what was done; don't price it as a lasting repair.
- The well needs work. Paying for a cup won't make that water fit to drink.

## Set G

- I live in {settlement}. That much I can attest. What account do you need?
- {contact} is here. Ask them for their own account; I won't put words in it.
- The well repair is entered as holding. That is the latest local account.
- The well was cleared for now. That entry does not promise it will hold.
- The current well record says fouled. I would not draw drinking water from it.
