The seven sets are distinguishable as groups, especially B/D/F/G. The safety lines intentionally converge toward plain language; that is preferable to making a warning obscure just to intensify faction style. A and E are the least confidently attributable from this sample. The contact placeholders must resolve to an actually present person, and the light remark must match a place with light; the input says state substitution is live, but this blind pass did not inspect that implementation.

No draft line resolves a Mystery Ledger question, gives Urqu intent, ranks an ending or claims a new magical power. I see no definite new service promise: the imperatives to rest/speak are ordinary invitations. The two fallback lines appropriately acknowledge missing information. This is a prose review, not native verification of condition gates or substitutions.
