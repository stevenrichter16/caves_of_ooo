## Clipping and clarity

One visible existing UI defect remains: the first hotbar action name, **En Garde**, is clipped at its cell's right boundary in the world screenshots. It is already visible in14 and persists in22, so the service transition did not introduce it. Treat this as a named hotbar label-width/layout issue, not as acceptance of a fully polished HUD.

The inventory description deliberately ends with an ellipsis and keeps **F1 Details** visible; this is announced inline preview truncation, not evidence of an inaccessible or silently clipped full reader. The pan and level-up readers' bottom control lines remain visible in the supplied captures. No service portrait overlaps the recipe or state text.

## Scope and next claim boundaries

The material and money conservation, actual pan/parcel owner identities, empty trays and retained uneaten meal are supported by the separate raw-save audit. The images provide the visible counterpart. The before/after art remains stable apart from the intended working-cover and ready-parcel cues. Orven's later wallet restock is not an art change. The collection level-up confounds the planned healing demonstration, and the meal remains uneaten. No additional sprite, mechanics change or broad coverage claim is justified by this review.
