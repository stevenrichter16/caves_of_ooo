Status after all five formation views: **sufficient bounded material integration checkpoint; full collision-affordance polish, area fullness and regional coverage acceptance remain open.** Reviewed the actual 71-frame RimForest and SummitScrub captures against their matching palette-v2 captures at the normal 1280×800 gameplay scale. This is a read-only image review by the source author, not an independent authorship review or a new gameplay execution.

## What changed visibly

- The thin outer rim is present around the displayed StoneDome masses. The upper/left edge is lighter and the lower/right edge darker. It makes the connected silhouette easier to follow, especially the stepped perimeter of the large upper-right SummitScrub mass.
- Interior tiles do not gain a grid of new borders. Walkable field, existing trees, leaf-cup tanks, actors and interface retain their prior identities and positions, subject to the different actual play moments. No new props or density were fabricated by this art correction.
- The darker stone body remains visibly distinct from the dusty ground. Pixel inspection of the unchanged viewpoint confirms that this is more than a screenshot-label change: the RimForest lower mass has 428 changed pixels in the bounded `(474,516)–(562,596)` image region. Representative body RGB is `(123–124,110,107)`, new upper rim `(136–137,121–122,116)`, lower rim `(113–114,101,100–101)`, and surrounding floor `(155–156,132,122)`. These are screenshot samples under the existing grade, not new source palette values or accessibility measurements.

## Remaining visual findings

**Collision affordance is still borderline, not accepted without qualification.** At ordinary size the rims are restrained. The extensive repeated horizontal grain and flat rectilinear body can still read as a dark patterned floor/paved pad rather than unmistakably blocking stone. The actual silhouette is now traceable, so this does not reproduce the original nearly indistinguishable floor/body defect. However, improved separation alone is not proof that a new player will infer collision. Root's real bump/action checks must remain separately reported; a blocked movement result proves mechanics, not visual intuition. Do not label this reference-quality final polish from these two pictures.

**SummitScrub has a real fullness limitation.** Its broad central visible ground area remains sparsely furnished, with a few trees/tanks and large stone clusters at its edges. That may be appropriate to scrub openness, but the screenshot does not establish the user's whole-chunk fullness gate. Repeated small marks or unrelated props would not automatically fix it. The dark viewport margins are not counted as bare terrain.

**RimForest already has coherent sparse clusters.** Tree/bush/tank groups and gaps form recognizable ribbons and open lanes. The correction improves the existing stone boundaries; it does not demonstrate that every viewport or the complete chunk is sufficiently composed. Remaining glyph actors/objects and black-backed hazard strips still interrupt the otherwise native field. Those are actual uncovered presentation gaps, not evidence that more decorative terrain must be invented.
