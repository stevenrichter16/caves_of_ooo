## Material decision

Reuse **only opening-ground frames 0–15**, unchanged, as the proposed water background. These are all sixteen cardinal **disclosed dry-edge masks**, with N1/E2/S4/W8 bits. The full atlas remains 336×24 with 21 native 16×24 cells; road 16–17 and soil 18–20 are outside this decision. All sixteen water cells are fully opaque. Frame 0 is a single quiet teal `#0C4344`; other frames add only muted `#184B45` and `#294935` inset bank pixels. They share the surrounding material language and leave the actual banks, actors and foreground plants readable without a second repeated wave pattern.

The water source PNG and current Resources copy are byte-identical, SHA-256 `b35f9d6a4b7051eb6971a00195a069f58d08a7d89e3060533745ee899a66dede`. Decode-only inspection confirmed the dimensions, full water-frame opacity, one-color interior frame and exact water palette: #0C4344, #184B45, #294935. These are source observations, not a render test. The existing 14-cell Grovelands material atlas and 22 borrowed forest cells remain unchanged.

**No new art or new imagegen pass is necessary for this reuse.** The existing actual-capture concept already covers the Fen interpretation, and no defect in these approved water pixels has been identified. A later actual matched view may justify a new derivative only if it exposes a named seam, contrast or material-readability defect. That would be a separate decision; do not pre-emptively recolor or embellish the approved atlas.
