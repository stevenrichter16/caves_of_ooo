## Verdict

The existing material kit now visibly carries across all six ordinary formation examples. Reed stands, peat faces, mire surfaces and the constructed board route give the unchanged layouts much more legible structure. The dry ground and these shared materials form a consistent palette across these views. The player and dry passages remain readable.

This is **a useful shared-material coverage extension, not whole-biome completion or generation-density acceptance**. The Copse water still makes its view look only partly converted; people, wildlife, loot, several hazards and small landmarks remain glyphs. Six centered frames cannot establish all sides of six chunks, their complete populations, neighboring seeds, authored sites, lairs, underground areas, or the broader all-biome queue. No new decorative generation is justified solely by these images.

## Smallest next static priorities grounded in these views

1. **Plain shallow water over real ground in Drowned Copse.** Treat it as actual `water` tile state, not MirePool/bog-mire or BrinePool. Preserve tree/reed ownership, water/mixture/heat/cold/charge/residue/mist semantics, wet-to-dry behavior and disclosure. A native base/overlay must not hide danger or silently paint dry Grass under water. Current unchanged blue waves are truthful fallback and an acknowledged high-priority graphical gap. This is material production, not a need for extra lakes or scenery.
2. **Shared world occupants and small props, in bounded families.** Receipts repeatedly expose Reedfrog/MawToad, Greatdew and portable containers (Crate/Sack/WovenBasket); Open Mire also has a real HollowLog. These have more cross-area impact than adding idle animation or more decorative ground pixels. Maintain exact owned identities, custody and state rather than blanket glyph-name substitution. Later additions include GinFrog/Bandfrog, DryBrush and the merchant reader/body.
3. **Existing architecture and travel/hazard landmarks.** Native VineWall exists in the earlier Sill study, but these actual Copse/Open Mire hash-mark enclosures need their own current-owner/state/neighbor verification before safe reuse. Reed Maze's real stair and vent must become recognizable without treating every ordinary stair as a lair or masking an active hazard. Peat Cuts' pipe/peat-bog feature and the remaining acid/brine pools need distinct material identities, not mire recolors.
4. **Fullness refinement only after missing visible owners read correctly.** Revisit Bog Face's upper dry field, Peat Cuts' center and Open Mire's long shore using actual wider traversal/interaction captures. The present frames identify review locations, not approved placement edits. Causeway's lattice repetition and dry-ground stipple are material/composition observations separate from object density. Protected approaches and clear central routing remain gameplay requirements.
5. **Keep worldwide breadth moving.** These tasks are a queue of reusable families, not permission to finish every Sodden detail before the next biome. Retain the all-biome/area ledger, then move the same playable-capture/native-production/reintegration loop to the next large uncovered regional kit. Nuanced animations remain deferred.
