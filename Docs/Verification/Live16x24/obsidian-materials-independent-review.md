## Remaining visual debt and acceptance limits

The repeated dark triangular wall recess/highlight motif is conspicuous on long runs and can read as a repeated chevron inset rather than varied natural glass fracture. Warm Cave grading reduces the source's purple identity; dim floor detail and olive borrowed Rubble produce uneven material cohesion. Floor is substantially more legible than baseline, but broad visible chambers still rely on sparse repeated rubble and a few existing objects. Glyph/mixed-content islands remain. Fog/memory/undisclosed black cells are not counted as bare generated space. These are explicit later composition/palette/repetition debts, not grounds for claiming these chunks polished.

This checkpoint accepts only the observed native material/body/reader continuity and bounded mechanical truth. One column/two depths cannot establish every11+ area, complete mixed-owner coverage, full80×25 composition, physical performance or fresh package delivery.
