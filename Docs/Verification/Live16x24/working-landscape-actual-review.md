The kitchen background is substantially less dominant. Its remaining hedge masses have different lengths and shapes, the wooded lower-left area remains a useful counterweight, and broad ordinary grass connects the visible spaces. The native player/props retain their established scale. The east zone’s flower placement now supports distinct places rather than acting as a repeating horizontal texture. The actual game keeps the real HUD and source art, avoiding the concepts’ extra trees, altered text and softened presentation.

### Remaining scene limitation, not a new blocker in this slice

The kitchen is **easier to find, but still a small service cluster on largely bare ground**. Its two existing masonry pieces provide a modest working edge; the distant hedge fragments do not yet create the deliberately framed inhabited court described in the broader design. The forge likewise remains a small object group in a large open area. The current pass should therefore be reported as reduced repetition and improved hierarchy, not as completion of all working-court composition. A later framing decision must use real cold-generated boundaries/owners and preserve service access; arbitrary props, brighter Cook art or a fake road would not solve it honestly.

No source-art revision is justified by these images. Do not reopen the accepted sprites. The size of the remaining quiet ground is not by itself a defect; further edits must demonstrate an actual improvement in place/approach recognition, not merely fill space.

### What is still visually weak

1. **The service setting remains sparse and only loosely framed.** The objects are now easier to see, but they still read as a compact exposed arrangement on broad uniform grass rather than a fully composed inhabited court. The two shelter stones are a modest backdrop. This is consistent across seeds and does not disappear with zoom. The valid next refinement is a purposeful relationship among actual working edge, open approach and existing nearby boundary/vegetation—not additional unrelated props or a fake painted road.
2. **The hedge’s per-cell texture remains conspicuous at2×.** Unequal geometry fixes the repeated-room problem, but long surviving runs still show an obvious repeated bright green mosaic. This is an honest remaining material/rhythm limitation. It does not justify repainting the approved character or service sprites; a later connected-edge treatment should be tied to a specific whole-scene improvement and actual visible neighbors.
3. **Complete opening coverage and cohesion remain unproven.** The supplied views still include peripheral glyph-like marks/liquid cues and largely blank expanses. These images do not establish every owner’s intended native treatment. The six-stop route, changed/failed service states, motion and packaged delivery require their own evidence. Do not call the new layout release-ready because its focused mask/tests and these views pass.
