## Significant remaining gaps

**Incomplete graphics, visible now.** In Green Rim 02, the black-backed
crop/stair cells look unfinished against the newly coherent pale plane.
The retained pre-change census identifies the corresponding existing owners
in ordinary 0,9 as `StairsDown` 56,7 and `HollowchimeCrop` 62,7 /60,5.
The two crop cells in bench04/05 correspond to `MargincressCrop` 72,2 /72,4
in ordinary 4,12. These identities/coordinates come from the retained census
and matched baseline context; this review did not query the after live graph.
Cultivated source floors have actual soil/crop meaning, so broadening the
plain-ground adapter to paint over that meaning is not a suitable visual fix.
The crop, its essential stages and its truthful soil presentation need their
own coverage, alongside the existing stair identity.

**Whole-view composition is not closed.** The Blank's sparse appearance is
more intentional than the old uniform glyph grid, and the approached
Pilgrimage rim visibly supplies a meaningful bench/empty-field relationship.
However, the centered Pilgrimage 03 screenshot provides almost no visual
reason to head toward that eastern activity. The recorded gameplay viewport
shows 59 of 80 columns. It is a reviewer inference that a source-grounded rim
cue or stronger approach composition could help; no navigation difficulty was
measured here. Adding noisy floor decoration, arbitrary clutter or a phantom
waymarker would not resolve this evidence gap honestly. Do not label overall
Overwrit fullness complete from the material pass.

**Other exact limits.** `BinderrootCrop` 72,11 /73,15 and the second bench 75,5
in ordinary 3,14 were retained in the baseline census but are outside these
after views. The Waymarker still has no naturally observed capture; native
source/fixture coverage cannot replace that. These views contain no evidence
for other depths, special sites, hostile encounters or all 22 eligible
ordinary addresses.

The source-contact caveats were checked at actual scale: the bench can read
like a chair in side profile, but the real reader and inward orientation make
its identity clear enough for this bounded pass. The shoot bases are visibly
linked, but the actual low growth and same-height reader do not warrant
another asset-only revision. These small silhouette concerns should not
displace the larger missing crop/stair coverage and unresolved composition.
