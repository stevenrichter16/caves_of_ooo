# East / kitchen independent source and visual review

2026-10-24. Read-only review of native contact, README/manifest, approved Cook, opening-anchor contact, v1/v2 concepts, and actual baseline kitchen-layout / forge-approach captures. No Unity calls or source edits. Native integration was pending when reviewed.

## Result

**No blocking source-art defect found. Approve this packet for bounded in-game integration, not release acceptance.** The native result follows the accepted 3–6-color flat cluster language substantially better than either concept. It preserves single-cell ownership and avoids the concept's invented anvil table, permanent flame, extra contents and replacement Cook.

- Forge and oven remain distinguishable: tall squared sage hearth versus shorter pale rounded/clay mouth. Both are quiet enough beside the approved character art.
- Pan's unchanged outline with dark crack / pale seal is an appropriate integrity-only change. Neutral trays do not claim that ingredients or completed output exist.
- Input tray and handled pickup board have different outlines and internal structures; at enlarged source scale they are clearly separate identities.
- Planted, growing and ripe claspbean have markedly different heights; only ripe has hanging pale pods. Seed, pulp and tied meal are sufficiently distinct for their small footprint.
- Sparse mauve flowers should reduce the very conspicuous repeated magenta marks visible in the forge baseline. This is a source-level expectation, not a claim about the integrated scene.
- Cook source remains the approved compact rust/cream figure; no new rendering style or detail was imposed on it. Colors and shape density across the new kit fit the opening anchors.

## Required observations on actual integrated captures

These are acceptance checks, **not confirmed defects** in the source packet:

1. **Pan repair cue at ordinary zoom.** The dark crack is only a small cluster and has less contrast than the pale repaired seam. Confirm both states read on actual ground, without selection tint hiding the difference. If they do not, adjust only that local cue; do not add a flame/meal/ready icon.
2. **Bed identity beside Cook and chair.** The narrow vertical cot is correct for one-cell ownership, but its repeated pale weave can resemble a rack or grille at minimum scale. Check the installed arrangement before changing it. A pillow/end-rail adjustment is an optional fallback only if the ordinary scene genuinely confuses it with the vent.
3. **Small growth mark and repeated flowers.** Verify stage0 survives existing ground/fog contrast, and many flowers remain subordinate to NPCs and stations. The contact's solid green background cannot prove that.
4. **Neutral trays versus service feedback.** The packet intentionally has no working/ready/contents variants. Retain truthful examine/menu feedback and do not claim a visually complete batch-state loop. Future state artwork requires actual custody and transfer repaint evidence, as the source sweep already specifies.
5. **Forge identity from approach.** Its muted frame may resemble masonry when unseen up close. Verify selection/examine exposes the real forge and that no false neighboring work surface is painted. Its shape is defensible now; avoid widening it into another collision cell merely to match the concept.

## Deferred preferences, not fixes

A longer pan handle, richer masonry texture, extra forge tools, larger objects, permanent orange coals and a wide horizontal bed are not improvements justified by this packet. They would respectively misrepresent the clay owner, over-detail the approved style, invent objects/state, or violate the one-cell footprint. Do not churn source art for those preferences.

The 1280×800 baseline shows a glyph-heavy but functional region; the strongest required next evidence is the same real kitchen / forge approaches after integration, including focus and one real repair/growth transition. A contact sheet or synthetic owner lookup cannot substitute for those captures.
