## Intended play

Give the existing northern field alembic a recognizable, occupied drying yard. One finite mendleaf plant grows on its exposed western margin. Two alchemy shelves and broken stone windbreaks form drying bays farther inside. A marlback patchbearer occupies the rear working space and carries its existing single healing tonic.

A player can gather the near herb and leave with useful material. One sprig makes a modest mending tonic through the existing field-brewing command. A player who presses into the yard meets a visible supply worth preserving: a badly wounded patchbearer will spend its own tonic instead of attacking or moving that turn. Control or a sufficiently decisive attack can leave that stronger tonic to recover after death. Letting the enemy use it, surviving, and withdrawing is a valid partial outcome; the bottle stays spent. Killing it after use does not generate a replacement medicine reward.

The shelves and stone windbreaks block movement, physical interception paths and sight, shaping the approach with actual opaque cover. The shelves begin as empty working furniture, not two more medicine containers. The existing still, stoneburr and frost lichen remain functional. No quest acceptance, reward flag, artificial injury, extra tonic cache, new healing service or fixed combat result is required.
