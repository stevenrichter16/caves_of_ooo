### Intentions and opportunities independent of purchases

1. **Patient thrower:** preserve a scarce projectile against armor; immediate attack/retreat wins against fast or fragile threats.
2. **Heavy displacer:** move a damaged survivor off a lane/into a real destination; ordinary throw or pull wins when pushing helps the enemy, armor prevents damage, or the destination is blocked.
3. **Field carrier:** secure the actual item while advancing/extracting; ground rethrow, dog or abandonment wins when custody is unnecessary or entry unsafe.
4. **Precise carrier:** spare a nearby ally/hostile from the wrong dose and put finite film in a choke; broad splash wins clustered threats/group healing, direct use wins urgent danger, keeping/drinking a tonic wins bad lines.
5. **Hybrid:** mix one or two throw options with actual evasive/control/companion options; pay for roots and give up reliability or other actions honestly.

Name-stripped verbs are retry, prepare, push after damage, step-and-secure, and narrow initial area. None duplicates another merely by changing element. Reliability can justify itself statistically without another command. World opportunities are lanes, armor, scarcity, custody pressure, allies, real terrain and enemy responses; none is spawned because a skill was purchased.

## 5. Complete payload policy and budgets

This is a **new shared throwing policy**, usable at0SP. It reuses TonicEffectFactory/ApplyTo, existing LiquidPool/contact/coating, tile reactions and gas; it does not create a second damage or AI framework. A recipe must classify explicitly as direct-only, film, energy, or existing gas. Do not infer physical volume solely from a display name. Deterministic content validation enumerates all authored tonic owners plus every emitted BrewRule effect/form and rejects unsupported new residue declarations.

### A3-D08 finite film contract

- Author opt-in finite film metadata queried by existing LiquidPoolPart. Natural AcidPool/OilSlick and ordinary vessel PouredLiquidPool retain existing behavior unless independently changed. Film has liquid ID, remaining integer exposure units, remaining terrain updates, source actor ID, throw serial and footprint cell, all primitive saved state. Its volume is exposure, **not recoverable drams**; Examine says “thin film; too little to collect.” Both LiquidVessel and Waterskin/other draw paths must reject sampling it; a fill→pour route must not launder it into a permanent pool.
- Standard AcidTonic and WaterTonic each supply9 total exposure units. The base budget for any qualifying equivalent status tonic/brew is `max(1,min(9,floor(9*min(1,effectiveMagnitude))))`, where magnitude≤0 uses factory default1. Thus ScarletSundewDew magnitude.35 gives3, not a full flask; potency>1 never creates extra physical matter. An explicitly authored override is required for another physical composition.
- Broad distributes one unit to each of its9 logical cells center-first, then clockwise N,NE,E,SE,S,SW,W,NW, repeating that order only if a declared future budget exceeds9.
