## 2. Design rules

- A site must offer a legible choice: risk an approach, wait and observe, collect something finite, trade an earned find, take another route or leave. A scripted quest is unnecessary.
- Compose existing producers' actual owners. Never compensate for a refused site by minting food, stock, equipment, animals or a traveller.
- Preserve consequences in the accepted graph: spent plants, empty sources, carried/deposited items, dead creatures and altered local relationships. Loaded old manifests keep their own version; new variants do not rewrite visited worlds.
- Keep quiet spaces and protected authored locations. Population and value budgets are separate from family selection; a selected family is not a claim that its required source existed.
- Let real control and faction rules win. Calm, party membership, current combat and legitimate hostility must not be overridden to enact a scene.
- Readability belongs to the implementation. Models, equipment, poses, warning text and changed resource forms must correspond to current owners and actual actions.
