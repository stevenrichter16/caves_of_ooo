# Planning prompt: deepen exploration, then expand its regional identity

Use `QUEST-FREE-EXPANSION-ASSESSMENT.md`, the current execution ledger in `QUEST-FREE-EXPLORATION-PLAN.md`, the actual repository, and the latest native evidence to plan the next expansion. The user wants useful, varied exploration without quests and has explicitly authorized creating and polishing the models and animations it needs.

Compare two approaches: deeper interactions among territorial actors, grazers, gathering, salvage and finite resources; and region-specific uses of those primitives elsewhere. Choose changes that give the player different approach/action/aftermath decisions. Do not treat more spawn entries, recolors, decorative objects or higher enemy counts as diversity by themselves.

Read the current implementations and their producer/consumer paths before assuming a mechanic works. Separate implemented, privately tested, imported, observed in live play and still proposed. Preserve failed observations. In particular, investigate the actual unconfigured grazers in the bounded normal-game sample and distinguish the new-zone scheduler's initial energy from a broken warning action. Do not weaken gameplay predicates or manufacture owners to satisfy a demonstration.

Produce one practical implementation plan with ordered, independently testable slices. For each slice specify the player choice, actual source and stock budget, existing behavior versus required work, saved aftermath, art/animation needs, failure and refusal behavior, meaningful tests and native observation. Identify exact interfaces and owned files where known; list remaining source audits instead of inventing APIs. Keep the existing twelve-family plan as the parent roadmap, and map new slices to it rather than silently replacing it.

Prioritize completing meaningful Spread interactions before spreading half-working systems across the map. A first regional pilot must use its own resource, geography and inhabitants; compare Beating scarcity/salvage and Sodden access before choosing. Keep the Spread acceptance gate explicit. Human enjoyment cannot be certified by automation, but an unavailable human observation must not become an endless harness project or prevent independent code/content work.

Preserve the approved 3D style, original creatures, existing save graphs, legitimate stock, protected sites, mandatory routes and player agency. Keep BitLocker dev-only, GlassScorpion neutral, plain Shambler spore-free, Gin Frogs silent, and the shipped Urqu rule. No permadeath/metaprogression, universal theft law, offscreen ecosystem simulation or combustion rewrite.

Follow `CLAUDE.md`: preimplementation sweep, actual failing-first evidence, paired controls, adversarial ownership/save/callback checks, Q1–Q4 review and the living ledger. Set finite investigation limits for low-impact issues; corruption, duplication, lost ownership, inaccessible mandatory routes and misleading combat danger remain blockers. Models and animations must be judged in the approved gameplay camera, during their actual actions, not only in an asset gallery.

End the plan with the first concrete implementation slice, the dependent parallel work, acceptance gates and honest deferrals. Then create an execution prompt from that plan and act on it. This prompt is an instruction to plan, not evidence of completed gameplay.
