## Bounded contracts

Hand bellows: reusable carried singleton, sound/unfrozen. One action moves exactly
one existing transient Gas owner of density1–40 from an adjacent cardinal cell
one further cell away from the actor. Both cells visible/explored and unobstructed
under shared movement rules. Stable/anchored, multi-cell, carried, equipped or
stationary clouds refuse. Same entity, ID, Creator, density, potency and Parts
remain; no split, spawn, merge or deletion. Normal gas turns can move it back,
decay it and expose anyone standing in its new area. This is temporary work-area
control, not permanent ventilation. Fan thinning, waiting, protection and detours
remain useful. Construction: one iron spike plus one oak haft and one leather
binding at an ordinary forge; consumed inputs, one reusable output, one action.
The manifest is not a separate capability.

Trail marks: spend one actual SilverSand unit on the actor's visible/explored dry
bare floor. Choose north/east/south/west, exit, water or danger. One nonsolid marker
per cell, no light, map revelation, remote knowledge, automatic pathfinding or
truth claim. The mark persists until brushed away or removed normally; brushing
it away takes one action and returns no sand. Ordinary landmarks and memory stay
valid alternatives. Material/payment and created marker join the transaction.

Door spike: fit one carried plain singleton IronSpikeComponent to an adjacent
closed, unlocked ordinary door with empty/own OwnerId. No scene-owned Morrowfast,
sealed archive or foreign-owned door. Lodge the exact spike on the doorway with
ordinary pickup disabled and a saved Part pointing to that door; opening and
opening-AI consult the same local obstruction rule. Bracing blocks both sides;
it adds no HP or universal key. Removing the brace returns the same spike to the
pack (refuse if no room), including after the door is destroyed. Destruction does
not create another spike or delete the one on the ground. No hidden ownership
transfer, source inventory copy or reconstructed refund. Existing locks and detours remain alternatives. Removal/destruction preserves the
spike where an existing owner supports it; the ordinary VillageDoor currently has
no DestructiblePart, and this batch does not add one or claim it can be broken.

Every selection binds actor origin, tool/input identity, state and exact targets.
Readers are pure; repeated current gathers retain a still-offered native choice.
Claims and commit revalidation protect callbacks and independent changes. Gas
movement publishes after committed payment; no claim of rewinding irreversible
world effects. Supply mutations and mark/spike transfers need scoped undo, not
