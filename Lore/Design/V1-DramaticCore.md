# The V1 Dramatic Core

> **Authority:** Canonical design document (Phase 11, M7). Commits the
> two climax redesigns (enactment, not retrieval — flaw F10) and the
> v1 scope triage. Level-design and writing build from this.

---

## I. The two climaxes, redesigned

The corpus's two most affecting committed beats — Selen's name and
the Naro question — were both structured as *exposition retrieval*:
find the document/informant, receive the truth, deliver it. Both are
now **enactments**: the player does something irreversible with what
they carry, and the doing is the beat.

### 1. The name spoken in the Deepest Cathedral

**What the player carries:** Selen's name — learnable from exactly one
source, Tovreth, and only after settling his one perfect debt (the
existing arc; unchanged). Learning it is the *setup*, not the beat.

**The beat.** The Deepest Cathedral, the Wedded's original body in
the central chamber. A recovered true name, spoken aloud with intent,
is a working of the practice-path kind (Phase 9); speaking *this*
name, *here*, is the largest such working a mortal has ever attempted.
The scene offers three castings, mutually exclusive:

- **Give it to her, asking nothing.** The substrate stills. The face
  the mycelium sometimes makes of her forms, and holds, and — for the
  first time in centuries — *matches* something: a woman hearing her
  own name from a friend's ledger, one thousand years late. This is
  the strongest opening of her Renewal-turn arc, and it forecloses
  using the name as leverage forever. (The gift cannot be un-given.
  Design note: this is the closure-ledger's logic at climax scale —
  a spoken gift is a closed act.)
- **Speak it to turn her.** Name-as-instrument: the utterance binds
  attention; she must attend wholly to the speaker while the name
  hangs in the air. The player gets the one negotiation nobody in
  history has gotten — bargaining with the whole Choir while it
  remembers being a person. Powerful, colder; her turn (if won this
  way) is a *treaty*, not a healing, and the Consume-vs-Renewal
  stakes stay live under it.
- **Withhold it.** Stand in the chamber, carrying the name, and say
  nothing. The Choir tastes that the player is holding something —
  *"you have brought us a thing you are not giving. We are patient
  about the difference."* The name remains leverage; the scene can be
  returned to; but the substrate's warmth toward the player drops a
  register, permanently. Withholding is not neutral. It is a chosen
  incompletion, and the world's physics notices.

**What may never happen:** the name delivered by proxy, written down,
or triggered as a fetch-quest turn-in. If the player never comes in
person, the beat never fires. Tovreth will not repeat it twice; the
one keeper, the one telling — *"the rate on that word, friend, is
that I only say it once."*

### 2. The staking (the Naro commitment)

**What the player carries:** evidence — assembled across the whole
game — for the three readings of the refusal
(`Lore/Design/NaroReadings.md`): the Declined's testimony, the
scraped line and the surveyor's list, entry forty-one. No source
confirms; the gods' own accounts disagree; Maeleth's is the
most-cited and least reliable.

**The beat.** At the Renewal ritual, the Naming-resolution requires a
declaration: the ritual's officiant (Saedis, a Namer, or the player
alone) asks, in the oath-grammar's formal register, **"What was the
refusal?"** — and the player must answer from one of the readings, in
their own chosen words, *aloud, in the circle, at the marked place* —
the same stage as the original no, one thousand and eighty years
later.

- Staking **Reading A** (the refusal of understanding) opens the
  practice-path resolution: if Naming can only be enacted, the
  circle enacts it now, together.
- Staking **Reading B** (the first Unsaying) turns the ritual toward
  *repair-by-vessel* — undo the wound the way it was made, with a
  spoken yes where the no was spoken. (A coherent, tragic choice:
  the vessel-path chosen *on principle*, not by greed.)
- Staking **Reading C** (the empty summons) is the dark horse: the
  player declares the whole seventh-position story a thousand-year
  error — and the ritual must then answer the question nobody has
  asked at the marked place before: *what if we owe nothing?* This
  stake shades toward Preserve or toward a Renewal built without
  reference to Naro at all.

**The world answers the stake without grading it.** No epilogue, god,
or record confirms the reading chosen. What changes is what the
player *built* by choosing: which resolution ran, who stood with them
in the circle, what the renewed world believes about its own founding
— a belief the player authored, true or not, exactly as the Six
authored theirs. (The meta-note, for designers only: this is the
practice-path's argument enacted on the player — commitment doing the
work certainty cannot. It must never be said aloud in-game.)

**Recanting clause:** a player who staked a reading may later come to
doubt it. Doubt is content — post-ending dialogue acknowledges it —
but the stake is never revealed to have been right or wrong.

## II. The v1 scope triage

The corpus is dramaturgy for a 200-hour CRPG; the game is an 80×25
solo project. The triage: **five deep, two mid, three background,
three-plus-braided god arcs.** Cut content is deferred, not deleted —
the docs remain canon for v2+.

| Tier | Factions | What ships in v1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep** (full arc, sects, quest-spine) | Rot Choir; the Recension; Pale Curation; Tent-Right; Catacomb-villagers | Full faction arcs per their docs; these five carry the main spine (Choir=Consume, Recension+Curation=Preserve+the Naro evidence, Tent-Right=practice-path, Villagers=the Rooted/Root gate) |
| **Mid** (hub presence, one arc each) | Saccharine Concord; Bower-Folk | Concord: ubiquitous trade + the Tovreth arc (his god-arc is load-bearing; the *faction* politics — sect wars, Bloom-trade exposé — defer). Bower-Folk: Posy, the eye-economy, the following-Caster beat; the Finisher/Found-Beauty war defers |
| **Background** (encounters, no arcs) | Driving Bloom; Catchers; Inkbound | Bloom as environmental threat + cure-via-Choir loop; Catchers as the named-three encounter set (Wenil/Kavin/Pais) without the Penitent reform arc; Inkbound as Quillhold color + one Body-Reading scene |

**God arcs in v1: three full, three short.**

- **Full:** Tovreth (the debt, the name, the rest); Dohren (the
  Listening villages, the dream-meeting, the double-convince — he is
  the Renewal gate and cannot compress); **Maeleth+Othren as one
  braided arc** (the estrangement is a single story with two doors;
  build it as one arc with two entrances, halving its cost).
- **Short:** Selen's turn compresses into the Cathedral name-scene
  and its aftermath (the deep Choir-internal politics defer); Ylaes's
  agreement is a single strong scene (the aesthetic negotiation);
  the sixth agreement — whichever the player earns last — arrives
  through the world-state the deep arcs produced, as one scene each,
  not six parallel epics. Renewal still requires all six *consents*;
  it no longer requires six full *campaigns*.

**Origin menu in v1:** River-Child of Sill (default), Wasteland
Guest, Catacomb Apostate. (Ledger-Hand, Failed Scribe, and Child of
the Declined defer — the Declined origin especially should wait for
the content that makes it sing.)

**Explicitly deferred systems:** house dramas beyond the two shipped
sets; the lineage-tier Inkbound vow; the forced-Renewal disaster
path; Spirit-killing (the Spirits ship, Pacts ship, killing defers —
its epilogue-warping costs need the full ending set stable first).
