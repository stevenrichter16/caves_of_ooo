## 0. Where the palimpsest already lives

The Phase-11 revision, without planning to, built the runway:

1. **The word is free.** The archivist faction is the Recension;
   "palimpsest" reverted to its cosmic sense — *the under-text, the
   old writing bleeding through* (`TERMS.md`).
2. **Three under-text relics are canon** — the tenth fire, the doll
   in the wall, the Glassblown Remnant (`MYSTERY-LEDGER.md` §§4–6) —
   with a restraint quota ("the quota is these three").
3. **Two canonical scrapings exist in shipped text**, both in
   Maeleth's own hand: the line where Naro's words should stand
   (`Codex/01`: "the only scraped line in her hand that we hold")
   and the three words scraped at the Tree's moment of regret
   (`Palimpsest.json`, the scribe's WhyMachines node).
4. **The world already shows under-text phenomena** in shipped
   dialogue: "Roads lead to towns no record founded. A fire burns in
   the far wasteland that no one feeds" — and the Reading shelves
   fragments "that do not fit any world at all… A hymn to a fire
   numbered ten," catalogued under **'prior.'**
5. **Erasure is already a moral act** in the culture layer: the
   catacomb-villagers' refusal-to-inscribe (the chiseled-out niche in
   `Codex/05`); the Reader's self-un-remembering of the Sealed
   Libraries; the Inkbound's taboo word *erase*.

So the question is not "where could a palimpsest go" but "which of
the places it is already trying to grow do we feed." The avenues:

## I. Cosmological avenues — the lore *reflects* a palimpsest

### A1. The Felling was an overwriting (the reframe, one sentence deep)

Current canon (Phase 5): the world predates the Tree; the Tree
"bound an older unbound chaos into Named coherence." The palimpsest
reframe sharpens this into scribal terms without contradicting it:

> **The Tree did not create the world. It scraped one and wrote over
> it.** Naming is inscription. The binding is the over-text. The
> unbound chaos beneath is not blank — *a page scraped often enough
> looks blank; it isn't* — and where the binding thins (the Hush,
> the high-Strangeness places), the old letters bleed through.

This single reframe retro-explains, at no cost to existing canon:

- **The Unsaying is scraping.** Urqu's pressure does not destroy —
  it *scrapes*: an Unsaid person or place is not gone but faint,
  under-textual. (Consequence with teeth: the Unsaid are partially
  **recoverable** — see A4.)
- **The Spirits are under-text** — drives written in the layer below,
  still legible through the over-text wherever behavior thins it
  (Phase 5's "behavior is the seam" becomes "behavior is where the
  page wears thin").
- **The lesser numina are pooled ink** of the old writing.
- **The *sari… sari…* gains a second, worse reading**: not only a
  god failing to be born — *the sound of a stylus on a scraped page*,
  the under-layer trying to write itself back up. (Both readings
  stay live; no in-game source adjudicates. The two are compatible:
  a failed incarnation and a re-inscription attempt may be the same
  event described at two distances.)

**The recursion question — new ledger entry.** If the Tree wrote
over an older writing… what wrote *that*? Is the "unbound chaos" the
first page, or the residue of a page scraped so many times it reads
as chaos? **Draft Mystery Ledger §9: "How many layers down does the
writing go?" — never answered, by anything.** This is the avenue's
mystery-dividend: instead of spending mystery, the palimpsest reframe
*mints* it — an infinite regress the canon promises never to bottom
out.

**Risk & rule:** the reframe must never resolve the under-text into a
coherent, mappable prior world (that would be re-explaining — flaw F1
resurrected). The under-text stays fragmentary *in principle*:
scraping destroys more than it leaves; what bleeds through is always
partial, always ambiguous, never a history lesson.

### A2. The self as an entry; death as the fate of writing

Phase 9 already commits "a self is a Naming (a binding-knot)." In
palimpsest terms: **a self is an entry in the world's page**, and the
canon's whole death-triangle re-derives as *what different powers do
to an entry*:

| Power | Scribal act | Existing canon it maps to |
|---|---|---|
| The Choir | **pulps and re-papers** — the entry's fibers rewoven into new pages; nothing legible remains, nothing is discarded | consumption-that-keeps-the-threads |
| The Curation | **laminates** — the entry sealed exactly as written, never to be revised, never to be read further | Salt-cure; "Status: continuing" |
| The Recension | **copies forward** — the entry re-entered beside its variants, forever | the Reading; Re-Membering |
| The Inkbound | **become fair copy** — the entry and its medium made one | the skin-page vow; annotation |
| Urqu / the Unsaying | **scrapes** — the entry removed, its ghost faint in the fibers | the Slip; the Hush |

*(Mirror-permit check, C7 rule 4: this is a re-description of five
existing commitments in one vocabulary, not five new mirrors. Adopt
the table as design-note orientation only — never as an in-world
taxonomy anyone recites.)*

### A3. Magic as the scribal arts

Phase 9's bind/unbind becomes **inscribe/scrape** at the vocabulary
level — folk-charms are *marginalia* on the world's page (small,
temporary, in the margins where there's room); deep workings write
in the text block itself; Urqu-taught magic is the scraping-knife.
The everyday-whimsy layer gains a lovely image for free: a
grandmother's festival flower-charm is a doodle in the margin of the
world, and the Thinning is what happens when the margins start
getting scraped for reuse.

### A4. Recovering the scraped — the Unsaid can be underread

The boldest cosmological consequence, and a whole content category:
if the Unsaying is scraping, then **an Unsaid person is a scraped
entry, and scraped entries can be partially recovered by a skilled
reader** (see D1, underreading). A village that lost someone to the
Slip can hire a reader; what comes back is *partial* — a person
recovered from under-text has gaps, and the gaps are load-bearing
(what did the recovery misread? whose hand filled the lacunae?). A
recovered person is a *conjectural emendation of themselves.*

This slots into existing systems: Re-Membering already exists
(Recension ritual); this extends it to Urqu's victims, at a cost, and
with the Inkbound's annotation horror waiting — a recovered person
can literally be *misread back into the world*, and know it.

**Risk:** undermines the Unsaying's dread if recovery is routine.
Rule: recovery is rare, lossy, and the loss is visible. The dread
shifts rather than dissolves — from "you can be erased" to "you can
be erased *and brought back wrong.*" Arguably scarier.

## II. The physical palimpsest — an object at the center

### B1. **The First Account** — the flagship artifact ★

The game already contains, in shipped text, the makings of THE
palimpsest: **Maeleth's original chronicle of the Felling** — the
oldest document in the world, in the god of Memory's own mortal
hand — carrying the two most important erasures in history:

1. **The scraped line** where Naro's spoken refusal should stand
   (`Codex/01`). She was asked; "she wept, and entered nothing."
2. **The three scraped words** at the Tree's falling moment — the
   place where every telling says it *tried to take it back*
   (shipped scribe dialogue: "the oldest account has three words
   scraped out at exactly that place in the telling").

Commit these as one physical object: **the First Account**, held in
the Quillhold deep stacks (or divided — its quires dispersed during
the Persecution, a gathering-quest). The god of Memory has performed
exactly two erasures in 1,080 years, and they are both *in this
book*, and the book is the only object in the world she cannot bear
to read through her scribes.

**The endgame use — and the Mystery Ledger compliance design.** The
Naro evidence-chase (`NaroReadings.md`) culminates in the player
gaining access to the First Account and — with the underreading
practice (D1) mastered — **recovering the scraped line.** And here is
the critical design move, the one that makes the object integral
*without* closing Ledger §1:

> **The recovery works. The words come back. And the words support
> all three readings.** The period grammar reads equally as
> prediction, as intention, or — the Reading-C chill — as a later
> hand's insertion (is the under-text *original*, or did someone
> write the refusal *into* the scraped layer afterward, to be
> found?). Recovering the physical truth hands the player the
> highest-resolution version of the ambiguity, not the answer. The
> Staking (V1-DramaticCore §I.2) then asks what it always asked —
> but now the player stakes their reading *with the recovered words
> in their mouth*, and different stakes quote them differently.

The palimpsest thus becomes the game's central evidence-object, its
recovery the crown of the investigation arc — and the mystery
survives contact with it, which is the whole point of both the
object and the theme: *the under-text is more writing, not more
truth.*

**Maeleth's scene.** Recovering the line in her archive, with her
attention present, is the strongest possible staging of her arc: the
player un-scrapes the one thing the god of Memory chose to destroy.
Whether she stops them, helps them, or simply *reads it back for the
first time in a thousand years* should depend on the Reader-arc
state. (Her line, if she helps, writes itself: "Slower. You are the
only copy of this moment, and I am — I am not writing. Go on.")

### B2. The vellum economy and the knife-tithe

Worldbuilding texture with doctrinal teeth: the post-Felling world
is **short of writing material.** True vellum is scarce; therefore
*everything is written over something older* — every mundane letter,
contract, and ledger in the world literally has under-text. Three
payoffs:

- **Universal texture:** "read the under of this for me" is a
  service any senior scribe sells; smugglers write secrets *as*
  under-text; the Concord's standard contract carries a scraped
  **clause the eighth** that no Factor will discuss (comedy seed —
  and a quiet hook: what liability did the Concord delete from its
  own boilerplate, and in whose hand was it?).
- **The Recension's material contradiction — the knife-tithe.** The
  order whose deepest law is *never correct the old hands* must, for
  scarcity, scrape something. Their answer: **a scribe may scrape
  only their own words.** Every Recension scribe tithes their own
  past writing to the knife — journals, juvenilia, early drafts —
  surrendering their words so the world's words can be kept.
  Seniority is measurable in how much of yourself you've scraped.
  The order that keeps everything is staffed entirely by people who
  have erased themselves. (This gives the Recension its missing
  faction-specific *cost* — and makes Maeleth's two scrapings
  suddenly legible as the founder's own knife-tithe, paid in the two
  moments she could least afford to keep.)
- **Items:** palimpsest documents as a loot/knowledge category with
  two read-states (surface / underread); Memory-Marble re-framed as
  *the unscrappable page* (why it seals the Root: you cannot scrape
  what it holds).

### B3. Lesser physical palimpsests (texture shelf)

The plaque-wall's chiseled blank (already shipped) as the folk form;
scraped way-markers on roads that lead to under-text towns; the
Bower-Folk re-composing installations *over* old ones and insisting
the buried arrangement "still participates"; a Catcher pamphlet
printed over an older, *sadder* pamphlet the cell won't reprint.

## III. Place avenues — the world's scraped pages

### C1. **The Overwrit** — the scraped region ★

Canon holds that the Great Manifestation (Gen 25) "unmade a region."
Reframe: the region was **scraped** — and a scraped page is not a
missing page. The region (working name: **the Overwrit**; candidates:
the Scraped March, the Blank Quarter) is the game's under-text
showcase zone:

- By day / normally: near-blank terrain, wrongness-of-absence, the
  *sari* loud. The map's margins literally empty.
- At thresholds (hours, weather, Strangeness spikes): **the old
  letters bleed through** — a road resolves, a well, a town square
  with its name almost readable — and the layer underneath is not
  the pre-Manifestation town alone. Deeper bleeds show older layers
  that fit no record: a marshy bookbinder-village that no history
  founded; nine tended fire-pits and an uncounted tenth; a shore
  where no sea is. *(This is where the repo's own superseded lore
  strata surface as world-texture — Reedhaven's ghost among them —
  per the under-text policy, and never explained, never mapped,
  never named in-game as "an older canon." Fragments only.)*
- Mechanically: **layered zone-states** — one 80×25 cell with two-or-
  more authored variants and a bleed-through rule (per-cell dirty
  hooks make partial reveals cheap; a "bleed mask" over the zone).
  The player with underreading (D1) can *hold* a bleed stable
  briefly; everyone else gets flickers.

The Overwrit is also the natural home of the Great Manifestation's
survivors' trauma (the Catchers' founding fields) — the place the
world got scraped in living memory, which is why people who saw it
throw salt at the dying.

### C2. Bleed-sites as a distributed phenomenon

Beyond the showcase region: any Tier 3+ place can carry a *small*
authored bleed (one cell, one object, one inscription). The tenth
fire and the doll are retro-classified as bleed-sites. Discipline:
bleeds are authored one at a time, hand-placed, never procedural —
the moment bleeds are generated, they become wallpaper.

## IV. Practice avenues — who reads, who scrapes

### D1. **Underreading** — the practice ★

One learnable practice, one verb, spanning every content type the
theme touches: **to underread** is to recover what was scraped —

- documents (the vellum economy's under-texts; the First Account),
- surfaces (plaque-blanks, way-markers, the Overwrit's bleeds),
- people (the Unsaid, per A4; arguably the Re-Membered),
- and at mastery, *places* (holding a bleed stable long enough to
  walk in it).

Sources for learning it: the Recension teaches document-underreading
(grudgingly — it borders their taboo); the Namers teach
person-underreading (naming what was un-named is their whole
theology); the deepest tier is self-taught in the Overwrit. Spirit
affinity: Inquiry, obviously — with the Inquiry-cruelty caveat live
(underreading a person's scraped grief *without consent* is the
cruel-knowing storylet).

**The moral edge:** every underreading is a small act against
someone's erasure-choice. The chiseled plaque-blank was a *court
verdict.* The knife-tithe was a *sacrifice.* Maeleth's scrapings were
a *god's mercy or a god's crime.* The practice that recovers the
under-text is also the practice that un-does every deliberate
forgetting in the world — and the game should never let the player
forget that some things were scraped *by the people who loved them,
for reasons.*

### D2. The scraping bargain (Urqu-side texture)

Mouthpieces gain their signature offer: **"we can scrape that for
you."** A debt, a grief, a crime, a name — removed from the world's
page, ghost-faint, unprosecutable, unremembered. The corruption mark
it leaves is, fittingly, *legible*: those who look closely at the
player see the scraped patch. (Existing bargain mechanics carry
this; it needs no new system — only the pressure-model discipline:
the *mouthpiece* wants things; the scraping itself is just the knife
the pressure provides.)

### D3. The Inkbound limit-case

The annotation horror already ships. The palimpsest completes it:
the gravest crime in the Inkbound's world (already seeded — the
Drosera-enzyme softening of a skin-page) becomes **scraping a
person-text for reuse** — and the darkest Inkbound rumor is that
somewhere in the deep stacks there is a *palimpsest body*: a skin-
page scraped and rewritten, an elder wearing a predecessor's vellum.
Never confirmed (ledger-adjacent; keep as rumor — the horror is
better unverified).

## V. Systems avenues — mechanics that rhyme

- **E1. Two-state documents.** Every document item can carry an
  `undertext` field, revealed by underreading. Cheap (KnowledgePart
  pattern), and instantly makes the theme *tactile* everywhere paper
  appears.
- **E2. The world-file as palimpsest (RPG-native).** The world
  persists (PROJECT-IDENTITY); player actions already leave state.
  Frame the *traces* palimpsest-wise: abandoned acts (closure-ledger
  "dangling") leave literal under-text at the site — a scraped-
  looking mark an underreader (including a future lineage character,
  per LINEAGE-DESIGN) can recover. Your unfinished business is
  written under the world, readable by your heirs. This couples the
  closure-ledger, the lineage system, and the theme into one loop
  with zero new architecture — the marks are just world-state with a
  read-verb.
- **E3. Zone layering** (C1's mechanic, reusable): authored zone
  variants + bleed mask + per-cell dirty updates. Perf-compatible
  with PERF-FOUNDATION rules (no per-frame allocation; bleed changes
  are event-driven cell dirties).
- **E4. Diag:** `category=undertext`, kinds `BleedRevealed`,
  `Underread`, `ScrapeBargain`, `RecoveryLossy` — per the
  observability rule, decided before implementation.

## VI. The meta-avenue, and the required Ledger amendment

The under-text policy's restraint clause ("the quota is these three")
was written when the palimpsest was a seasoning. Making it integral
requires amending the clause **deliberately, in writing** — per the
Ledger's own rule. Draft amendment:

> **Under-text policy, amended (Phase 12).** The under-text is
> promoted from relic-seasoning to *structural stratum*: the world is
> canonically an overwriting, and bleed-through is a general
> phenomenon (cosmology per A1; showcase region per C1; practice per
> D1). The protection inverts accordingly: mystery is no longer
> guarded by *scarcity* of under-text but by its **permanent
> illegibility in aggregate** — hard rules: (1) no under-text content
> may ever resolve into a coherent, mappable prior world; (2) no
> in-game source may date, name, or systematize the layers; (3) the
> named relics (§§4–6) remain individually unexplained; (4) new
> ledger entry §9, *how many layers down does the writing go*, is
> protected forever; (5) recovered under-text yields ambiguity at
> equal-or-higher resolution, never adjudication (the First Account
> is the type specimen).

And the quiet meta-note, for the design layer only: the game itself
is a palimpsest — Adventure-Time content under Palimpsest-corpus
content under god-tree canon, each stratum bleeding through the one
above (a candy citizen still walks the caves; a tenth fire still
burns). The revision chose to stop fighting this and canonize it.
This avenue is that choice, made load-bearing. *(Never said in-game.)*

## VII. The recommended spine ★

Everything above is compatible, but integrality comes from a small
number of load-bearing commitments, not from doing all of it. The
recommended bundle — **"the world is a page" spine**:

1. **A1** — the one-sentence cosmological reframe (Felling as
   overwriting; Unsaying as scraping; "a page scraped often enough
   looks blank; it isn't") + Ledger §9 (the layers question).
2. **B1** — the **First Account** as the flagship physical
   palimpsest; the recovered-line design as the Naro arc's
   evidence-crown (ledger-compliant by construction).
3. **D1** — **underreading** as the practice that unifies documents,
   places, and people under one verb.
4. **C1** — **the Overwrit** as the showcase scraped region (and the
   sanctioned home of strata-bleed content).
5. **B2** — the vellum economy + the **knife-tithe** (the Recension's
   missing cost; universal document texture).
6. **VI** — the Ledger amendment that makes all of it legal.

Defer (good, not spine): A4 person-recovery (big content category —
v2), D3's palimpsest body (keep as rumor), E2's lineage-trace loop
(design with the lineage system, not before it), B3/C2 texture
(authoring-time garnish).

**What NOT to do, whatever is chosen:**
- Do not resurrect the old corpus as *history* — the bleeds are
  phenomena, not lore deliveries; Reedhaven's ghost gets no
  explainer NPC.
- Do not let underreading trivialize erasure (recovery is rare,
  lossy, and morally loud).
- Do not build a fourth faction around the palimpsest — it is a
  *stratum*, and the existing ten factions each already have a
  relationship to writing/erasure; giving it to one owner would
  flatten it (and re-tempt the mirror formula).
- Do not name the mechanic layer after the theme in-game
  ("palimpsest points"); the world calls things *the under*, *the
  bleed*, *scraped*, *underreading* — workaday words, per the voice
  cards.
