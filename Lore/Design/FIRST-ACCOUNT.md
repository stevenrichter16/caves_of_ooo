# The First Account

> **Authority:** Canonical design document (Phase 12, M12.2 — spine
> item B1). The flagship physical palimpsest. Bible §IV carries the
> bare facts; this file carries the object, the quest shape, and the
> compliance rules. In-world texture: `Codex/13_FirstAccount_ConditionReport.md`.

---

## I. The object

**The First Account** is Maeleth's original chronicle of the Felling
— begun in the first days after the Tree came down, in her own
mortal hand, before the Recension existed to have rules about hands.
It is the oldest continuous document in the world. Nine quires,
tepui-calf vellum, the binding replaced eleven times around pages
that may not be touched with bare skin.

It bears **the only two erasures the god of Memory has ever
performed**, both already canonical in shipped text:

1. **The scraped line** (quire two): where the words Naro spoke in
   the circle should stand. "She was asked, and the record of the
   asking says: *she wept, and entered nothing*" (`Codex/01`).
2. **The three scraped words** (quire two, earlier): at the moment
   in the telling where every tradition says the falling Tree tried
   to take it back.

The knife-work is hers. The Recension's conservators have confirmed
it across a thousand years of condition reports, and the confirmation
is the closest thing the order has to a wound it keeps professionally
dressed: the founder of never-scrape scraped, twice, in the most
important document there is. (The knife-tithe custom — scrape only
your own words — was formalized centuries later; the order has always
quietly understood whose example it was retro-fitted to. See
`Factions/02_Recension.md` §III.)

**Location:** the Quillhold deep stacks, in a chamber the catalogue
lists with an empty locus field — not hidden, *unshelved*: the one
item the system cannot bring itself to assign a place. Senior
conservators know the room. **Access is the reward of the deep
Recension arc**, not a lockpick problem.

**The Reader and the book.** Maeleth reads through every scribe in
every archive — except here. By standing instruction (hers, ancient,
unexplained in-world), no scribe reads the First Account aloud, which
is the only way she can be prevented from reading it. The god of
Memory has arranged to never encounter her own two erasures. Every
conservator knows this. No conservator has ever written it down
plainly until the condition report the player can find (`Codex/13`).

## II. The recovery — quest shape

Prerequisites (all existing systems):
1. **Deep Recension standing** (access to the unshelved room).
2. **Underreading at mastery** (`UNDERREADING.md`) — the First
   Account is the practice's capstone use, and the practice's
   teachers say so with dread.
3. **The Naro evidence-chase state** (`NaroReadings.md`) — the
   recovery is the crown of the investigation arc; a player who has
   gathered no readings can still recover the line but has no frame
   to stake it in, which is its own (poorer) outcome.

**The scene.** The unshelved room. The book. And the Maeleth
question: her attention is everywhere in Quillhold, and the player is
about to do the one thing she built a thousand-year instruction to
avoid. The scene branches on the Reader-arc state:

- **Unearned (player forced entry / low arc):** she stops it — not
  with force; the ink simply will not resolve; the under stays
  under. The room empties of attention. The player has spent the
  attempt. (Retry gated on arc progress — the book is not a skill
  check; it is a relationship.)
- **Earned, unreconciled:** she permits it and cannot watch — the
  channeled attention withdraws from the whole wing; scribes
  three floors up feel her go. The player underreads alone. What
  comes back, comes back to them only.
- **Earned, reconciliation-arc live:** she attends. The strongest
  staging in the game: the player un-scrapes the one thing the god
  of Memory chose to destroy, while she reads it back for the first
  time in a thousand years. Her committed line: *"Slower. You are
  the only copy of this moment, and I am — I am not writing. Go
  on."*

## III. The recovered under-text — compliance design (the load-bearing part)

**The recovery works.** Both loci resolve. And per the type-specimen
rule (Bible §IV; Ledger amended policy rule 5):

- **The line of the refusal** comes back *whole and ambiguous*: the
  period grammar reads equally as **prediction** ("it will not
  hold" — Reading A's Naro, who understood), as **intention**
  (Reading B's Naro, who wounded), and — the coldest possibility,
  seeded for Reading C — as **a later hand's insertion**: underread
  vellum cannot prove *when* the under-layer was written; someone
  may have scraped the original and written the famous refusal
  *into* the scraped layer, to be found. The physical palimpsest
  hands the player the highest-resolution version of the ambiguity
  that exists, and nothing more, ever.
- **The three words** likewise: they read as taking-it-back, as
  letting-it-fall, or as an address to the seventh — each fitting
  the surviving strokes. (Design-canon does not resolve this either;
  the Tree's regret in Bible §IV is design-tier and stays there.)

**At the Staking** (`V1-DramaticCore.md` §I.2), a player who carries
the recovered words quotes them — and the *same words* are quoted
differently under each stake, which the ritual scene should make
audible: the reading is in the reader. That is the object teaching
the theme with no one narrating it.

**Hard rules:** no NPC, god, document, or diag string ever grades the
recovered text. Maeleth, present at the recovery, does not confirm —
*she does not know either; she scraped it precisely so that she could
stop knowing it*, and whether that worked is hers to keep (her
account of the refusal is already canonically the least reliable of
the six).

## IV. Do-nots

- The book never leaves Quillhold; it is not an inventory item (the
  *recovered words* are the carryable — a KnowledgePart fact, not an
  object).
- No copy exists; no proxy recovery; no second underreader offers a
  competing recovery to compare (one recovery, one witness-set,
  forever — scarcity is the beat's spine).
- The condition report (`Codex/13`) is findable *before* the room;
  it is the object's herald and must carry no spoiler of the
  recovered content — it describes knife-work, not words.
