# The oath of the cloth

*The guesting-oath of the Beating, as spoken. The words are old and
are not improved. A host says them with the first water; a guest may
answer with the answer given, or with silence, which binds the same.*

---

**The host says:**

There is water. There is shade.
Sit, and be named a guest.

For three days what is mine is between us:
the fire's side, the bread's half,
the wall at your back while you sleep.

For three days I will not ask your road,
nor your burden, nor your name if you keep it,
nor set my price on any of the three.

If your enemy comes to my cloth
he will find me between you,
though he be my brother;
though he be my better;
though he be right.

On the third day I will ask you three things,
and you may answer, or go,
and going is no offense —
but go *spoken*, guest.
Leave no silence where a no should stand.
A swallowed no is a seed of the Hush;
we do not grow that crop here.

**The guest answers, or keeps the binding silence:**

I eat the bread's half and leave the half.
I warm at the fire's side and leave the side.
Three days I am your peace,
and your peace is mine,
and what I refuse, I will refuse aloud.

**And both together, or the host alone, ending it:**

The cloth over us.
The cold outside it.
Every named thing under it, welcome.
