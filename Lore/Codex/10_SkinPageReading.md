# Transcript of a reading: the scribe Ollun, folio three

*Quillhold, deep stacks. A Body-Reading, transcribed by the attending
junior. The subject took the skin-page vow in life; the reading is of
the left forearm, called by the Readers "folio three." Marginal
corrections are reproduced where they are entered — that is, in the
skin — with their hands identified.*

---

THE READER (aloud, from the page): "In my thirtieth year I kept the
bridge-toll at Sumphold, and I was not kind. I counted true but I
was not kind. A woman crossed with a child wrapped grey, in the rain,
and I made her count out the last of it, small coin by small coin,
because the ledger was mine to keep and I was proud of keeping it. I
have carried her face longer than I carried the ledger."

*[Margin, entered in the hand of Master Oradin, small and upright:]*
The toll-rolls of Sumphold survive for this year. They record the
keeper Ollun waiving one crossing, in the rain, marked "short count —
forgiven — my error." The rolls do not record a face. He carried the
wrong thing. Let both stand.

THE READER (continuing): "I came to the ink late, at forty, when my
hands began to be honest—"

*[Margin, a second hand, rounder, unattributed:]*
He came at thirty-eight. He always aged himself when ashamed.

*[Beneath it, Oradin's hand again:]*
Entered without authority. The correction is true. The entering of it
was not yours to do. Both of these sentences will remain in him, as
will this one. We are read by what we cannot resist writing. — O.

THE READER (continuing): "—and I asked the Order: what is the vow
for? And the Silent one wrote me an answer I did not understand for
years: *the air forgets. We are how it apologizes.*"

*(Here the attending junior records that the Reader's voice changed —
became, for six words, older, and very tired, and not the Reader's —
and that the six words were:)*

"I remember the rain that day."

*(The reading was closed for the evening. The page continues under
the collarbone. The junior notes, for her own file and not for the
Order's: nobody in the room dared ask whose memory that was.)*
