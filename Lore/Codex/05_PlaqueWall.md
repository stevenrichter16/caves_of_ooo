# Readings from a plaque-wall

*Transcribed from the family wall of a middle-depth village, west
gallery, rows nine through thirteen, with the Plaque-Tender's spoken
notes in brackets. Names are given as inscribed.*

---

**Row nine, third niche.**
RIANE. Patch-Tender. Widowed the year the green light came back.
Tended forty years. Says the patch sang once, and no one believes
her, and she does not care.
[She's my grandmother's grandmother. The patch did sing. We just
don't say so in front of guests. — the Tender]

**Row nine, fourth niche.**
OSSU. Beetle-Master. Went upstairs young and came back old and never
said. Good hands. The wall keeps him anyway.

**Row ten, first niche.**
MERRIN, called MOTH. Child. One winter.
The light dimmed the night she went and the old people say it was
courtesy.
[We re-cut her plaque every generation. The letters must never wear
past reading. That one is the whole wall's business, not just her
line's. — the Tender]

**Row ten, second niche.**
— — — — —
[You'll ask about the smooth place. Everyone asks. That was a man
who sold the tunnel-map of this village to walkers who came back
with knives. The council voted refusal-to-inscribe. I won't say his
name; that's the point of it; he isn't anywhere now, not even here.
I'll tell you this much — my mother's mother voted against. She said
no wall should have a hole in it. She's row eleven; you can tell her
you agree, if you do. — the Tender]

**Row eleven, sixth niche.**
VASHTI. Tongue. Spoke for the village to three Factors, two Curators,
and once, badly, to a god. First Plaque cut at twelve; died at
ninety-one; the plaque she cut at twelve is behind this one, and it
says, in a child's letters: I WILL SLEEP HERE WHEN I AM DONE. SAVE MY
PLACE.
[We saved it. — the Tender]

**Row thirteen, floor level, oldest.**
The letters here are worn beyond the alphabet. The Tender puts her
lamp low and does not translate.
[Some of the wall we read. Some of the wall we tend. Go on up now;
the dims are coming. — the Tender]
