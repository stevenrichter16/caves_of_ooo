# Condition report: the First Account

*Quillhold, conservators' bench. The annual condition report on the
item the catalogue lists with an empty locus field. This is the
report of year 1079, in the hand of the senior conservator, who has
written forty-one of these and buried the conservator who wrote the
thirty before that.*

---

ITEM. The First Account. Nine quires, tepui-calf, first hand
throughout. Binding: eleventh, sound. The boards from the tenth
binding are retained and stored flat, per practice; we do not
discard what has held her words, even the wood.

HANDLING. Gloved, as always. Quire two by bone folder only. The
lamp at half-wick. No reading aloud — the standing instruction,
older than every conservator who has ever obeyed it, unexplained
and not to be explained. We do not read this book to the room. The
room, we are given to understand, must not hear it.

CONDITION, GENERAL. Stable. The vellum drinks the damp of the room
and gives it back; after a thousand years the pages still move like
pages and not like leaves. Whatever else may be said of the first
hand, she prepared her materials to outlast a world, and they have.

CONDITION, QUIRE TWO. The two scraped loci, examined as every year.

The lesser locus, three words' width, at the place in the telling
where the accounts say the Tree — I do not enter what the accounts
say; this is a condition report. Three words' width. The knife-work
is even, patient, thorough: the strokes lifted wet, the surface
burnished after. This is the scraping of someone with time, who had
decided.

The greater locus, one full line, where the seventh's words would
stand. The knife-work is not even. It begins evenly. By the line's
end the angle wanders and the pressure doubles, and there are two
places where the blade cut past the letters into the clean vellum
below. The burnishing was never finished. She stopped partway, and
no one in a thousand years has presumed to finish it for her.

Both loci conform, for the record, to first-position tithe-custom:
the words removed were her own. Whatever was taken from this page,
she took only from herself. The tithe is paid. The tithe, in this
house, is always paid.

GHOSTING. Under raking light the greater locus shows fiber-shadow
consistent with a recoverable under-layer. I am required by honesty
to enter this, and I have entered it for forty-one years, and for
forty-one years I have ended the entry the same way: it is
recoverable, and it is not ours. The knife was hers. The words were
hers. The silence is also hers.

RECOMMENDATIONS. Re-burnish nothing. Replace nothing. Continue.

*— entered, with the customary prayer of this bench, which is one
sentence: that she never asks us what is under.*
