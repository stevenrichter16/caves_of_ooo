# Standard contract of carriage (short form)

*Saccharine Concord standard form, sixth revision. Printed at Tally.
The traveler signs at the mark. The counter-signature is already
entered; it is entered on every form; no signatory in the surveyed
history of the form has read it.*

---

THE CONCORD undertakes to carry the goods herein listed from the
first-named place to the second-named place, whole, dry, and
unopened, for the rate agreed at the desk plus dust-surcharge as
posted.

**Clause the first.** The rate is the rate.

**Clause the second.** Delivery is guaranteed against bandits,
weather, beasts, faction seizure, and acts of the six gods, this last
at double surcharge, payable in advance, non-refundable, and — the
signatory's attention is drawn to the wording — *defined narrowly.*

**Clause the third.** Delivery is NOT guaranteed beyond the post
called the Last Counter. Beyond that post the Concord guarantees
only that the caravan will have *departed.* Signatories wishing
assurance of *arrival* in such territory are directed to religion.

**Clause the fourth.** Goods lost to the Hush are not lost, for
purposes of this contract, but *indefinitely in transit.* Claims
against goods indefinitely in transit mature upon delivery.

**Clause the fifth.** The Concord will not carry: unboxed spores;
bodies not sealed to Curation intake standard; anything that sings;
anything the Bower-Folk have asked after twice.

**Clause the sixth.** Where the signatory dies before settlement, the
debt passes by lineage, per the Concord's schedule of kin. The
Concord extends its condolences and its invoice, in that order.

**Clause the seventh.** All disputes will be heard at Tally, at the
high desk, before the Counter himself, who has heard every dispute
there has ever been, and who is so very tired, signatory. Consider,
before you dispute, how strong your case truly is. He will.

SIGNED at the mark: ________________

COUNTER-SIGNED in the standing hand: *[a name, small and even,
worn into the plate from centuries of printing. There is always a
moment, pen down, paper turning, when a signatory's eye passes
across it. It has somewhere better to be.]*
