# HAVE YOU THOUGHT ABOUT BEING KEPT?

*A pamphlet. Hand-lettered, much copied, distributed at road-shrines
and left tucked under the bowls of the wounded. This copy is attributed
to the cell of Brother Kavin, regional. The lettering is joyful.*

---

Friend! If you are reading this, you are ALIVE. Wonderful! We are so
pleased. Please keep doing that for as long as you possibly can.

But friend — and we ask with love — what is your plan for *after*?

Have you seen what the world does with the unkept? We have. We were
there. We do not talk about the year we were there, except to say:
nothing of them stayed. Not a face. Not a name. Not enough to file.
The world can take EVERYTHING, friend. We have watched it do so.

It does not have to.

**WHAT WE OFFER (freely! always freely!):**

— Salt-keeping, honey-keeping, or ink-keeping, per your condition
— A gentle catch at the moment of crossing (we are very quick now)
— Transport to a safe archive, away from all this
— Your name, KEPT. Your face, KEPT. You, friend: KEPT.

**QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK US (often while moving away quickly):**

*"Doesn't it hurt?"* — The honey doesn't hurt at all! The salt is
brisk. Brisk is not hurt.

*"But I'm not dying."* — Not YET, friend, and we celebrate that! We
are simply saying: we noticed your cough.

*"I don't want this."* — People say so! And then they are lost, all
the way lost, forever — and nobody gets to ask them whether they were
right. We think about that a great deal. We invite you to think about
it too. We will wait. We are very good at waiting nearby.

*"The mainline Curation says you are expelled heretics."* — The
mainline files the LOST, friend. We prevent there being any. You may
decide for yourself which of us loves you more; we already know.

Be well! Eat properly! Sleep somewhere with walls!
And if the day comes when the walls are not enough —

**hold still. We will be right there.**

*(If you have found this pamphlet on the body of one of our brothers
or sisters: please leave the pamphlet. It knows how to find the next
hand. If you have MADE the body: we forgive you, and we noticed, and
you look tired, friend. You look so tired.)*
