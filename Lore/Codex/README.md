## Gate record (the external tone review)

Per the M5 gate, a fresh-context reviewer with **no access to the
lore corpus** was given ten unlabeled excerpts (one per culture) and
ten neutral culture descriptions, then asked to attribute, to say
honestly what was funny, to flag same-voice leakage, and to flag
design-document register.

**Run of 2026-07-14:**

- **Attribution: 10/10 correct** (7 high confidence, 3 medium). The
  reviewer noted the mapping formed "without forcing."
- **Comedy identified unprompted:** the contract's religion clause,
  the file's "Reviewer is almost sure this was a jest," the
  ceiling-idiom line, "the red is thinking."
- **Quality:** rated against the Disco Elysium / Qud / KRZ bar;
  set-average 7.6/10; the skin-page reading and the Salted's file
  judged "publishable as-is."
- **Failures found and FIXED (not re-graded):**
  1. The Catcher excerpt recited its faction's creed ("That's the
     whole of it. That's all we believe") — "a wiki entry spoken
     aloud." Cut; the honey line carries the doctrine alone.
  2. The Recension closer "That is the truest sentence I have"
     admired its own humility. Cut here and in `01_…Account.md`.
  3. A shared epigram cadence leaked across four cultures (the
     capped self-annotating aphorism). Worst instances cut or
     reworked (Tent-Right's "we are both lighter" → "Drink before
     you go"); the cadence is now flagged in the voice cards' banned
     register.

Future codex additions run the same gate: intent card before
drafting; blind attribution + honesty questions after; failures are
rewritten, never re-graded.

**Run of 2026-07-15 (Phase 12 pieces: `13_FirstAccount_ConditionReport`
+ the clause-the-eighth insert in `06`):**
