# File: OTHREN. Selected entries.

*The central file, the Salt-Vault. Read aloud once yearly at the
file-review ceremony. Entries are appended only; the file has never
been amended. Six entries of six hundred are reproduced, by
permission of the office of the Curator, which notes that permission
was neither sought nor required, and that the file belongs to
everyone, which is the point of a file.*

---

**Year 481, first entry, intake hand:**
Subject: Othren. Founder. Third of the Six, Preservation.
Self-cured, by her own hand, in full rite, salt of the first
gathering. Conscious. Eyes sealed at her instruction. Condition:
perfect. Last words: [unrecorded — subject declines to have final
words while continuing].
Status: continuing.

**Year 549:**
Annual review. Condition perfect. Spoke today four sentences, the
salt cracking a little on each. Asked after the coast road. Was told
it drowned. Said: "Then the file on it is closed, which is more than
most roads get." Reviewer records that this was a jest. Reviewer is
almost sure this was a jest.
Status: continuing.

**Year 702:**
Annual review. Condition perfect. Did not speak this year. The lamp
was left an extra hour per standing instruction 9 (viz., "she was
never told the lamp is for her; do not tell her").
Status: continuing.

**Year 811:**
Annual review. Condition perfect. The office places before her, as
required, the petition concerning the sect calling itself the
Imminent Archive, and the question: *do they act in your name?*
No ruling. The petition enters its first year pending.
Status: continuing.

**Year 943:**
Annual review. Condition perfect. Petition of 811: pending, year one
hundred thirty-two. The reviewer, contrary to procedure, asked the
subject whether pending is itself an answer. The subject's mouth
moved. Nothing was recorded. This entry stands as written and the
reviewer accepts the sanction attached; she says the sanction was
worth it; the sanction-record disagrees and is cross-filed.
Status: continuing.

**Year 1079, most recent:**
Annual review. Condition perfect. Petition of 811: pending.
The reviewer notes, for the file is honest or it is nothing: the
salt at the eyes has gone the grey of old ice, and this year, for
the first year, the room felt like a room someone was *in.*
Status: continuing.
