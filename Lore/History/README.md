# Lore/History — the development trail

These are the **phase-history documents** (Phases 0–9) of the lore
project: the record of how the canon was built, bet by bet, revision
by revision. They were moved here in Phase 11 (M8) so that the `Lore/`
root contains only currently-authoritative material.

**Authority rule (unchanged):** `Lore/10_Bible.md` supersedes these
on every matter of bare fact; `Lore/11_SecondSpine.md` amends the
Bible; Phase-11 revision banners inside `05_Spirits.md` and
`06_Plot.md` mark the superseded passages. These files remain *canon
where aligned* and are the sole detailed source for much current
content (the cast in `07_Characters.md`, the place-network in
`02_Geography.md`, the material grammar in `08_MaterialCulture.md`,
the magic system in `09_Magic.md`) — "history" describes their layer
of authority, not their usefulness.

Do not edit these files except to correct a bare-fact conflict with
the Bible, and mark every such correction in place (the Phase-11
corrections show the pattern).
